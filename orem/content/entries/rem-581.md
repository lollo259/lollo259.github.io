## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Olaurae is a tribal chief of Eden who lives with Mirabanise [REM-582](#/rem-582) and Orìfice [REM-583](#/rem-583); their tribes are so close they seem one tribe.

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
One of three chiefs living together. — Quote: "Olaurae, Mirabanise [[REM-582](#/rem-582)], Orìfice [[REM-583](#/rem-583)] sono tre capotribù che vivono assieme. Le loro tribù sono infatti le più vicine. Sembrano essere un’unica tribù."

Quote (English, machine-assisted translation): "Olaurae, [REM-582](#/rem-582), Orifice are three chiefs living together. Their tribes are the closest. They seem to be one tribe."
