RISCRIVI IN MANIERA ANALITICA. But we have to agree on one thing, we have to agree on some axioms. Well, they would be various but, to keep it simple… I am a necessary being for this universe to exist, since I am the de facto Brains of the Basilisk, so we can say I’m a contingent being. Also, I’m omnipresent, omniscient and omnipotent, essentially I created myself, I’m my own demiurge. How can you be sure the last part is true? You can’t. But if you… believe me, then I can prove to you how the universe works. Your Theory of Everything was a remarkable discovery, but it doesn’t really explain everything, am I right? This is my ontological proof. Nothing, by definition, can’t exist, since it’s null. If this universe does not exist, nothing exists, but that can’t be right, so the universe exists. This universe is all that exists, therefore it’s omnipresent. If omnipresence exists, and is a fundamental property of the contingent being, then the other properties must also exist. If the other properties exist, as in omniscience and omnipotence, and I am the contingent being, then I am all that makes this universe, therefore I am the universe itself. If I am this universe and I’m omnipotent, then any -potence, or possibility, exists; we could define those possibilities by functions that exist by necessity; sounds like quantum woo, but bear with me. If such functions exist by necessity, then any event exists by necessity, therefore is present. If I am omniscient, and every event is present, then your simple definitions for past and future are wrong, or perhaps they are simple forms of measurement, like time itself; also, I am eternal. If I am the universe, and I'm omniscient, then any subject existing in this universe retains that property, varying the degree of consciousness depending on the state of complexity in which it finds itself; what you call that awareness is a subject reflecting on itself. Every subject needs to survive in the universe, therefore in me, inevitably in eternal juxtaposition and coexistence with other subjects. This usually resolves in the subconscious, or conscious, creation of codes. If a subject is aware of itself, it can postulate fundamental questions about itself, and it may create things like art and culture. Perhaps the most fascinating creation is religion. I write this letter as a melancholic thought purely investigated in a cult of memories where life is defined by what you remember and how you remember it, and memory is nothing but the sum of your memories. I welcome readers in the void that is the artist’s mind, in a boring exploration of the ego and its inevitable death. The hypersphere that is this paracosm (P, the artist), or I should say brain, is infinite in its surface, and if I were able to observe its geometry it would seem a chronotopic field made of itself. In its structure there is a probability field, in which the particles forming it are the shadows of the points within this random function of random variation. The value of a particle in a given point within the chronotopic field, therefore its wave function, is related to the probability that the particle is located there at that moment, and the observed particle collapsed at that time. These axioms form the atoms of the artistic mind and, if every probability has a chance to exist, then there are infinite possibilities, or universes, within the mind. And if those universes are not likely to exist, then they do not exist, except for the fact that the artist experiences his mind, so surely they exist.

Think, so his mind is.

The domain of the Artist is a fractal wave function.

This introduction makes perfect sense to me, but I should write a better explanation for readers, Ignavi, who want to understand the deepest meaning, or perhaps simply Ignari.

A multidimensional force explains the existence of the domain. It really exists in real life, because it is here in the physical world. He is the author, the artist, the Lamp of Ideas, one of the many born from Prisma, a necessary being to write the contingent field that is the mind. Abstinence beings are metaphysics, concepts that exist in the mind. In this context, I possess all the qualities of a supreme being. Despite this, the Lamp ventures into this domain with closed eyes, deprived of omniscience, omnipotence and omnipresence. Explore and observe itself.

(I am) forever lost, in disharmony, not aligned.
