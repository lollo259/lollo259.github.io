## From the source: LANDE
<!-- type: description | source: LANDE | translated: yes -->
The main army requires very hard evidence of admission; the simplest consists in taming with bare hands a seaweed, then attempting to tame hippopotamuses, Quetzalcoatlus, Calicoter, Orsi delle caverne until the Proof of the King. In fact, he must prove to be the strongest by killing a sea elephant in his bare hands; his clothing will therefore be composed of the skin of marine elephant.
