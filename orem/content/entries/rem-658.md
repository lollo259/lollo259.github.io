## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Atlas (Atlante) is a titan, son of Adam [REM-503](#/rem-503) and Eve [REM-069](#/rem-069) in the d6 family tree. His sons, the Atlanthideus [REM-688](#/rem-688), were drowned by Poseidon [REM-507](#/rem-507) while trying to escape to another continent (see [REM-659](#/rem-659)).
