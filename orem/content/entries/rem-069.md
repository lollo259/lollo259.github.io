## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Eve (Object Class: Aetheric) is an entity resembling a humanoid female of Latino descent, of unparalleled beauty. She was once Lucifer [REM-504](#/rem-504), first-born daughter of Rangi [REM-501](#/rem-501) and Papa, titan of Time and Goddess of the Unknown, twin of Adam.

## History
<!-- type: article -->
Wanting the Universe to return to darkness, Lucifer planted part of herself in the soil so that a tree would grow whose fruit would age Adam. Adam pierced her snake form with the Sword of Ra [REM-506](#/rem-506), opening a breach in Time; before dying she moulted, and a new female body, Eve, came out of her mouth with no memory and one objective: make Adam eat the fruit. They fell in love; when Adam ate it he aged, and Eve stopped him in Time with her gaze, turning him into a statue. She swore to stop all her siblings in time so they would never die. A variant says she later remembered everything and slowly became Lilith. She lost her powers over time and lives in a temple, waiting for the end of the Universe to meet Adam again.

## History
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia; Paradis; Città; Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: partly -->
Eve was once [REM-504](#/rem-504), a titan and the first-born daughter of [REM-501](#/rem-501) and Papa. She and Adam were "two different twins": Adam, titan of Strength, and [REM-504](#/rem-504), titan of Time. [REM-504](#/rem-504) is both the titan of Time and the Goddess of the Unknown: "[REM-504](#/rem-504) is literally the concept of time... time does not exist outside of [REM-504](#/rem-504)", and she is the "snake titan, the Goddess of the Unknown".

[REM-504](#/rem-504) was among the children who wanted to return to the darkness. Adam was the only one strong enough to destroy the Universe, so she planted part of herself in the soil, and a tree grew whose fruit Adam was meant to eat, so that he would age and lose his strength.

In a legendary battle Adam pierced [REM-504](#/rem-504), in her snake form, with the [REM-506](#/rem-506), made from Ra's spinal cord, opening a breach in Time. Before dying she moulted, and a new female body came out of her mouth: Eve. "Just like a caterpillar when it becomes a butterfly, the new body does not remember anything from past life, and has only one, prescribed objective: make Adam eat the fruit."

Eve and Adam fell in love. When the fruit grew, Eve gave it to him, and bite after bite Adam suddenly aged. Blaming herself, she stopped him in Time with her gaze and turned him into a statue so that he would not die. She swore to stop all her siblings in time so that they would never die. (A variant in Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] says she later remembered everything and may have eaten the fruit herself, slowly becoming Lilith, mother of all succubi.)

The author confirms that the older and the younger Eve are the same person. "She was reborn as a beautiful woman. After The Myth of Creation and The First Wars she slowly lost all her powers, making her as small as a normal human being. She lives in a temple, waiting for the end of the Universe hoping to meet his beloved Adam again."

## Description
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Description:
REM-069, referred to as "Eve," is an anomalous entity resembling a humanoid female of Latino descent. The entity is noted for possessing an unparalleled level of beauty, which has been described by observers as otherworldly and transcendent. However, this beauty serves as the source of its anomalous properties, inducing a wide range of psychological and physiological effects in those who observe, hear, or come into contact with it.
It is important to note that the entity appears to exert a cognitive influence over those who interact with it, causing individuals to become fixated on its appearance and characteristics. This fixation leads to significant memory distortion, with individuals often unable to recall their personal identities, relationships, or experiences while in the presence of REM-069. Some subjects report being overwhelmed by an unrelenting desire to approach or be in close proximity to the entity, often abandoning personal responsibilities and relationships in pursuit of this goal.
Direct visual exposure to REM-069 has been associated with a variety of adverse effects, including but not limited to:
Temporary amnesia, with subjects unable to remember their personal histories or even basic concepts of self.
A heightened state of desire or obsession, which often results in behavioral changes, including promiscuity or abandonment of family units.
Severe physiological distress, including strokes in male individuals who encounter the entity in person.
Unexplained injuries, such as ocular hemorrhages leading to complete blindness, in subjects exposed to representations of REM-069, even in abstract forms.

## Addendum REM-069-A
<!-- type: addendum | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Addendum REM-069-A:
Exposure to REM-069 has also led to the discovery of a unique ritual that the entity engages in regularly, referred to as the "Cleansing Ritual [[REM-304](#/rem-304)]." During this ritual, REM-069 submerges itself in a pool of water infused with biological matter. The substance, though unpleasant in nature, is reported by the entity to have a rejuvenating effect on its physical appearance and abilities, maintaining its apparent immortality and perpetuating its anomalous qualities.
This ritual is believed to be part of the entity's self-preservation mechanism, though the exact properties of the fluids involved remain under investigation. Chemical analysis of the pool water has proven inconclusive, as subjects who attempt to examine the fluid are typically rendered unconscious or experience significant cognitive dissonance.
