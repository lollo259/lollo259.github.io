## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Makes the angels appear terrifying. — Quote: "…intervenire. A però è innamorato di B, perciò è contrario e vuole salvarla. Alcuni angeli sono d’accordo con lui. Tramite l’anello della paura, però, vengono resi terrificanti agli occhi altrui."

## From the source: Monaco
<!-- type: description | source: Monaco | translated: yes -->
The angels follow the word of what they define as a god, or X, so they accept not to intervene. But he is in love with B, so he is against it and wants to save it. Some angels agree with him. Through the ring of fear, however, they are made terrifying in the eyes of others.

B, because of the ring of fear, he simply sees a demon who kills another to have him prey, and then tries to grasp it.
