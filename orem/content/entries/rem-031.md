**[REM-001](#/rem-001):** I am the admiral of ev'ry reality yond exists.  Beholdeth mine own self.
**Vados:** We bow before your majesty, great leader. We wonder what the reason for your visit could be.
**[REM-001](#/rem-001):** As thee shouldst already knoweth, thy univ'rse hast many pow'rful beings [the █████]. T hast been obs'rv'd yond those beings groweth stout'r the m're those gents square.  Although highly improbable, i cannot alloweth their pow'rs to beest near mineth.

**[REM-001](#/rem-001):** T is the fate of the whole omniv'rse yond is at stake.  I und'rstand thee cannot comprehend the grand scheme of things, but i require thee to handeth me the subject
