## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Four Horsewomen were the best warriors of the continent of Araceli [REM-1018](#/rem-1018). Members include Layla [REM-621](#/rem-621), the strongest, also known as the Red Harlot [REM-692](#/rem-692).
