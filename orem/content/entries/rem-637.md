## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Confederation of Slavery had one empress, its first and only: Jessica Lexy Bordeaux [REM-625](#/rem-625), last Empress of Tomorrow of the Lexi Empire. Flagged explicit.

## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Bordeaux, Jessica Lexy [[REM-625](#/rem-625)] (let. God beholds) was the last empress of the Lexi Empire and the only one of the Confederation of Slavery. She tried to commit seppuku, and despite showing a pained expression she was not beheaded; the house were she was having the ceremony was burned right after, leaving her to die burned by flames.

Lexi servatoris Imperii [[REM-1001](#/rem-1001)] (let. Lexi savior Empire, shortening of Lexi saviour of the Empire, often shortened to Lexi Imperii [[REM-1001](#/rem-1001)]) is the second biggest empire by land in the entire world and the third biggest territory in the world. In almost all its history it was guided by an Empress, called Empress of Tomorrow [[REM-1002](#/rem-1002)]. It was named after one of its Empresses. It was later changed into the Confederation of Slavery, a short-lived sovereign with the last empress still having full powers but not utilizing them. It ended with the new reigns taking full control of themselves.
