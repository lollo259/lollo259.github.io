## Description
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM — Complete Compendium (English) | translated: yes -->
Per the author, the Orem is first of all the afterlife. The dead arrive there, and the Orem is also the organization that runs it (the "Organization" of the dossier).
Compendium: "Orem / the Orem — the organization (the "Organization" of the dossier); humanity's one hope; holders of the divided Tecnica."
"The Machinist takes the Tecnica and divides it into five (or however many members Orem has), giving each one a specialty and keeping for himself the ability to create artifacts."
"Paradis — planet (and goddess) of the first book; Orem is on Paradis."
Zones of interest of the Orem include Babel ("a branch of Orem dedicated to the writing of books"), the Librarii Ofel Isandarìa with the Bestiary, and The Farm: "a series of structures in the asteroid ring "Armageddon", controlled by the Astral Task Force and intended for experimentation and the creation of new things, in particular for the re-education and training of the new recruits (the dead)."
In the saga plan the fourth book is "the classic good vs bad, Orem vs Saints", and the Eonian Wars are "the final battle of the wars between Saints and Orem".
