BOOK FOUR: LEXICON

Chaining is the practice of blocking someone's movements with chains.

Banquetting is the practice of putting one chain attached to both the neck and the legs, on an external, immovable object, usually a table. It is usually seen in the periphery of the cities of Victoria, Love and Lexi.

Strapping is the practice of blocking someone's movements with straps.

Piercing is the practice of piercing the body with metals. Originally a slavery practice, it merged into pop culture. The most common type of piercings are on the nipples and on the ears.

Adam (let. First Man) is the primogenit son of Rangi and Papa and the God of Power.

Atlanthideus (let. From Atlante) are the sons of Atlante. They were drowned to death by Poseidon.

Aura (let. Aurora, Ether) is the thirdgenit daughter of Rangi and Papa and the Goddess of Ether. She died impaled by Poseidon after three days and three nights of agony.

Phutha Clan (let. Clan of Pitch) is a clan whose members are all over the world, between the Lexi Empire and the Hel Empire. They have visibly pitch black hair.

Phutha ,Dorei (let. Soil) is the most notorious prostitute in the world, living in the Hel Empire. She’s so rich she wears only jewlery.

Bloodsuckers Clan (let. Clan of Bloodsuckers) is the second most notorious clan in the continent of Araceli, and the most feared in the entire world. Almost all of its members are all dead, and the remaining ones are mostly hunted.

Bloodsuckers, Layla (let. Born during the night) remembered only as The Red Harlot, was the first female warrior to reach the West Anisodon rank, and the first to reach the East Anisodon rank. When the previous Kaidou chose to retire she had to fight him, losing in the end. She was then renamed The Red Harlot in a disregarding way to remember her.

Bloodsuckers, Layla (let. Born during the night) was one of the Four Horsewomen, the best warriors in the entire continent of Araceli. She was strangled to death by her adoptive father, Kemonokaidou.

Bordeaux Clan (let. Clan of Bordeaux) is a clan bloodly related to the Bloodsuckers, but they have not red hair. The name only refers to their relation.

Bordeaux, Jessica Lexy (let. God beholds) was the last empress of the Lexi Empire and the only one of the Confederation of Slavery. She tried to commit seppuku, and despite showing a pained expression she was not beheaded; the house were she was having the ceremony was burned right after, leaving her to die burned by flames.

Bordeaux, Lancillotto (inv. Throwing a lance), commonly referred to as Sir Lancillotto or Sire Maniac, was a prince resident in the city of Love. He’s one of the most sadistic and violent individuals on the face of the planet. He collects various exotic beings from all over the world, including the infamous Furia Joheol. He was strangled to death by Furia with her chains.

Bubbling is the practice of piercing the nipples. It involves:

Rings can be either little or large. The little ones are either simple, or they have an enlarged part. That part can be small or big.

Gemini are small cylinders closed by stoppers. The stoppers can either be spheres or pyramids.

Squared Rings are part gemini and part squared rings. The stoppers are always spheres.

Cowing is the practice of piercing one or more chains on the nipples. It requires at least simple, circular rings to pierce the nipples. Only the most desired slaves, or the royalties, practice this form of piercing. Desired slaves that expertise in colloquial entertainment receive two chains. Even more knowledgeable slaves receive two more chains, each one of them attached to one nipple and the vaginal lips; they can also receive a Triple Crown chain. A royalty may also receive a Golden Bra.

Demiurge Clan (let. Clan of Creators) is an ancient clan that was prosperous during the Age of the Gods. Every member has the power to create anything from nothing.

Demiurge, Jelena (let. Torch) is an Airmen. Nicknamed “The Monster” because of her strange factors, she lives with an apparent illness, that is actually the power of her clan, that makes her cells multiply continuously in chaos changing her genes. The result is the formation of a phallus and a clone. She’s the only reported case of a human with red hair other than the clans Bloodsuckers and Rodhår. Her destiny is to bring a new light to the world, hence the name. She lives almost in poverty in an old farm in the territories of Öý.

Demiurge, Lašiša (let. Salmon-colored) dresses with the remains of a young Argentavis Magnificens that she killed. She tames a gruesome cave bear with relative ease. She’s one of the many wives of Kemonokaidou and she has four children, all males. She has no power regardless of her origin clan; in fact, the only strange attribute about her is her hair colour. She died after being injured and laying down after the Battle of the Four Kingdoms, trampled by a herd of horses. She died the next day, 11 hours after that.

Eden (let. Paradise) is a region in the Black Continent.

Hḗphaistos (let. God of Fire) is the son of Ra.

Lexi servatoris Imperii (let. Lexi savior Empire, shortening of Lexi saviour of the Empire, often shortened to Lexi Imperii) is the second biggest empire by land in the entire world and the third biggest territory in the world. In almost all its history it was guided by an Empress, called Empress of Tomorrow. It was named after one of its Empresses. It was later changed into the Confederation of Slavery, a short-lived sovereign with the last empress still having full powers but not utilizing them. It ended with the new reigns taking full control of themselves.
