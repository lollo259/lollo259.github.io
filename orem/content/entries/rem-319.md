[Translated from Italian:] Terrarium Task Forces (TTFs) are elite units composed of personnel drawn from all over the Orem and are mobilized to face specific threats or situations that sometimes exceed the operational capacity or experience of regular field personnel and, as the name suggests, can be transferred between different facilities or locations, as needed. TTF personnel represent the "best of the best" of the Orem. [English in source from here:] Il Formicaio is the training facility of the Organization. Subsequently all its personnel gain hysterical strength and unmeasured stamina. The so-called "Leeches" are therefore extremely violent and brutal during missions. Uniform: They wear little to no clothing, or revealing vests, to enhance their libido and make easy access to their bodies. In fact, they always fight in battles while engaging in sexual activities. TTF Alpha-2 ("Support Class") | Task Force Mission: Terra Task Force Alpha-2 is formed by snipers, paramedics and suppliers. Uniform: Suppliers wear large vests with multiple gadgets on the inside, including sniper rifles, medical equipment, and standard equipment. Snipers wear standard uniforms, and a robotic eye capable of seeing infrared, ultraviolet, and at long distances. Paramedics wear standard uniforms and large metal shields. TTF Alpha-3 ("Enhanced Interrogators") | Task Force Mission: Terra Task Force Alpha-3 is formed by highly trained and skilled personnel formerly utilized to test different types of torture methods. Uniform: They wear different types of bondage devices under their standard uniforms. | Ethics protocol: It's mandatory to announce to the victim their treatments in detail, before proceeding with the torture. TTF Beta-1 ("Chicks") |Task Force Mission: Terra Task Force Beta-1 is formed by newly employed personnel awaiting assignment, rookies, relegated agents, and voluntary non-military personnel. ## Per Aspera: Terra Task Forces
<!-- type: description | source: Per Aspera | translated: no -->
**Terra Task Forces**

Terra Task Forces (TTFs) are elite units composed of personnel drawn from across the Organization and are mobilized to deal with specific threats or situations that sometimes exceed the operational capacity or expertise of regular field personnel and \- as their name suggests \- may be relocated between facilities or locations as they are needed. Terra Task Force personnel represent the "best of the best" of the Organization.
