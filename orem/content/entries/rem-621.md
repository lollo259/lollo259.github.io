Bloodsuckers, Layla (let. Born during the night) was one of the Four Horsewomen, the best warriors in the entire continent of Araceli [[REM-1018](#/rem-1018)]. She was strangled to death by her adoptive father, Kemonokaidou [[REM-525](#/rem-525)].
