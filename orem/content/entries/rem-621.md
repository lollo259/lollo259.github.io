## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia (from former REM-624: Bloodsuckers, Layla)
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Bloodsuckers, Layla (let. Born during the night) remembered only as The Red Harlot, was the first female warrior to reach the West Anisodon rank, and the first to reach the East Anisodon rank. When the previous Kaidou chose to retire she had to fight him, losing in the end. She was then renamed The Red Harlot in a disregarding way to remember her.

Bloodsuckers, Layla (let. Born during the night) was one of the Four Horsewomen, the best warriors in the entire continent of Araceli. She was strangled to death by her adoptive father, Kemonokaidou.
