## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Maelstrom A is a small spiral galaxy of 29,000 light years, a parody of Sagittarius A. The Hel system [REM-278](#/rem-278) lies in its galactic habitable zone, about 14,500 light years from the central black hole. The nearest star is Ander Ster [REM-149](#/rem-149), 4.57 light years away.
