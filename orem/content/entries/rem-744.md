## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Slave Formula is a drug that numbs parts of the brain, first made by enchanters to reach the lustful state; some women use it to feel life's burdens less. Because users lack certain cognitive functions, many are prone to enslavement, and the slave market is legal only for them. Flagged explicit.

## From the source: Djesica Imperii
<!-- type: description | source: Djesica Imperii | translated: no -->
Some girls in the nation use a particular drug to feel less the weight of the burdens of life. That drug numbs some parts of the brain, rendering those women essentially pets who want to be treated as kittens. It was firstly crafted by enchanters to reach the lustful state by numbing certain parts of the brain. It's called [Slave Formula] since many of these women are prone to be enslaved, because of their lack of certain cognitive parts in the brain. Therefore the slave market is legalized, but only for those women.
