## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The ice planet was rendered completely ice when Phasis' sword [REM-156](#/rem-156) fell on it. Jai [REM-178](#/rem-178), from this planet, lifted the sword and became one with it; the future Machinist then arrived with the Archon and recruited him into the Beyonders squad.
