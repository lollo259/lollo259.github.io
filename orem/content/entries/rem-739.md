## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Diviners are the scariest and most dangerous magicians: through logograms they hold vast knowledge, especially of the future. Using linguistic relativity, they summarise past and present events in symbols and then a single logogram, from which they predict paths of the future and can understand and manipulate a known subject.

## From the source: Djesica Imperii
<!-- type: description | source: Djesica Imperii | translated: no -->
The magician uses linguistic relativity to change their perspective on reality. The past and present events are summarized in symbols, and those symbols in one single logogram. From there, the magician is able to predict certain paths of the future based on this new way of thinking. The practice can be used to better understand and manipulate a known subject: since the persona is formed and/or influenced by memories, they are translated in single logograms, meaning that the magician knows exactly how to behave in order to influence the subject. The magician can also study its personal logograms to predict its own future, better if combined with the logograms of the surrounding world. Theoretically, if a magician produces logograms for everything it will be in the dream of ether, thus gaining omniscience, but practically that's impossible. The magicians able to produce logograms are called diviners. A diviner can also understand and speak any type of language in the world.

Monks reach the low state via meditation, not hypnosis. They are often experts in their craft, meaning in combat and spying. Monks are usually disciples of diviners, or secret disciples of enchanters, since the latter is considered more sinful (the diviners try to suppress the enchanters to control the number of possible new diviners, basically of possible opponents). Monks use many different, particular weapons (the number refers just to the number of weapons, and the order is just alphabetical):

The Garden of Eden is a great mangrove forest over the swamps of the Great River, basically an entire ecosystem inside the city. It was the native land. Many dangerous predators live there, therefore the human residents were modified to live there. They have pointy, protruding ears for 360° hearing, and some have cocks and testicles to reproduce.
