Demiurge, Lašiša [[REM-629](#/rem-629)] (let. Salmon-colored) dresses with the remains of a young Argentavis Magnificens that she killed. She tames a gruesome cave bear with relative ease. She’s one of the many wives of Kemonokaidou and she has four children, all males. She has no power regardless of her origin clan; in fact, the only strange attribute about her is her hair colour. She died after being injured and laying down after the Battle of the Four Kingdoms [[REM-664](#/rem-664)], trampled by a herd of horses. She died the next day, 11 hours after that.
