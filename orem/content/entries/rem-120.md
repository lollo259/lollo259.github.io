## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Geraas Wall is the "wall" between Art and the audience, made of unshared emotions, which the artifact (the performer or the artwork) must break down. In Paracosmia, the Star Troopers [REM-132](#/rem-132) begin building a wooden-like wall, the future Geraas Wall, around their territory, the Pastures in the Sky [REM-242](#/rem-242).

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Wall between art and audience; walls of titan bones. — Quote: "L’artefatto (l’esecutore, o l’opera d’arte in sé) deve necessariamente abbattere il “muro” (chiamato Muro di Geraas) tra l’Arte e il pubblico, cioè le emozioni non condivise… Emozioni che il pubblico potrebbe o meno portare con sé, ma che l'artefatto proietta, creando…"

Quote (English, machine-assisted translation): "The artifact (the performer, or the work of art itself) must necessarily break down the “muro” (called Muro di Geraas) between the art and the public, i.e. unshared emotions... Emotions that the public may or may not bring with him, but that the artifact projects, creating"
