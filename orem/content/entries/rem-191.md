## Willbreaker (sword)
<!-- type: description | source: Author's chat notes (13 Oct 2026; original Drive file not located) | translated: yes -->
Willbreaker: touching it inspires fear; it pierces the victim's will. Touching the hilt makes victims see themselves cut even while it is sheathed. Partly unsheathed, it breaks the will of everything around it, objects included. Nobody has seen it fully extended except the Watchmaker [REM-XXX](#/rem-xxx).
