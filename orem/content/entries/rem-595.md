## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Hel is the explorer of the Eastern Empire [REM-574](#/rem-574) who arrived in Eden with his crew in the Era of Slavery, seeking the source of gravitational distortions detected by compasses: the Breach in Time [REM-615](#/rem-615), which breaks space-time. The catalogue records that the expeditions enslaved elves. Flagged explicit.
