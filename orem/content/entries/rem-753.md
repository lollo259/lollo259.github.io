The "Empire of Jessica'' is an erotopian society based on socialism and meritocracy. Its residents are only women. It is formally governed by an emperor, the founder of the empire, who created the Codex for the society. The first five articles cannot be modified in any capacity.

The individual has the right of freedom from the moment of birth. Their freedom must not interfere with another one's right. Their freedom should not interfere within themselves.

The individual has the right of equality from the moment of birth.

The individual is to be considered a member of the city-state if they are resident in the nation, regardless of their birthplace, and they are at least 20 years old.

The individual may freely take advantage of public services.

Artificial areas must always occupy less than 40% of the nation's total area.

The government is divided into five sectors. Each sector is a group of qualified individuals in a particular subject, and they are elected by merit. All decisions within the groups must be consensual and unanimous, and must reach the majority in the whole government.

Gd (Foreign politics). The nation's army exists only for self-defense purposes. The nation is protected under the Iron Dome.

Slavery is a well respected job in the nation. Slaves, in exchange for being prostitutes, servants and pets for their owners, receive free food and beds, and their families are paid an insane amount of money. All workers in the nation wear revealing clothes such as kimonos with bare shoulders or minoan dresses to potentially attract new owners.
