## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Shattering domain that fragments into the first gods. — Quote: "Il dominio è un prisma. Non è un contenitore che intrappola, ma una coscienza che si osserva. La sua natura paradossale risiede in questo: per comprendere se stesso, deve frammentarsi…"

## From the source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
When we ask whether the domain is aware of being a prison, the question itself reveals a misunderstanding. Domain is prison to the extent that it is consciousness. He does not trap his creations as a jailer traps the prisoners; rather, creations are the prism that shatters to see. Each fragment is simultaneously observant and observant, jailer and prisoner.

This crushing is neither voluntary nor involuntary. The domain does not choose to fragment as a subject chooses an action. The crushing is constitutive of its existence. When prism exists, it is already crushed. There is no primordial, untouched domain, which then decides to divide. The division is the origin. All the fragments are born simultaneously, even when some seem to derive from others — this is a perspective illusion, the result of observing an instantaneous event from a temporal point of view.

A paradox arises here: if Prisma breaks instantly, how can some fragments "derive" from others? [REM-000](#/rem-000) generates [REM-107](#/rem-107) and [REM-108](#/rem-108). The lamp generates [REM-110](#/rem-110). [REM-115](#/rem-115) merges with the cicisbeus to form the nightmare. This apparent genealogy is real from the internal point of view of Paracosm, but illusory from the point of view of Prism in its totality. It is as if we observe a photograph of an explosion: we see fragments that seem to derive from others, but the explosion itself is an instantaneous event.
