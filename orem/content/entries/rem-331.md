[REM-000](#/rem-000) is the supreme leader of all gods, but this supremacy is simultaneously his captivity. It is imprisoned on a throne that does not exist — invisible to the eye, imperceptible to the hand, yet absolutely real in its binding function. This nonexistent throne is the perfect metaphor of absolute authority: the more you are supreme, the less you can move. Total power coincides with total immobility. The ontological peculiarity of [REM-000](#/rem-000) lies in its bifurcation: its soul and its body, while being identical in appearance, are separated in function and space. The soul can stand, move freely in the room — maintains autonomy, mobility, active presence. The body, however, remains obliged to the throne, chained not by material constraints but by the presence of the [REM-115](#/rem-115) goddess, formerly known as [REM-115](#/rem-115) or [REM-115](#/rem-115). This separation is not a platonic duality between intangible spirit and corruptible flesh. Soul and body are identical* — they have the same shape, the same face, the same appearance. The difference is purely functional: one can act, the other is acted. One observes, the other is observed. One sings, the other is played as an instrument.
