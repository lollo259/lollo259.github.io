LAYLA BLOODSUCKERS

Abandoned as a baby, she was raised by the Vilkai Klan [[REM-635](#/rem-635)].

She had a son named Amoux, whom she was very protective, because of her past life as an orphan. As a result, Amoux grows extremely protected from her mother.
