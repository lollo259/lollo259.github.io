## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
LAYLA BLOODSUCKERS

Abandoned as a baby, she was raised by the Vilkai Klan.

She had a son named Amoux, whom she was very protective, because of her past life as an orphan. As a result, Amoux grows extremely protected from her mother.
