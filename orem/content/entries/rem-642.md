## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Succubae draenei are the offspring of a Succubus [REM-641](#/rem-641) and a Homo torus: nearly identical to Succubi but sometimes wingless, with torus-like legs sometimes furred like a faun's. Flagged explicit.

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Succubus offspring with Homo torus, faun-like legs. — Quote: " A succubus offspring with a Homo torus is a Succubae draenei, basically identical to a Succubus except for their sometimes lack of wings, and torus-like legs sometimes covered in fur like a faun."
