## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Fairies are winged humanoids of Paradis. They typically grow to 50 cm before being killed, but theoretically could grow forever. Their wings are the Valus [REM-412](#/rem-412); a horde of igniting fairies is the Horde [REM-414](#/rem-414).
