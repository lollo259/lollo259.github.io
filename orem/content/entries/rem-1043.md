## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23]
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: no -->
Hel (death) and Mana (desire) lived embraced together for eternity. Two titans were born, two different twins. Adam, titan, God of Power (kinetic energy, strength, speed); the other was a female, Loosipher [[REM-1042](#/rem-1042)], snake titan, asexual God of Flow. Again two titans were born, again two different twins. Poseiddon, titan, God of Seas (elektrokinesis) and Ra, titan, Goddess of Scorching (fire, lava, plasma). The third daughter was born, Aura, titan, Goddess of Ether. The fourth and fifth daughters were born simultaneously. D'inn [[REM-1045](#/rem-1045)], Goddess of the Unknown [[REM-1045](#/rem-1045)], and Anjel, intersexual winged titan; the last one is not a Goddess and she has no powers.

Living in the constant darkness brought by the embrace of Hel and Mana, some of the gods wanted to see the light. They were Adam, Ra and Jinn. They had to separate their parents, to see the light. Adam, the strongest amongst the sons, lashed out at Mana, reducing her to billions of billions of billions of pieces. One of them blinded Dea from an eye, and she kept watching everything with the good eye (God's Eye Nebulae), while another one became Paradis. Adam prohibited his sisters from having contacts with the other titans but Ra wanted to see his beloved Poseidon, so she tried to meet with him. As soon as he found out, Adam completely smashed her body by beating her to death. The disgusting mess left behind was too gross to be described. Her blood dripped through the fractures of Paradis. It began to melt the earth, creating magma. Adam melted her spinal cord to create a sword. Poseiddon wept the death of his beloved Ra, creating the seas.
