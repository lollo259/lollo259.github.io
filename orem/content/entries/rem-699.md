## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Jehoel, Abaddon also Abbadon (let. Place of Destruction) was the daughter of Hḗphaistos and Anjelique Metatron. She had a violish body colour. She died forced to lick and spread through her mouth melting metal by Hḗphaistos while being raped.

Jehoel, Furia (let. Anger) is referred by historians as being “the almost perfect copy of the Holy Abaddon Jehoel; every single attribute of her body resembles her descriptions, and the many songs written and dedicated to her”. She was one of the most feared warrior in the Lexi Empire until she was captured by Sir Lancillotto.

Jehoel, Melyssa (let. Honey bee) was part of the Triangulo de la Muerte. She died by having acid in her mouth.

Jinn (inv. Desire) is the fourthgenit of Rangi and Papa. She’s the Goddess of Desire.

Lexi servatoris Imperii (let. Lexi savior Empire, shortening of Lexi saviour of the Empire, often shortened to Lexi Imperii) is the second biggest empire by land in the entire world and the third biggest territory in the world. In almost all its history it was guided by an Empress, called Empress of Tomorrow. It was named after one of its Empresses. It was later changed into the Confederation of Slavery, a short-lived sovereign with the last empress still having full powers but not utilizing them. It ended with the new reigns taking full control of themselves.
