## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Conductor is a TTF Phi-1 agent, a male Libra of unknown origin. Ability: fungus manipulation. He controls a special body-snatching fungus created by TTF Omega-1 [REM-307](#/rem-307), which gives him control over many specimens, including the Lily Springs facility [REM-911](#/rem-911), and indirectly over TTF Upsilon-1 [REM-317](#/rem-317).
