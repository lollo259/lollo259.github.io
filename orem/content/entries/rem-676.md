## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Berserker Army is the most feared army in the world, about 350,157 warriors: 157 legendary and 350,000 front-line. Its units include the Royal Guards [REM-649](#/rem-649), Anisodon [REM-650](#/rem-650), Winged Arrows [REM-651](#/rem-651), the Greedy [REM-652](#/rem-652), Barbarians [REM-653](#/rem-653) and Brutes [REM-654](#/rem-654).
