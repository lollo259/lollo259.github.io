## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Leti debris ("detritus of the edge") is the debris disk of the Hel system [REM-278](#/rem-278), between 34.67 and 42 AU, bounded by the dwarf planets Doppelgangger [REM-279](#/rem-279) and Phantasus [REM-281](#/rem-281).

## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23]
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: no -->
The Hel system [[REM-278](#/rem-278)] has a diameter of approximately 84 AU, with the Leti debris (let. "detritus of the edge", its debris disk) located between 34.67 AU and 42 AU. At these two points are located respectively the biggest dwarf planet of the system, Doppelgangger (let. "double, twin", referring to Tuonela [[REM-212](#/rem-212)]) and the smallest one, Phantasus [[REM-281](#/rem-281)] (let. "fantasy"; its existence is only speculated but it has never been seen, thus the name).
