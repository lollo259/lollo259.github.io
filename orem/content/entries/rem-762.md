## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The newlywed couple travelled by tram in TEDIO while waiting to buy a new car; since the wife mysteriously died, only the man remained.

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Passengers on the tram. — Quote: "…maniera diversa dal singolo. E quindi spiegava come per lui e per i suoi clienti era diversa. C’era anche una simpatica coppietta, appena sposata, che in attesa di comprare una nuova auto viaggiava in tram; ma da quando la moglie era misteriosamente morta, era rimasto solo l’uomo,…"

Quote (English, machine-assisted translation): "different from the single. And so he explained how he and his clients were different. There was also a nice newly married couple waiting to buy a new car traveled by tram; but since the wife was mysteriously dead, only the man had remained,"

## From the source: ROMANZO FILOSOFICO.docx
<!-- type: description | source: ROMANZO FILOSOFICO.docx | translated: yes -->
PROVOCED TYPE FROM A UNIFORMITY PIATTA

(monotonia)

Criticism towards man’s position towards reason: man defines certain things as incomprehensible things, because our minds cannot explain or conceive them. Man has no confidence in himself. Our mind is, on the contrary, complete with everything. We understand anything.
