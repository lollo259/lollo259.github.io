## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Phutha Clan (let. Clan of Pitch) is a clan whose members are all over the world, between the Lexi Empire and the Hel Empire. They have visibly pitch black hair.

Bordeaux, Jessica Lexy (let. God beholds) was the last empress of the Lexi Empire and the only one of the Confederation of Slavery. She tried to commit seppuku, and despite showing a pained expression she was not beheaded; the house were she was having the ceremony was burned right after, leaving her to die burned by flames.

Jehoel, Furia (let. Anger) is referred by historians as being “the almost perfect copy of the Holy Abaddon Jehoel; every single attribute of her body resembles her descriptions, and the many songs written and dedicated to her”. She was one of the most feared warrior in the Lexi Empire until she was captured by Sir Lancillotto.

Shoge Clan (inv. Clan of Fields) is a clan based on the periferic territories of the Lexi Empire. They are specialized agricoltours.

uMvuni Klan (let. Clan of Reapers) is a clan bloodly related to the Vilkai Klan. It’s based in the most outer peripheral regions of the Lexi Empire. They have purple, violet, blue, red tattoos. They gain protection from the Vilkai Klan.
