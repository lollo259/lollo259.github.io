## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Lilith is the titan most preached and used by magicians. Her body fluids can be evoked, more useful the sicker and denser they are, worn as sentient armour or used to create spider nests; magicians can also evoke parts of her external body. Explicit details are kept in the source excerpts.
