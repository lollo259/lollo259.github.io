## Description
<!-- type: article -->
TTF Omega-1 ("Astral") is the Terra Task Force deployed in space. It controls all satellites, the Rods from God, the Space Antenna [REM-190](#/rem-190) and the space stations across the universes. It monitors the cultivation and maintenance of a space fungus on [REM-248](#/rem-248) and is responsible for reusing captured deities. It runs The Farm [REM-371](#/rem-371). The Azrael [REM-333](#/rem-333) are an Astral task force.
