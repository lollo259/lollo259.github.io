## TTF Omega-1 ("Astral")
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
TTF Omega-1 ("Astral")
Task Force Mission: Terrarium Task Force Omega-1 is formed by personnel deployed in space. It controls all the satellites, the Rods of God, the Space Antenna and the Space Stations throughout the universe. They monitor the cultivation and maintenance of a space fungus on the Armageddon Loop and are responsible for the reuse of captured deities.
TTF Zeta-1 ("Limbo")
Task Force Mission: Terrarium Task Force Zeta-1 is a secret task force formed by former TTF Alpha-1, 2 and Beta-2 personnel, now highly qualified for logistics missions captained by TTF Phi-1 agent "the Man of Miracles". They specialize in following the agent's movements, acting as his "magical reality-bending puppets".
TTF OMEGA-1: ACCESS AUTHORIZED TO MONITOR ALL DEVICES USED FOR WAR

## Interview by a TTF Omega-1 member with an unknown deity
<!-- type: interview | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
TTF Psi-1 ("Tartarus")
Interviewed: Unknown deity
Interviewer: Author (the artist)
Foreword: What follows is an interview between a member of TTF Omega-1 and a nameless deity, after their first encounter with TTF Phi-1.. The interview was set before the daily reorganization of the deities.
<Begin Log>
Me: Please, identify yourself.
Deity: I... don't remember who I am anymore, sir...
Me: I see. Have you ever been on the Arcane's ship?
Deity: Don't... please, don't... name it again...
Agent: How…
Deity: They... They did things to me... I don't want to remember, sir...
Me: Did they explain the reason to you?
Deity: I... I'm not sure, sir... They said I was... dangerous…
Me: And you didn't agree with that statement?
Deity: No... no, I swear I didn't!
Me: Do you want to change your treatment?
Deity: To... change? Is it possible?
Me: The current treatment is necessary, but in addition we can numb your brain. You will be a sentient being, but your mind will not reflect the information it assimilates. You will not feel oppressed or tormented.
Me: Only basic emotions, although you will not be able to control them.
Deity: I don't like it, sir... I...
Me: You will never again register pain the way you are feeling it now. You won't even notice it.
Deity: Oh... OK, then... Promise?
Me: It's a guarantee.
<End Log>
Closing statement: The deity has been authorized for daily reuse. They will be brought back to the Arcane's ship.
