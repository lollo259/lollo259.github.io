## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Boei is a named technique in An Hourglass of Blood and Dreams, a fan fiction narrated by a swordsman [REM-804](#/rem-804). The narrator coats his body in Conqueror's Haki to defend himself.

## From the source: An Hourglass of Blood and Dreams
<!-- type: description | source: An Hourglass of Blood and Dreams | translated: no -->
I coated my body with Conqueror’s Haki, and proceeded to defend myself.

“Aisusukai [[REM-165](#/rem-165)]”

I extended my palm, blocking the attack. With the left hand I tried to take my sword, but I felt short when a cannonball coated with haki and fire struck me.

“Tiadragon [[REM-826](#/rem-826)]”

It was Azael [[REM-811](#/rem-811)], the former Wyper, eater of the hito hito no mi, model: lunaria.

“Gurash [[REM-829](#/rem-829)]”

On my right, Apollo, the former Gem, was ready to punch me with all his might. He was about to blow off.

“Wall”

I exclaimed with tears in my eyes. Bakara [[REM-807](#/rem-807)], surmounted by my Conqueror’s Haki, acted as a defensive wall with her own body. An enormous explosion approached her, and she tanked it. Shere left his place to not get blown off. With that, my right hand was now free. I quickly reached for my sword.

“Warai Sanchou: Yakkodori”

I extended the tip of the sword to my right, while my back was facing Bakara [[REM-807](#/rem-807)]. An enormous slash generated behind me, extending and expanding outwards. Apollo dodged it. Instead, he jumped high in the air, proceeding to reach me with a strong punch. I handled with both hands the sword in front of me, with the blade facing me.

“Hanauta Sanchou: Gamengiri [[REM-159](#/rem-159)]”

I rapidly turned around, slashing him. Not even a second later, I landed on him with a palm strike to keep him from exploding again.

“Tatsumaki Shotei [[REM-830](#/rem-830)]”

I was already exhausted, but there were still the other two to deal with. A moment later, Shere fully landed a Kamusari [[REM-827](#/rem-827)] this time. I dropped my sword, it was too much weight on my arms. I knew what I had to do.

“Gyojin Shima Yurashi [[REM-831](#/rem-831)]”

I scratched his body, severely wounding him, while the water in the air kept distorting until it resolved in earthquakes. A moment later I was face to face with the bazooka rod of Azael [[REM-811](#/rem-811)], flying in front of me. And he threw another Tiadragon [[REM-826](#/rem-826)], cracking my skull. When I fell on the ground, he kept hitting my head with the bazooka coated in haki. Those bastards were fighting with their strongest moves. With the rod pressed on my face, Azael [[REM-811](#/rem-811)] hit me with a third Tiadragon [[REM-826](#/rem-826)], inadvertently throwing himself miles backwards because of the strike.

It was impossible for me to win the fight. Three admirals, all at once? I knew I was going to lose.
