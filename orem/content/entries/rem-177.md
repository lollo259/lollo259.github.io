## A.N.G.E.L. blueprint by Doctor Daedalus
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
The A.N.G.E.L. suit is a device with divine properties (very similar to "divine cells"; anything with the same adjective was born randomly out of nothing; for more information on divine cells read "Body systems of Paradise") whose blueprint is kept in an unknown space station currently orbiting the Armageddon Loop. The blueprint was made by Doctor Daedalus.

## Daedalus as possible identity of the Machinist
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
The Machinist is the founder of the Orem. Although his identity is unknown, it is hypothesized that he is Antikristos Jay Shuha, the forgotten brother of Saint Messiaen Jay Shuha. It is hypothesized that the first recorded inventor in the history of the Libra, Daedalus, is him; the same goes for the steampunk genius, the Clockmaker. Another possible identity is that of Doctor Daedalus, the mind behind many inventions of TTF Omega-1. Known as The Admiral in the days of his voyages, he now resides in the Space Antenna, an elevator located inside a portal connected to the entire paracosm. The Machinist is a Libra.
