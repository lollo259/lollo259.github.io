## From the source: The Beyonders 2023
<!-- type: description | source: The Beyonders 2023 | translated: no -->
SAMARA:  Park Eun-suh  is a vengeful ghost. Being a dead woman, her body is cold as ice. Her eyes are lifeless, and she cannot feel anything except the coldness of her own skin. She can feel her body only if someone else touches it. She barely has the strength to move, sometimes not being able to stand up on her own. Little is known about her life, or how she was murdered. Moments before dying she signed a contract with [REDACTED], allowing her spirit to live in exchange for her body. Her spirit was then impressed on various tapes, and once the tape is played, she will appear on the monitor, and she can bypass the display until the recording ends. If she doesn't return inside the monitor, her body simply stops moving until the tape is reloaded again.
