## From the source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
Author: In a very physical manner, your body could be sold in markets, as a piece of furniture or trophy; if an artist buys you, you'll be manufactured as displayed art, becoming a statue or similar. Your body could also be treated as food, especially in the archipelagos. For parades in the Laka Imperii, you could be lobotomized and revived. But one thing is certain; if you were a magician, your body WILL be molded into magic potions. For the Orems, your brain could be replaced with the Bypass AgentTM formula, and you will become a brainless agent, guided by the formula. Or you could be fed to BLACKBOXX. For political reasons, you may be part of a marriage even if you're dead.
