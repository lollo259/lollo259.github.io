## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Senshi Rodhår ("Warrior") is the West Anisodon. Layla Bloodsuckers [REM-621](#/rem-621) was the first woman to reach this rank.

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Holds the West Anisodon rank. — Quote: " Rodhår, Senshi (let. Warrior) is the West Anisodon."

## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Bloodsuckers, Layla [[REM-621](#/rem-621)] (let. Born during the night) remembered only as The Red Harlot [[REM-692](#/rem-692)], was the first female warrior to reach the West Anisodon rank, and the first to reach the East Anisodon [[REM-1014](#/rem-1014)] rank. When the previous Kaidou chose to retire she had to fight him, losing in the end. She was then renamed The Red Harlot [[REM-692](#/rem-692)] in a disregarding way to remember her.

Rodhår, Ulaka [[REM-633](#/rem-633)] (let. Aggressive) is a female Raider. She paints her face with a war paint reminiscent of three scratches.

Shoge Clan [[REM-1019](#/rem-1019)] (inv. Clan of Fields) is a clan based on the periferic territories of the Lexi Empire. They are specialized agricoltours.

Shoge, Kyoketsu [[REM-618](#/rem-618)] (inv. Runner) is the originator of a famous weapon, called kyoketsu-shoge in his honor.
