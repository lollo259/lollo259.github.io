## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23]
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: no -->
Primates are a diverse order of mammals. Gigantopithecus are enormous orangutans. They feed on plants (leaves and fruits) and they use two great canines to defend from their predator. Wendigos, on the other hand, are enormous apes with a skinny but strong body. They are carnivorous, and they hunt other primates.
