## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Enormous skinny apes, prey and rivals of nimfs. — Quote: "…orangutans. They feed on plants (leaves and fruits) and they use two great canines to defend from their predator. Wendigos, on the other hand, are enormous apes with a skinny but strong body. They are carnivorous, and they hunt other primates."
