Metatron Clan

Metatron, Anjelique [[REM-510](#/rem-510)] (let. Angel) was the fourth genit of Rangi [[REM-501](#/rem-501)] and Papa, a winged titan with no powers. She died crucified by Adam, after he cut her wings.

Mortuusclan [[REM-1009](#/rem-1009)] (let. Clan of Deadmen) was a clan inhabiting the territories of Öý. They are named after their rituals that consist of reviving dead people to maintain their bloodline. The clan lasted for millennia before being annihilated by the Populus Inferorum [[REM-1010](#/rem-1010)].

Mortuus, Equuleus [[REM-626](#/rem-626)] (little horse), a fearsome warrior, was the wife of a Kaidou. Living in what was the biggest village, when there were no nomads, she chose to become the first breeder in order to set a truce with the centaur that was about to destroy them. The centaur accepted the truce, having sex in front of the village. The huge size difference killed her. The centaur respected the truce, starting to leave the village. Her husband couldn't live with what he just saw, so he attacked the centaur. After a short but intense battle, the centaur killed him with his own weapon, saying that he can no longer respect the truce and attacking the village. From now on every village became nomadic and the breeding offer was set as a valid option for defending against centaurs. She’s always remembered not only for her combat abilities, but also for her immense courage and generosity.
