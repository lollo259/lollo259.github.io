## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Jinn is a titan of Desire, female, one of the fourth-born twins of Paradis with Anjelique [REM-510](#/rem-510). In the encyclopedia version she is D'inn [REM-1045](#/rem-1045). Jinn, Ra and Adam wanted to see the light.
