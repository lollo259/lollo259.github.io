## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Cleansing Ritual is performed regularly by Eve [REM-069](#/rem-069): she submerges herself in a pool of water infused with biological matter which, though unpleasant, she reports rejuvenates her appearance and abilities, maintaining her apparent immortality.
