## Messier object 111 (full SCP draft)
<!-- type: description | source: Messier object 111 | translated: no -->
[[>]]
[[module Rate]]
[[/>]]

**Item #:** SCP-XXXX

**Object Class:** Keter

**Special Containment Procedures:** Messier object 111 is to be contained in a windowless cell guarded by 2 or more agents. The door is to be removed completely, giving the personnel full access and sight at all times. The subject is not allowed to leave the room or distance himself from the guards more than 2.5 meters. 

**Description:** SCP-XXXX has been a source of interest for scientific exploration ever since it had made contact with SCP-3812 [[REM-902](#/rem-902)]. However, it wasn't until a test subject entered an empty cell that SCP-XXXX became the subject of intense scrutiny.

The test subject, who we'll refer to as Subject X, entered the cell and moments later, an entire dimension collapsed in a volume of 5×2.8 cubic meters. This collapse was caused by subject [REDACTED] left eye pupil being the center of the entire 5th dimension. The photon absorption caused the pupil to fold itself in HIGHER dimensions, expanding the absorption of photons in a layer of darkness. The result of this collapse brought about a series of strange and terrifying consequences.

Subject X's left eye became the gateway to a dimension beyond human comprehension. From the outside, Subject X seemed normal, but from the inside, he was a completely different creature. He saw the three-dimensional world as a mere dot in the middle of nothing and was effectively considered blind from his left eye by normal standards. While the main body of the subject remained the same, almost half of his face was covered by a pitch-black shadow that seemingly expanded, deleting the borders of his face. The darkness then covered half of the cell even when illuminated.

Various expeditions were sent to explore SCP-XXXX, each lasting 4 to 6 days. From the inside, the whole dimension had been observed via a telescope to have a minimum real size of 94 billion light years thanks to the discovery of an unnamed edge detected as sparks of fluorescent green light, after which the observation results impossible.

The continued exploration of SCP-XXXX was a daunting task, as each mission posed new and unique challenges. Nevertheless, the endless mysteries inside this dimension proved too fascinating to pass up. The danger of getting lost was ever-present and the need for strong communication and navigation skills was crucial for the team inside. Sometimes, it would take several teams just to explore a small fraction of what this dimension had to offer.

As the exploration continues, more peculiarities were discovered. The shadows that covered the surface of SCP-XXXX seemed to have a life of their own, slinking into shapes and morphing away again. The green sparks that were observed by the telescope were found to be pockets of raw energy that could be harnessed for human use, but at what cost? 

Morphed with the object, what was before a mathematical dimension became a sentient being. 

SCP-XXXX remains an enigma, continuing to offer more questions than answers. What secrets are contained within this vast, seemingly endless universe? Only further exploration will reveal the answer to this question.



 Charles Messier was a French astronomer who discovered nebulae and star clusters that became known as Messier objects. His catalog originally included 110 objects.

SCP-XXXX is a test subject whose left eye pupil is the centre of the entire 5th dimension collapsed in a volume of 5×2.8 cubic meters. The dimension collapsed after a test subject entered an empty cell, with its photons being absorbed by the subject's pupil. Subsequently the pupil folded itself in two dimensions higher, expanding in the absorption of photons in a layer of darkness. The subject sees the three-dimensional world as a mere dot in the middle of nothing, and is effectively considered blind by normal standards from his left eye. While the main body of the subject remains the same, almost half of his face is covered by a pitch-black shadow that seemingly expands deleting the borders of his face. The darkness then covers half of the cell even when illuminated. 

Various expeditions have been sent to explore it internally, lasting 4 to 6 days each. From the inside, the whole dimension has been observed via a telescope to have a minimum real size of 94 billion light years thanks to the discovery of an unnamed edge detected as sparks of fluorescent green light, after which the observation results impossible.
