Special Containment Procedures:

## Description
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Description:
REM-420, referred to as the "A.N.G.E.L. Suit," is a highly anomalous and divinely engineered exosuit. The suit is constructed from a combination of an aviator mask (including helmet, goggles, and oxygen mask) and a pair of wings derived from the divine entities known as seraphim. The wings, which are strapped to the user's back, are biologically and metaphysically connected to the suit’s power source.
The suit’s core design and functionality are based on five distinct divine entities, each bestowing extraordinary enhancements to the wearer. These attributes are as follows:
The Energy of Amaterasu: The suit’s power source, which provides an unquantifiable amount of energy. This energy is metabolized by the user, granting them superior physical and metaphysical capabilities.
The Wisdom of Nabu: Enhances the wearer’s cognitive functions, allowing them to understand and utilize the suit’s powers effectively.
The Strength of Gilgamesh: Endows the user with immense physical strength, enabling feats beyond normal human comprehension.
The Invulnerability of Esfandiyār: Provides near-invulnerability to the user within the paracosm, allowing them to withstand unimaginable physical and metaphysical stresses.
The Stamina of Laka: Grants the wearer an extraordinary ability to endure and process the immense energy produced by Amaterasu without succumbing to its destructive force.

## Addendum REM-420-A - Deity Erasure Effects
<!-- type: addendum | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Addendum REM-420-A - Deity Erasure Effects:
Following the death of Nabu, REM-420’s functionality has been drastically impaired. The removal of any of the aforementioned divine entities from existence has the following consequences:
Amaterasu: If the goddess is erased, REM-420 ceases to function, as her energy is the foundation of the suit’s power.
Nabu: The wearer will lose the ability to utilize the suit’s divine attributes, rendering the suit effectively useless, as the wearer lacks the necessary knowledge to activate or control its powers.
Gilgamesh: While the user remains invulnerable, they are unable to perform any other action, essentially leaving them incapacitated despite their physical resilience.
Esfandiyār: The user’s body will begin to deteriorate with each feat of strength, as the invulnerability does not extend to the physical limitations imposed by the suit’s use. 
Laka: Without Laka’s stamina, the user will be overwhelmed by the energy, leading to immediate incineration or death due to the sheer force of Amaterasu’s energy.

## Addendum REM-420-B - Recorded Feats of Strength
<!-- type: addendum | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Addendum REM-420-B - Recorded Feats of Strength:
The following are documented instances of REM-420’s power when worn by an authorized test subject:
Grasping Power: The user demonstrated the ability to manipulate the chronotope field, bending and folding spacetime itself. Test subject REM-420-A grasped the air with one hand, causing the surrounding space to follow the motion of the arm.
Punching Power: When striking, the user was capable of fracturing the chronotope, causing distortions in the fabric of spacetime. Each punch was accompanied by the appearance of an invisible fracture in reality itself.
Throwing Power: The suit allowed the user to propel themselves across vast distances, reaching the other side of the Universe within moments. Test subject REM-420-A demonstrated the capability to move at unquantifiable speeds by utilizing the suit’s physical strength and the inherent temporal manipulation of the chronotope.

## A.N.G.E.L. Suit (Quadrante version)

A.N.G.E.L. SUIT
The A.N.G.E.L. suit is a device with divine properties (very similar to "divine cells"; anything with the same adjective was born randomly out of nothing; for more information on divine cells read "Body systems of Paradise") whose blueprint is kept in an unknown space station currently orbiting the Armageddon Loop. The blueprint was made by Doctor Daedalus.
The suit is made of an aviator mask (including helmet, goggles and oxygen mask) whose respiratory tube is directly connected to a pair of seraphim's wings. The wings have two straps attached, and the user is able to wear the suit on their back.
The acronym of the name of the suit indicates the enhancements provided to the user of the device.
The energy of Amaterasu
The wisdom of Nabu
The strength of Gilgamesh
The invulnerability of Esfandiyār
The resistance of Laka
Following the death of Nabu, the device is no longer usable. In fact, the only option to incapacitate a user of the device is to erase from existence the deities mentioned above. In particular, the disappearance of Amaterasu would render the device completely useless, since the goddess is the source of the energy metabolized by the suit. The user is able to endure and process the incredible amount of energy thanks to the stamina of the goddess Laka. The invulnerability of Esfandiyār functions only inside the paracosm; if the user were to find itself outside of the fictional setting, it would cease to exist like any other matter.
