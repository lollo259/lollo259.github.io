## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Artefacts that were organs of the ancient titans. — Quote: "…almanacco di artefatti, di creazioni dimenticate, di traslazioni dimensionali tra titani e divinità. Il Bestiario. Questi manufatti, chiamati Mostri, facevano parte degli antichi titani, figli delle Idee (titani primordiali), come organi degli stessi. Un titano che scende di dimensione non è misurabile…"

## The Bestiary and the Lower Dimension
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM — Complete Compendium (English) | translated: yes -->
Librarii Ofel Isandarìa - a ring-shaped library, but titanic, as big as a planet. Here there are billions of books and/or pieces of information, including: the Bestiary (encyclopedia of the "Monsters", that is, of the magical artifacts)
The Bestiary - an encyclopedia on the Monsters, each with its own story, its inventor, and its section in the Library
Lower Dimension - A collapsed hyperverse that serves as the physical manifestation of Boman's body, simplified into one dimension.
The Stalkers' ability to integrate with other monsters (both human-made and anomalous in nature) has proven to be an invaluable asset. These creatures, referred to as "Monsters," serve as both distraction and combat support.
