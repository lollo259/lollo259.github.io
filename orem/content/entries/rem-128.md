## The Bestiary and the Lower Dimension
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM — Complete Compendium (English) | translated: yes -->
[REM-126](#/rem-126) - a ring-shaped library, but titanic, as big as a planet. Here there are billions of books and/or pieces of information, including: the Bestiary (encyclopedia of the "Monsters", that is, of the magical artifacts)
[REM-127](#/rem-127) - an encyclopedia on the Monsters, each with its own story, its inventor, and its section in the Library
Lower Dimension - A collapsed hyperverse that serves as the physical manifestation of [REM-000](#/rem-000)'s body, simplified into one dimension.
The Stalkers' ability to integrate with other monsters (both human-made and anomalous in nature) has proven to be an invaluable asset. These creatures, referred to as "Monsters," serve as both distraction and combat support.
