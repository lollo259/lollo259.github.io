## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Monsters (Mostri) are magical artifacts that were once organs of the ancient titans, the sons of the Ideas (the primordial titans). A titan that descends in dimension cannot be measured by that dimension. Its translation becomes an object with unknown, vast and unlimited powers. Every Monster, including the swords and weapons, is recorded in the Bestiary [REM-127](#/rem-127), which is kept in the Library [REM-126](#/rem-126).

## Habitat
<!-- type: article -->
Monsters are seen in the Lower Dimension, a collapsed hyperverse that serves as the physical manifestation of [REM-000](#/rem-000)'s body, simplified into one dimension. The author's ink sketch shows Monsters seen there.

## Use in the field
<!-- type: article -->
The Stalkers [REM-467](#/rem-467) can integrate with other Monsters, both human-made and anomalous. This ability has proven an invaluable asset: the Monsters serve as both distraction and combat support.

The Stalkers' ability to integrate with other monsters (both human-made and anomalous in nature) has proven to be an invaluable asset. These creatures, referred to as "Monsters," serve as both distraction and combat support.
