## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Cocktail made from a fairy grown in a jar. — Quote: "Tinkerbell | A cocktail made out of fairies. An egg, or a middle-sized fairy, is captured in a jar. When the fairy reaches adulthood, it will grow bigger than the bottle opening. Finally, the juices produced by the fairy will have an incredible taste."
