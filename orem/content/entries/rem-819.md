## From the source: An Hourglass of Blood and Dreams
<!-- type: description | source: An Hourglass of Blood and Dreams | translated: no -->
That was the last time I had seen her. As soon as I found out she had left, I too departed. I had been traveling the world for years, spending my time fighting to become stronger, learning from those who had come before me, and making a name for myself.  I built connections with the likes of Vegapunk and Germa 66. I learned swordsmanship. I fought with one of the Supreme Grade Swords, the black blade Lazarus. But when I first wielded it was just a common sword. But never, not even once, I forgot about her.  Not once did the memories fade from my mind. I would think about her, dream about her, daydream about her. I wanted her again. More than anything.  And there she was. Beautiful, radiant, and vulnerable. She was alone now, as alone as I had been. A scorch of light trilled on her figure, revealing that her body was covered in scars. I stared at her scars and her body, taking in everything. I approached her, but I knew there was something off. It was a trap.
