## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Madman (character 5) of the philosophical novel TEDIO is not really mad: he has understood the truth and left ignorance, while the others remained in it and see him as mad because he is outside the ordinary. He always rides the tram [REM-761](#/rem-761), motionless; at the end he becomes the killer [REM-759](#/rem-759).

Character 7: the killer. At the end of the novel the madman, or fake crazy, kills all the passengers, sick of their impossibility to understand the Reality. He justifies his actions by pretending crazy, so becoming what other people thought it was.

 “See that lamp there? If you look at it will be like seeing the whole universe. What do you say? Are you confused? It is normal for you to say this. The human being since birth is surrounded by confusion, unknown. He has inside himself, and out of himself, an immense confusion. In fact, I would say infinite. A child sees countless things, understands some, but does not understand many others. Well we can say that he doesn’t understand anything: if the things he understands are few, and the things he doesn’t understand are endless, any number related to infinity is zero. He doesn't understand anything, yet he learns. How is that possible? Is it possible for someone more superior than a god? Well, yes, because God exists. Negalo, and by doing so, you'll contradict yourself.

The fool kills all the passengers in his car.

The Infinite Reason thus prevails on the Limited Reason.
