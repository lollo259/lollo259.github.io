## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Lilith (Object Class: Paracausal) is a parasitic fungal organism synthesised from the divine flesh of a forgotten deity [REM-918](#/rem-918) and created by Dr. Daedalus [REM-XXX](#/rem-xxx). It can attack, control and assimilate divine entities, specifically targeting candidates for the A.N.G.E.L. Suit [REM-420](#/rem-420). Captured deities are brought to Lily Springs [REM-911](#/rem-911) and forced into a contractual binding whose details are redacted. The catalogue record also calls it the titan most evoked by magicians. The goddess Lilith's main body lies at the deepest point of the Hollow of Lily [REM-044](#/rem-044).

## Containment
<!-- type: article -->
It is contained in a specialised research facility on the largest asteroid of the Armageddon Loop [REM-248](#/rem-248), near the Lily Springs complex [REM-911](#/rem-911), where it is cultivated in an enormous pit and spreads through spores. Access requires clearance from the Director of the Lily Springs Project [REM-305](#/rem-305); personnel nearby undergo routine cognitive scans.

## History
<!-- type: article -->
The A.N.G.E.L. Suit activated itself through Lilith during the Black Mass [REM-124](#/rem-124) conducted by Holy Orpheus [REM-000](#/rem-000), in which Aphrodite [REM-110](#/rem-110) disappeared from the paracosm. Infected deities' minds degrade until they become extensions of the organism; a minor deity's resistance once caused a paracosmic breach. Dr. Daedalus recorded ethical doubts in a private journal. A Lilith fungus now lives inside BLACKBOXXX [REM-999](#/rem-999) as its data centre.

## Addendum REM-920-B – Observations on Divine Control and Cognitive Disintegration
<!-- type: addendum | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Addendum REM-920-B – Observations on Divine Control and Cognitive Disintegration:
Research on REM-920 continues to reveal disturbing insights into the parasitic fungus’s abilities. As the fungus infects deities, their minds degrade at an exponential rate, with significant portions of their divine cognition being consumed by REM-920. The process appears to be gradual, and while the host deity may initially retain some level of consciousness, they become increasingly subsumed by the fungus’s influence over time.
The precise mechanics of REM-920's control remain unknown, but early experiments suggest that deities who have been fully infected undergo profound transformations, often becoming mere extensions of the organism’s will. Some deities even express a form of surrender, having realized that their existence is now entirely dependent on REM-920's parasitic nature.
One particularly disturbing observation came during a failed experiment involving a minor deity that attempted to resist the influence of REM-920. The results were catastrophic. The deity’s resistance caused the organism to become unstable, resulting in a paracosmic breach and the dissolution of several realities within the immediate vicinity. This highlights the potential dangers of further experimentation with REM-920—the organism’s ability to collapse entire realities cannot be understated.

Addendum REM-920-C – Theological Implications and Philosophical Concerns:
The existence and operation of REM-920 have raised significant questions among researchers, especially in regards to the ethical and philosophical ramifications of using such an entity to control or replace deities. Are we not the true creators, the ones who play with their lives as if they are mere constructs of our own desires?"
Given the volatile and unpredictable nature of REM-920, all research efforts must be carefully monitored, with continued emphasis on maintaining containment and preventing any further contamination.

Lily Springs [[REM-911](#/rem-911)] is a facility located within the Armageddon Loop [[REM-248](#/rem-248)], where it has been discovered that a small, but active cult has taken root. This cult, composed of both personnel working at the facility and others who have been lured into its influence, worships an idealized form of the goddess Lilith.

The belief system that has emerged from this facility is centered around dual themes of fertility and sexual pleasure. Central to their doctrine is the representation of Lilith in the form of sexual imagery, specifically the act of procreation and its subsequent indulgence in sexual pleasure. Symbolic representations frequently depict a penis as both penetrating the womb, representing the continuation of life, and extending toward the mouth, symbolizing oral pleasure disconnected from reproduction.

The cult’s religious practices blend the themes of survival, pleasure, and indulgence. The cult’s ceremonies involve intense rituals where members perform acts of devotion, often related to the worship of Lilith as a figure embodying these dual aspects. These rituals have been shown to cause significant psychological effects on individuals, leading to an intense fixation on sexuality and a breakdown of traditional moral boundaries.

Cult members demonstrate a deep reverence for Lilith, viewing her as a guiding force of both creation and sensuality. The worship of Lilith has led to the construction of numerous altars, shrines, and depictions of the goddess, many of which emphasize sexual symbols as part of their rituals.

Exposure to the teachings and rituals of the Lily Springs [[REM-911](#/rem-911)] cult has proven to have severe psychological consequences. Individuals who have been indoctrinated into the cult often exhibit obsessive behavior focused on the symbolic imagery of Lilith. These individuals show an extreme deviation from normal societal behavior, with increasing tendencies towards indulgence in hedonistic activities, often at the expense of personal relationships or societal duties.

Additionally, cult members frequently express an almost fanatical attachment to the idea of Lilith as a goddess of dual purpose—one who represents both the survival of the species and the pursuit of personal pleasure. Those who have had prolonged exposure to the cult’s ceremonies often begin to lose their ability to distinguish between pleasure-seeking behavior and moral or ethical considerations.
