## Special Containment Procedures
<!-- type: containment | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Special Containment Procedures:
REM-920, known as Lilith, is contained within a specialized research facility located on the largest asteroid within the Armageddon Loop, near the infamous Lily Springs complex. The complex is designed to both house and study Lilith while also monitoring the infected personnel and deities exposed to the fungal organism. The containment area is to remain under constant surveillance by a specialized team of REM-920-affected researchers, who maintain the fungal growth and track any potential risk of contamination beyond the designated site.
Access to Lilith is strictly prohibited without clearance from the Director of the Lily Springs Project. Unauthorized attempts to breach the containment or disturb the growth of the organism will be met with immediate termination. Personnel working within proximity to REM-920 must undergo routine cognitive scans to ensure they have not succumbed to the parasitic influence of the fungus. Any signs of excessive cognitive dissonance or alteration of personal agency should be reported immediately, and affected individuals must be quarantined and treated for fungal assimilation.

## Description
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Description:
REM-920, designated Lilith, is a parasitic fungal organism synthesized from the divine flesh of REM-918, a forgotten deity, and the creation of Dr. Daedalus. REM-920 is capable of attacking, controlling, and assimilating divine entities, specifically targeting those who are considered suitable candidates for the A.N.G.E.L. Suit, REM-420. The purpose of REM-920 is to serve as a vessel for controlling or replacing Nabu, whose existence was erased after the deity’s destruction, leaving a vacuum that REM-920 seeks to fill.
Once a deity is captured, they are brought to Lily Springs and forced into a contractual binding. Details of this contract are [REDACTED] due to the disturbing and incomprehensible nature of its paranormal effects on both the deity and the researchers involved.
The fungus itself is primarily located in an enormous pit within Lily Springs, where it is continuously cultivated, expanding its influence over the surrounding environment. REM-920 spreads through micro pores in the surrounding area, infecting any living beings within proximity. The spores are particularly effective at infiltrating the minds of those who come into contact with them, causing a slow degradation of cognitive and physical autonomy.

## Addendum REM-920-A – The Black Mass Ritual and Aphrodite’s Transformation
<!-- type: addendum | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Addendum REM-920-A – The Black Mass Ritual and Aphrodite’s Transformation:
A pivotal moment in the life cycle of REM-920 occurred when the goddess Aphrodite became a participant in the Black Mass ritual conducted by Holy Orpheus. The ritual involved Aphrodite’s voluntary surrender of various aspects of her essence—her heart, breath, body, and eventually, her soul. This ritual marked the final stage of REM-920's parasitic influence, activating the A.N.G.E.L. Suit (REM-420) and permanently binding the goddess to the suit’s energy-based properties.
During the ceremony, Aphrodite's body was rendered immobile, as the A.N.G.E.L. Suit affixed to her, including wings sourced from a forgotten seraphim, and a gas mask disconnected from all conventional sources of oxygen. The transformation caused Aphrodite to levitate, an event that marked her disappearance from the paracosm—effectively erasing her from existence within the fabric of reality.
The significance of this transformation cannot be understated. The A.N.G.E.L. Suit activated itself through REM-920, and Aphrodite’s existence was sacrificed as a necessary offering for her perceived freedom. The ritual reflects the higher truths of existence within this paracosm: true freedom comes at the cost of erasure.

## Addendum REM-920-B – Observations on Divine Control and Cognitive Disintegration
<!-- type: addendum | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Addendum REM-920-B – Observations on Divine Control and Cognitive Disintegration:
Research on REM-920 continues to reveal disturbing insights into the parasitic fungus’s abilities. As the fungus infects deities, their minds degrade at an exponential rate, with significant portions of their divine cognition being consumed by REM-920. The process appears to be gradual, and while the host deity may initially retain some level of consciousness, they become increasingly subsumed by the fungus’s influence over time.
The precise mechanics of REM-920's control remain unknown, but early experiments suggest that deities who have been fully infected undergo profound transformations, often becoming mere extensions of the organism’s will. Some deities even express a form of surrender, having realized that their existence is now entirely dependent on REM-920's parasitic nature.
One particularly disturbing observation came during a failed experiment involving a minor deity that attempted to resist the influence of REM-920. The results were catastrophic. The deity’s resistance caused the organism to become unstable, resulting in a paracosmic breach and the dissolution of several realities within the immediate vicinity. This highlights the potential dangers of further experimentation with REM-920—the organism’s ability to collapse entire realities cannot be understated.

## Addendum REM-920-C – Theological Implications and Philosophical Concerns
<!-- type: addendum | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Addendum REM-920-C – Theological Implications and Philosophical Concerns:
The existence and operation of REM-920 have raised significant questions among researchers, especially in regards to the ethical and philosophical ramifications of using such an entity to control or replace deities. Dr. Daedalus has expressed concerns about the inherent dangers of employing REM-920 as a tool for controlling or manipulating divine forces. In a private journal entry, Dr. Daedalus remarked:
"In this process, we have become the architects of our own gods. But in doing so, we lose the very thing that makes them divine: their independence. They are no longer true deities; they are tools—sacrificial tools in the pursuit of power and knowledge. And what does that make us? Are we not the true creators, the ones who play with their lives as if they are mere constructs of our own desires?"
Given the volatile and unpredictable nature of REM-920, all research efforts must be carefully monitored, with continued emphasis on maintaining containment and preventing any further contamination.

## The fungus code name Lilith
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: no -->
The fungus, code name Lilith, is cultivated in an enormous pit on the biggest asteroid of the Armageddon Loop. A facility in the form of a small city, named Lily Springs, has been crafted near the pit to monitor the harvested fungus. The organism spread micro pores in the surrounding area, infecting all the scientists working there.
The pit currently hosts Aphrodite and various researchers trapped inside.
