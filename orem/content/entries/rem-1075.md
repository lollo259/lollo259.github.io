## From the source: LEGGI DEL PARADISO
<!-- type: description | source: LEGGI DEL PARADISO | translated: yes -->
ORACLE: attorno al Tempio Sacro

The Sacred Temple is home to the legendary Rebel of the Eastern Empire, Llaurentio. He denies his Motherland as unable to bring peace to the world.

He lives undisturbed in the Sacred Temple; if someone dared to profane it would burn the entire Black Forest.
