## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Sacred Temple at the centre of the Black Forest [REM-593](#/rem-593) is the Totem of Eden. Per the Laws of Paradise, no one may profane, usurp or damage it; further information is in the Holy Book, accessible only to tribal chiefs. The Oracle stands around it.

## From the source: LEGGI DEL PARADISO
<!-- type: description | source: LEGGI DEL PARADISO | translated: yes -->
ORACLE: attorno al Tempio Sacro

The Sacred Temple is home to the legendary Rebel of the Eastern Empire, [REM-703](#/rem-703). He denies his Motherland as unable to bring peace to the world.

He lives undisturbed in the Sacred Temple; if someone dared to profane it would burn the entire [REM-593](#/rem-593).
