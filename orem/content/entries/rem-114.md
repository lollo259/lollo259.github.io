## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Hephaestus (Efesto) is the young god of fire, the artisan who created with his hands instead of his voice. Before Orpheus [REM-000](#/rem-000) sang universes into existence and before the Prism [REM-106](#/rem-106) was shattered in three, the Prism was made almost entirely of two fragments: the Observer (later the Lamp [REM-104](#/rem-104)) and Hephaestus, who carried both the ability to create and the ability to see.

## Works
<!-- type: article -->
He worked in a cave with a furnace, forging all kinds of metal objects. He forged [REM-113](#/rem-113); the Crown of thorns [REM-1037](#/rem-1037) is recorded as his gift. The draft contains explicit material.
