## From the source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: no -->
Following the death of Nabu, the device is no longer usable. In fact, the only option to incapacitate a user of the device is to erase from existence the deities mentioned above. In particular, the disappearance of Amaterasu would render the device completely useless, since the goddess is the source of the energy metabolized by the suit. The user is able to endure and process the incredible amount of energy thanks to the stamina of the goddess Laka. The invulnerability of Esfandiyār [[REM-224](#/rem-224)] functions only inside the paracosm; if the user were to find itself outside of the fictional setting, it would cease to exist like any other matter.

Amaterasu: the device ceases to function;

Nabu: the user lacks the knowledge of utilizing the powers of the device, therefore it cannot use it;

Gilgamesh: the user remains invulnerable, but cannot do anything else;

Esfandiyār [[REM-224](#/rem-224)]: the user's body succumbs on every feat of strength;

Laka: the overwhelming energy burns the user to death.

L’Orem is researching the missing god (since Nabu doesn't exist anymore, the collective memory of the god is also lost). They have synthesized a parasitic fungus from the flesh of Innominandum, able to attack and control deities, in the hope to find the best match for Nabu. Once the deity is brought to a special facility, it will be forced to make a contract for the A.N.G.E.L. Suit [[REM-420](#/rem-420)]. The paranormal functioning of the contract is [REDACTED].

The fungus, code name Lilith, is cultivated in an enormous pit on the biggest asteroid of the Armageddon Loop [[REM-248](#/rem-248)]. A facility in the form of a small city, named Lily Springs [[REM-911](#/rem-911)], has been crafted near the pit to monitor the harvested fungus. The organism spread micro pores in the surrounding area, infecting all the scientists working there.

The pit currently hosts Aphrodite [[REM-110](#/rem-110)] and various researchers trapped inside.

## Mentions in other documents: THE OREM
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
The Energy of Amaterasu: The suit’s power source, which provides an unquantifiable amount of energy. This energy is metabolized by the user, granting them superior physical and metaphysical capabilities.

The Stamina of Laka: Grants the wearer an extraordinary ability to endure and process the immense energy produced by Amaterasu without succumbing to its destructive force.

Amaterasu: If the goddess is erased, REM-420 ceases to function, as her energy is the foundation of the suit’s power.

Laka: Without Laka’s stamina, the user will be overwhelmed by the energy, leading to immediate incineration or death due to the sheer force of Amaterasu’s energy.
