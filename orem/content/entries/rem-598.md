## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Temple of the Gods is a large neoclassical temple full of statues, led by a priestess. Her subordinates wear a bandana over their eyes, said to be out of respect for the blind priestess; in reality she is Medusa. See the City of Temples [REM-665](#/rem-665).
