## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Cadets are all newborn girls of Eden, including chiefs' daughters except the firstborn, trained in the Black Forest [REM-593](#/rem-593) for 18 years. Those judged ready become Screaming Warriors [REM-1074](#/rem-1074).

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Trained 18 years in the [REM-593](#/rem-593) (translated). — Quote: "5. Tutti i nuovi nati di sesso femminile, comprese le figlie del capotribù a eccezione della primogenita, vengono allenati all’interno della Foresta Nera [[REM-593](#/rem-593)] per 18 anni. Diventano cadette."

Quote (English, machine-assisted translation): "5. All new female births, including the daughters of the capotribù except for the firstborn, are trained in the [REM-593](#/rem-593) for 18 years. They become cadets."
