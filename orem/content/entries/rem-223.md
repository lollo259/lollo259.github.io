## Description
<!-- type: article -->
One of the five deities whose powers the A.N.G.E.L. Suit [REM-420](#/rem-420) harnesses. Gilgamesh provides strength. Without Gilgamesh the user remains invulnerable but can do nothing else.

## From the source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: no -->
Gilgamesh: the user remains invulnerable, but cannot do anything else;

Esfandiyār [[REM-224](#/rem-224)]: the user's body succumbs on every feat of strength;

Laka: the overwhelming energy burns the user to death.

L’Orem is researching the missing god (since Nabu doesn't exist anymore, the collective memory of the god is also lost). They have synthesized a parasitic fungus from the flesh of Innominandum, able to attack and control deities, in the hope to find the best match for Nabu. Once the deity is brought to a special facility, it will be forced to make a contract for the A.N.G.E.L. Suit [[REM-420](#/rem-420)]. The paranormal functioning of the contract is [REDACTED].

The fungus, code name Lilith, is cultivated in an enormous pit on the biggest asteroid of the Armageddon Loop [[REM-248](#/rem-248)]. A facility in the form of a small city, named Lily Springs [[REM-911](#/rem-911)], has been crafted near the pit to monitor the harvested fungus. The organism spread micro pores in the surrounding area, infecting all the scientists working there.

The pit currently hosts Aphrodite [[REM-110](#/rem-110)] and various researchers trapped inside.
