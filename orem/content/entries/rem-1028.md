## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The nymph is the only living nymph, in "Search of Her": Groyl Vilkas [REM-632](#/rem-632) wants to see her beauty, kills all the priests of her realm and finds her in a circular pond in the biggest hall; he gains the upper hand only at the end and assaults her. Flagged explicit.
