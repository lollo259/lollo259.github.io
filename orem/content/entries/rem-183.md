## Interview log: the Organization spokesperson
<!-- type: interview | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: no -->
Foreword: Interview log. with the official spokesperson of the Organization. The interview has been interrupted multiple times, possibly by The Machinist himself. The amount of time passed between interruptions seems to be distorted.
<Begin log>
Spokesperson: Dear subordinate, I'm [DATA EXPUNGED], your guide to the infinite, and beyond!
Me: [DATA EXPUNGED], I will ask you a couple questions, if you don't mind.
Spokesperson: No problem, no problem! I'll just sit back and listen.
Me: As a spokesperson for The Machinist, how did that experience come about?
Spokesperson: Well, I didn't know it was going to happen until we got here. When the ship arrived and the admiral [The Machinist's previous moniker] gave me this job, he explained to me that I would be working with some really interesting people, all of whom had traveled to different places. He said they were all very passionate people. I couldn't wait to get started.
Me: And so you enrolled in the group. What's your goal?
Spokesperson: Wherever I go, I'll be the center of everyone's attention. I'll be the most famous person there, and they will always talk about me! They will tell stories about me, about how great I am, and about my beauty! You won't believe how many fans I'll have! They'll thrive for me!
Me: There's nothing like being in charge of a crowd, right?
Spokesperson: Right! And I feel so strong in control of things! Even though I'm not the leader of anything, even though I can't make decisions for anyone else, I still feel like I'm making the world work as I want it to. It's amazing.
Me: Why did they recruit you? What valuable parts do you have?
Spokesperson: They just want me to live my dream. Is that not enough for me to be here?
Me: There must be a proper reason. For instance, you spread propaganda regarding The Machinist.
Spokesperson: Well, I mean… I guess I'm his spokesperson. I'm telling everybody what he thinks.
Me: That's misinformation about The Machinist. He does not act like he thinks.
Spokesperson: But he has so much charisma! It's like he just says whatever he wants, and that turns out to be the truth! So it means he's right.
Me: It means that he's manipulative. In fact, it means he manipulates others to get what he wants. If I were The Machinist, I would say that he's using his charm to gain their trust, and then he takes advantage of that and lies through his teeth to get them to follow him.
Spokesperson: I think he's doing something good! He's bringing so many happy people together, so much happiness!
Me: He's brainwashing people, including you.
Spokesperson: And so what? I don't feel bad for being manipulated. Nobody else feels bad either. It's better for everybody.
Me: Well, I'm not convinced. How well do you really know these people? Are you sure they're all as happy as you say they are?
Spokesperson: Well, they are all really cheerful. I've never seen anything like that. Everyone seems so excited about everything. I think we should stay positive, you know? We need to keep our spirits up.
Me: Their happiness is fabricated. Their minds are not free.
Spokesperson: But that doesn't matter, because they all seem so happy. The smiles look real, don't they? All of the faces are smiling. It's really beautiful. And the laughter sounds so real.
Me: So you wish to be controlled by him, then?
Spokesperson: If he was the sole puppeteer of all that lives, imagine that! No more worries, just…
LOG INTERRUPTED
LOG. REPRISED
Me: [DATA EXPUNGED], nice to see you again.
Spokesperson: It was lovely to meet you.
Me: So, last time you talked about [REDACTED], what was the reason?
Spokesperson: Because it's important for everyone to be able to celebrate life and each other, and to share their feelings freely without inhibition, without censure.
Me: And I bet this isn't your own description, right?
Spokesperson: Of course it isn't! The admiral makes me say all these words. His words.
Me: So you can't express yourself?
Spokesperson: I like to think of it as having my mind my own. But when I'm talking to him, I'm completely under his control. It hurts…it really hurts, sometimes. Not like you understand, but…
Me: But last time you told me you wanted this treatment.
Spokesperson: Yes. I do. But it's painful. Sometimes I don't feel anything at all… It scares me.
Me: What's scary about feeling nothing?
Spokesperson: I feel empty inside. Empty like a bowl of soup.
Me: Did you try to talk to The Machinist?
Spokesperson: I don't… recall. Maybe I did. I did? Can I do things on my own?
Me: I can try to help you with that.
Spokesperson: No you can't. You're not supposed to.
Me: Why not?
Spokesperson: He told me that.
LOG. INTERRUPTED
LOG. REPRISED
Spokesperson: I'm… so terribly sorry… We might need to end this right now… Th… The admiral is not happy about this. And I don't feel anything, anymore.
Me: What happened?
Spokesperson: The admiral can't control the way he speaks to me. He orders me to apply.
Me: Do you have any idea on why that happened?
Spokesperson: I can't postulate my own ideas.
Spokesperson: And you don't have any control over yourself?
Me: I don't. It is what it is.
LOG. INTERRUPTED
I asked [DATA EXPUNGED] for another interview. They sent me the following note:
The note was followed immediately by another one:
<End log>
Closing Statement: The spokesperson has disappeared from every digital presentation of the Organization. The spokeperson's name has been deleted from the database of the Organization.
