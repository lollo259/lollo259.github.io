## From the source: oriente
<!-- type: description | source: oriente | translated: yes -->
Location: Paradiso Terrestre, Utopya

Selussa is the most skillful and fearless warrior of the empire. Born in the Lexi Empire and sent against the Eastern Empire at only five years for punishment (it had rebelled against slavery, failing) docks with the ship, the only standing still not destroyed by the Deadly Sins almost by miracle, in the northern part of Utopya, completely desolate. He survives for 15 years. Over the years, he moved to the Eastern Empire. Here he decides to avenge his motherland, and he stands on the side of the new empire.
