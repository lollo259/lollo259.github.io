## > **Interviewed:** SCP-001-F1
<!-- type: interview | source: notes on beyonders | translated: no -->
> **Interviewed:** SCP-001-F1
>
> **Interviewer:** 05-█
>
> **Foreword:** The following is an interview between a member of the 05 Council and SCP-001-F1, after direct request from SCP-001-1a [[REM-002](#/rem-002)]. The interview was set before the [REDACTED] experiment on SCP-001-F1 ordered by SCP-001-1a [[REM-002](#/rem-002)].
>
> **<Begin Log>**
>
> **05-█:** Please identify yourself.
>
> **SCP-001-F1:** I… I don't remember who I am anymore, sir…
>
> **05-█:** How would you describe your treatment on this ship?
>
> **SCP-001-F1:** They… They did things to me… I don't want to remember, sir…
>
> **05-█:** Did they explain the reasons behind that?
>
> **SCP-001-F1:** I.. I'm not sure, sir… They said I was.. dangerous…
>
> **05-█:** And you didn't agree with that affirmation?
>
> **SCP-001-F1:** I didn't… no, I swear I didn't!
>
> **05-█:** Do you want to change your treatment?
>
> **SCP-001-F1:** To… change? Is that possible?
>
> **05-█:** Your current treatment is necessary, but in addition, we can numb your brain. You will be a sentient being, but your mind won't reflect the assimilated information. You won't feel oppressed or tortured.
>
> **SCP-001-F1:** But… what would I feel?
>
> **05-█:** Just basic emotions, though you won't be able to control them.
>
> **SCP-001-F1:** I don't like that, sir… I…
>
> **05-█:** You will never register pain anymore the same way you are right now. You won't even notice it in the long run.
>
> **SCP-001-F1:** Oh… OK, then… You promise?
>
> **05-█:** It's a guarantee.
>
> **<End Log>**
>
> **Closing Statement:** SCP-001-5a has been cleared for experiment [REDACTED].
