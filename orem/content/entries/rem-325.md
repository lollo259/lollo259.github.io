## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Angels are a nation of so-called "perfect" beings, and so androgynous; in reality a corrupted, racist nation that exterminated or held captive half of themselves. They reach their fullest power in orgies, which is why they appear from a distance as seraphim or masses of wings; churches are vowed to them. The Anjelic War notes tell how the spirit of the angel Metatron [REM-185](#/rem-185) visited Samsara of Dumas [REM-232](#/rem-232), wife of Merlin Jay Shuha, conceiving Antikristos [REM-174](#/rem-174); Merlin later left to become a druid. Some content is explicit.

## From the source: Documento senza titolo

Anjelic War

Angels reach their fullest power in orgies; that's why they often appear in the distance as seraphim, or masses with multiple wings. Churches are just vowing to them, meaning that nuns, priests, etc, marry angels.

The Holy Bishop is the herald of the First Virgin.

This is the story of a woman, Samsara of Dumas [[REM-232](#/rem-232)], and her beloved husband, namely Merlin Jay Shuha. During the night, the spirit of the angel Metatron came inside Samsara's uterus, impregnating her with the most charming and powerful son, Antikristos. She was caring so much, eventually falling in love with her own son. Merlin subsequently left the family, pursuing a life of pardon and becoming a druid.

Samsara's Background:

Samsara of Dumas [[REM-232](#/rem-232)] was born into a family of healers and mystics in the ancient city of Dumas.

From an early age, she displayed an unusual connection to spiritual energies and mystical forces.

The Arrival of Antikristos:

The night that changed everything - the spirit of the angel Metatron visited Samsara in a vivid dream.

Metatron's presence was overwhelming, filling her with both fear and awe as he imparted his divine message.

Embracing Motherhood:

Despite the unconventional circumstances of Antikristos' conception, Samsara embraced her pregnancy with unwavering love and devotion.

She could feel the extraordinary power coursing through her unborn child as he grew within her.*Samsara's son had a powerful presence in her uterus.*

Samsara's son seemed to possess an extraordinary energy that pulsed through her as he developed within her womb. She felt a unique and powerful connection to him, as if he carried within him an ancient force waiting to be unleashed.

Merlin's Departure:

In the wake of these extraordinary events, Merlin Jay Shuha felt called to pursue a path of spiritual enlightenment.

He left behind his family to seek forgiveness and wisdom as he embarked on a journey to become a druid.
