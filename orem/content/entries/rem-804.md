## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The narrator of An Hourglass of Blood and Dreams is a first-person swordsman who learned the way of the sword in Wanokuni [REM-823](#/rem-823) under Master Kiku [REM-805](#/rem-805). He travelled the world for years to grow stronger, built connections with Vegapunk [REM-818](#/rem-818) and Germa 66 [REM-819](#/rem-819), and wields the black blade Lazarus [REM-820](#/rem-820). He uses Conqueror's Haki [REM-832](#/rem-832) and Mutoryu techniques. The story is violent and explicit.
