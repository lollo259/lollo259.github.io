## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Rodhår Clan ("Clan of Red-haired") is the most notorious clan in the world, mostly because of Araceli Rodhår [REM-619](#/rem-619) and her descendants maintaining the name's prestige. Members include Blade [REM-1013](#/rem-1013), Niji [REM-1014](#/rem-1014), Senshi [REM-1015](#/rem-1015), Anjelina [REM-630](#/rem-630) and Ulaka [REM-633](#/rem-633).

## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Rodhår, Anjelina [[REM-630](#/rem-630)] (let. Little Angel) is one of the four Airmen of the most dangerous army in the world. Her hair tends to be more orange-red. She wears a medium braid on the right side of the head, covering her head with a viking helmet with mouflon horns. She then has rest are brown leather belts wore under-boob; brown leather gloves wore under-elbow that cover white lace gloves wore over-elbow are in turn covered by brown metal forearm guards with fur; white lace socks wore over-knee with no end are covered by brown circular metal thigh guards with fur; fur sheets covering legs held by leather belts under-knee.

Rodhår, Araceli [[REM-619](#/rem-619)] (let. Monument of the skies) was the protagonist of The Birth of Araceli [[REM-1016](#/rem-1016)], the most infamous story in the whole continent of a woman who single handedly drove away the Devil’s Army from the continent by making a clever deal. The Northern Continent [[REM-1018](#/rem-1018)] is named after her. She’s known to be one of the most clever and courageous women to ever live, and obviously one of the most important.

Rodhår, Blade [[REM-1013](#/rem-1013)] (let. Blade) was the daughter of Araceli [[REM-1018](#/rem-1018)] Rodhår, born from a demon.

Rodhår, Niji [[REM-1014](#/rem-1014)] (let. Rainbow) is the East Anisodon [[REM-1014](#/rem-1014)].

Rodhår, Senshi [[REM-1015](#/rem-1015)] (let. Warrior) is the West Anisodon [[REM-1015](#/rem-1015)].

Rodhår, Ulaka [[REM-633](#/rem-633)] (let. Aggressive) is a female Raider. She paints her face with a war paint reminiscent of three scratches.

Shoge Clan [[REM-1019](#/rem-1019)] (inv. Clan of Fields) is a clan based on the periferic territories of the Lexi Empire. They are specialized agricoltours.

Shoge, Kyoketsu [[REM-618](#/rem-618)] (inv. Runner) is the originator of a famous weapon, called kyoketsu-shoge in his honor.
