## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Rodhår, Anjelina (let. Little Angel) is one of the four Airmen of the most dangerous army in the world. Her hair tends to be more orange-red. She wears a medium braid on the right side of the head, covering her head with a viking helmet with mouflon horns. She then has rest are brown leather belts wore under-boob; brown leather gloves wore under-elbow that cover white lace gloves wore over-elbow are in turn covered by brown metal forearm guards with fur; white lace socks wore over-knee with no end are covered by brown circular metal thigh guards with fur; fur sheets covering legs held by leather belts under-knee.

Rodhår, Araceli (let. Monument of the skies) was the protagonist of The Birth of Araceli, the most infamous story in the whole continent of a woman who single handedly drove away the Devil’s Army from the continent by making a clever deal. The Northern Continent is named after her. She’s known to be one of the most clever and courageous women to ever live, and obviously one of the most important.

Rodhår, Blade (let. Blade) was the daughter of Araceli Rodhår, born from a demon.

Rodhår, Niji (let. Rainbow) is the East Anisodon.

Rodhår, Senshi (let. Warrior) is the West Anisodon.

Rodhår, Ulaka (let. Aggressive) is a female Raider. She paints her face with a war paint reminiscent of three scratches.

Shoge Clan (inv. Clan of Fields) is a clan based on the periferic territories of the Lexi Empire. They are specialized agricoltours.

Shoge, Kyoketsu (inv. Runner) is the originator of a famous weapon, called kyoketsu-shoge in his honor.
