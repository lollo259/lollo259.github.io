## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Posterity is an agent of the Astral task force Azrael [REM-333](#/rem-333) and the owner of the A.N.G.E.L. Suit [REM-420](#/rem-420). The suit harnesses the powers of the deities named by the letters of "Angel", who are locked in The Farm [REM-371](#/rem-371). Their fusion manipulates the soul of the paracosm: Posterity breaks the paracosm, creating cracks in space, and can grab it and drag it toward himself (a teleport effect) or throw it away. The name is unrelated to the excluded Posterity Era.
