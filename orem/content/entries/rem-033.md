## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Venxia, "She who slurps", is a succubus listed with the Hollow of Lily [REM-044](#/rem-044) and also described in the Djesica Imperii magic notes.

## Appearance
<!-- type: article -->
She has a woman's body with legs morphed into scaled heeled boots, two thin red tails ending in a stylised heart, two small red horns, elf ears and green tentacles for hair. The source describes further explicit anatomical features, kept in the source excerpts.
