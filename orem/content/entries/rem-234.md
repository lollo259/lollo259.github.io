## Source excerpts
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
REM-000's origin is traced back to the event known as "Absolute Zero", a point at which two primeval entities, Ander Ster and Andro Meda, gave rise to Boman, effectively making him the first of the Demiurge class entities. His creation was not an act of birth in the traditional sense, but an emanation of pure thought, an Idea taking form. As the progeny of Ideas, Boman's abilities include the power to mold realities, create universes, and alter the fundamental laws of existence.

Boman's early creations were of an abstract nature, existing as "bubbles" within an ever-expanding conceptual dimension. Each bubble represented a universe, each with its own fundamental laws, some of which were directly manipulated by Boman, while others were self-sustaining. These universes exist within multiple layers, constantly folding and re-folding across dimensions. Due to the inherent complexity of these structures, Boman’s ability to create and destroy is more of a conceptual process than a physical one.

At approximately 19.8 billion years of age, Boman expanded his domain into what is known as the "Eden Hyperverse"—a collection of universes that adhered to the Many Worlds Interpretation of quantum mechanics. Within this hyperverse, every universe is governed by a unique set of fundamental laws, which Boman either controls directly or allows to exist independently. This hyperverse has been described as Boman's playground, his personal realm, where he holds dominion over all created entities and events.

At the age of approximately 9 trillion years, Boman enters a phase of conflict and self-discovery. He finds himself in a confrontation with his progenitors, Ander Ster and Andro Meda, within the Church of Light, a monumental, conceptual structure. In this environment, Boman confronts the deeper existential truth of his existence and is pushed toward a mental breakdown.

During this period, Boman undergoes a transformation, dividing his existence into three distinct realms:

Lower Dimension - A collapsed hyperverse that serves as the physical manifestation of Boman’s body, simplified into one dimension.

These three dimensions are interconnected but independent, each containing different aspects of Boman’s existence. His soul resides in Pascoli nel Cielo, his body in the Lower Dimension, and his Ideas in the Outerverse. This division leads to further internal conflict, as Boman is confronted with the limitations of his own existence.

In the later stages of Boman's existence, he encounters one of his own creations, an entity now known as the Saints. These beings have attained a level of power that allows them to subjugate Boman, forcing his soul to remain outside of his physical form. This event marks the beginning of Boman's descent into a more passive state, as he is no longer in complete control of his creations or his own destiny.
