## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Belphegor Morningstar (Belfagor) is a Star Trooper of the Morningstar family [REM-259](#/rem-259), "the insatiable Belfagor". He was a member of the first command meant to rule the artist's domain in place of Orpheus [REM-000](#/rem-000). In the Silver City he represents Laziness.
