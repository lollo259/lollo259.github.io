## From the source: Pascoli nel cielo (sheet)
<!-- type: description | source: Pascoli nel cielo (sheet) | translated: no -->
Phalo | The male penis, as called by the fairies. They are attracted to flowers, nectar, and various flavors. Male semen is the quintessential flavor. They often erect sculptures of Phalo, made of wood and decorations.

Valus | The wings of a fairy. Once full grown, they can cover the fairy like a vest.

Fairy | A humanoid with wings. Typically, they grow to the size of 50 cm before getting killed, but theoretically they could grow forever.

Horde | A horde of igniting fairies, unstoppable. The worst storm on Paradis.
