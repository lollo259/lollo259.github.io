## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Shikigami are spirits populating the Prairies in the Sky with the Saints. They look like variants of eels; some horses even have half their bodies eel-like.

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Eel-like spirits populating the Prairies in the Sky. — Quote: "The Prairies in the Sky are populated by Saints and Shikigami spirits. The shikigami look like variants of eels, and even some horses have half of their bodies Eel-like."

## Author's notes (16 Oct 2026)
<!-- type: note | source: Author's chat notes (16 Oct 2026) -->
Shinigami are completely free and cannot be tamed, like Namygal [REM-146](#/rem-146); shikigami are the controlled ones. Shikigami are not only eels: they are the animals of the Pastures, such as the Fisherman's pseudo-cormorant. See the Orem [O-REM](#/o-rem).
