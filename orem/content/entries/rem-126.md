## 2.1 Librarii Ofel Isandarìa

Librarii Ofel Isandarìa - a ring-shaped library, but titanic, as big as a planet. Here there are billions of books and/or pieces of information, including: the Bestiary (encyclopedia of the "Monsters", that is, of the magical artifacts), Information HAZARD "First Empress" (the letters of Friona, the first empress of Salolem as well as the only being of Paradis more powerful than a god), Bypass Agent (a virus made of decryption information, able to adapt to any subject), BLACKBOXXX (we already know it).
