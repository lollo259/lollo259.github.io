## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Librarii Ofel Isandarìa is a ring-shaped library, titanic and as big as a planet, holding billions of books and pieces of information. The artist draws on it to populate his own garden. It holds the Bestiary [REM-127](#/rem-127) and BLACKBOXXX [REM-999](#/rem-999).
