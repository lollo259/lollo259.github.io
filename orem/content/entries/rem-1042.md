## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23]
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: no -->
Hel (death) and Mana (desire) lived embraced together for eternity. Two titans were born, two different twins. Adam, titan, God of Power (kinetic energy, strength, speed); the other was a female, Loosipher, snake titan, asexual God of Flow. Again two titans were born, again two different twins. Poseiddon, titan, God of Seas (elektrokinesis) and Ra, titan, Goddess of Scorching (fire, lava, plasma). The third daughter was born, Aura, titan, Goddess of Ether. The fourth and fifth daughters were born simultaneously. D'inn [[REM-1045](#/rem-1045)], Goddess of the Unknown [[REM-1045](#/rem-1045)], and Anjel, intersexual winged titan; the last one is not a Goddess and she has no powers.

All of the titans knew the strength of Adam, so they chose not to interfere. Everybody except for Loosipher, who began a meticulous plan to kill him. First they planted part of themselves in the soil, giving birth to a colossal tree. Inside its one blossom, a poisoned fruit containing their morals, waiting to be eaten. Somehow they needed to persuade Adam into falling for that. But how?

On what is now called The First Day [[REM-1046](#/rem-1046)], a violent encounter between Adam and Loosipher took place. In a legendary battle, Adam pierced through the titan's body with the Sword of Ra [[REM-506](#/rem-506)], creating a breach in the flow of things. About to die, the titan mutated through their mouth into a new vessel, just like a caterpillar. But the new, female body, didn't remember anything about its past life.
