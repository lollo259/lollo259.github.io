## From the source: Djesica Imperii

Totality

Conceptual awareness:

The magician uses linguistic relativity to change their perspective on reality. The past and present events are summarized in symbols, and those symbols in one single logogram. From there, the magician is able to predict certain paths of the future based on this new way of thinking. The practice can be used to better understand and manipulate a known subject: since the persona is formed and/or influenced by memories, they are translated in single logograms, meaning that the magician knows exactly how to behave in order to influence the subject. The magician can also study its personal logograms to predict its own future, better if combined with the logograms of the surrounding world. Theoretically, if a magician produces logograms for everything it will be in the dream of ether, thus gaining omniscience, but practically that's impossible. The magicians able to produce logograms are called diviners. A diviner can also understand and speak any type of language in the world.

Magicians may also have trained in controlling and crafting their own dreams, by writing them in logograms immediately after the awakening. The reason is the lack of interest in the surrounding world that has become more predictable for them. The result is called lucid dreaming, and it can have one major purpose other than enjoyment: discovering the subconscious logograms. In a process called hypnotic reincarnation logograms of a particular persona are instructed to the subject into making it that particular persona; this is similar to transferring one's mind, way of thinking, and memories, into another, like some sort of fake immortality. The magician can also do that process to itself, either with the following ability to see that persona in its dreams, or to be able to become that persona, also like having multiple personalities that can be triggered by specific orders, or nothing at all.

The major expert of lucid dreaming is the founder of this nation itself. This practice allows him to evoke four different succubus with long tongues that grants him infinite pleasure in his sleep.

Venxia [[REM-033](#/rem-033)] is a woman with green tentacles as hair, two small red horns, elf ears, and lips and tongues instead of nipples, and a tongue inside of her vagina. She also has two long but thin red tails with a heart-like ending, and she has fawn legs. She usually appears near candles in a night setting.

Succy is a woman with red hair that shapeshifts into her demonic form with pleasure. In this form she has elf ears, four brown horns (two on the front and two smaller ones near the back), cephalopod eyes, a tail, and a pinkish skin color.

Slan is the infamous character of Berserk. She can be treated with as much pain as desired, since she feels even more pleasure like that.

Bel is the most different looking succubus. She has an extremely long tongue, big ox horns near the side of the head, elf ears, black hair, a tail with an arrow ending, and a reddish body resembling an amphibian.

Diviners are the scariest and most dangerous types of magicians, because of their knowledge of logograms they have vast knowledge of any kind, especially future predictions and persona. They also tend to be expert hypnotizers, since they need that particular craft to make the logograms. They usually don't need monk enhancements because of their predictions, but they can access those crafts if needed. The logograms are inspired by Heptapod B from the movie Arrival. Diviners have also crafted the most easy-to-comprehend language. [see chapter LANGUAGE]

Enchanters tend to have little knowledge about logograms. Instead, they reach the supercritical emotion (lust) through sexual practices.

"The atmosphere around an enchanter could not be described without thinking of arousement and sexual entertainment. The ambiguity of their look and manners makes for an immediate disadvantage for anyone. They are masters in their craft, real cheaters."

Enchanters have also introduced the first drugs to stimulate one's hormones to remain in a constant state of lustfulness. The results are constantly erect nipples, wet vaginas, and an embarrassed gaze.
