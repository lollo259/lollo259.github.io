## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Lexi servatoris Imperii [[REM-1001](#/rem-1001)] (let. Lexi savior Empire, shortening of Lexi saviour of the Empire, often shortened to Lexi Imperii [[REM-1001](#/rem-1001)]) is the second biggest empire by land in the entire world and the third biggest territory in the world. In almost all its history it was guided by an Empress, called Empress of Tomorrow. It was named after one of its Empresses. It was later changed into the Confederation of Slavery [[REM-637](#/rem-637)], a short-lived sovereign with the last empress still having full powers but not utilizing them. It ended with the new reigns taking full control of themselves.
