## From the source: Documento senza titolo
<!-- type: description | source: Documento senza titolo | translated: no -->
The Annuaki are the main inhabitants of the planet Tuonela. A technologically advanced nation of bloodthirsty titans, they are responsible for the recent genocide of angels (they hunt angels for fun), and for the destruction of Tuonela's atmosphere (they constantly test numerous atomic and hydrogen bombs). The capital of their empire is the former Silver City, where emperor Apollyon Khan resides. The Organization deploys TTF Psi-1 to eliminate Apollyon Khan.
