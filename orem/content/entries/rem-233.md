## From the source: Documento senza titolo
<!-- type: description | source: Documento senza titolo | translated: no -->
The Annuaki [[REM-320](#/rem-320)] are the main inhabitants of the planet Tuonela [[REM-212](#/rem-212)]. A technologically advanced nation of bloodthirsty titans, they are responsible for the recent genocide of angels (they hunt angels for fun), and for the destruction of Tuonela [[REM-212](#/rem-212)]'s atmosphere (they constantly test numerous atomic and hydrogen bombs). The capital of their empire is the former Silver City, where emperor Apollyon Khan resides. The Organization [[REM-300](#/rem-300)] deploys TTF Psi-1 [[REM-318](#/rem-318)] to eliminate Apollyon Khan.
