## From the source: Città
<!-- type: description | source: Città | translated: yes -->
In a legendary battle Adam kills Lucifer, creating a cut, a breach in time. It is in fact the only reason X is able to travel over time. Before he dies, he mutates, and in Orochimaru style comes out a woman (Eva). Like when a caterpillar does the mute and the butterfly only remembers very few things, Eva only remembers having to feed the fruit to Adam, but it is as if he was another person compared to Lucifer.

## Mentions in other documents: EDEN STORIA.odt
<!-- type: description | source: EDEN STORIA.odt | translated: yes -->
SCHIAVITU. One day there comes an explorer along with his crew. Hel (the Eastern Empire) sought the source of gravitational distortions detected by compasses. It is in fact the Time Breach that, breaking space-time, generates gravitational abnormalities. (DOMANDA: Why doesn't a black hole come up? Answer: There is a breach in time but not in the flow. The near material tends to change the flow of its particles, creating mutations). The source is located in the middle of the forest. They begin to dig, as they model the dug land to create a structure to protect the excavations. The excavations take place using elves.
