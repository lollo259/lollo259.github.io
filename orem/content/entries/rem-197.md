## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Zodiac was born from the vomit of Mbombo [REM-103](#/rem-103) in the Terrarium [REM-196](#/rem-196): the imperfect reflection of the body of Orpheus [REM-000](#/rem-000), the most unruly desires of the Cicisbeo [REM-117](#/rem-117), "Morphei of the demiurge", lesser muses.

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Born of [REM-103](#/rem-103)'s vomit. — Quote: "Il creato nelle fogne dei pascoli nel cielo è un Terrario. Dal vomito di Mbombo [[REM-103](#/rem-103)] nacque lo Zodiaco. La riflessione imperfetta del corpo di Orfeo [[REM-000](#/rem-000)]. O, in parole povere, i più sregolati desideri del cicisbeo. Morfei del demiurgo. Muse inferiori."

Quote (English, machine-assisted translation): "The creation in the sewers of pastures in the sky is a Terrarium. The Zodiac was born from the vomiting of [REM-103](#/rem-103). The imperfect reflection of the body of [REM-000](#/rem-000). Or, in plain words, the most unreasonable cycisbean desires. Morphei del demiurgo. Lower muscles."
