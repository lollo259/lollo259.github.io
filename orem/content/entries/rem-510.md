## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
The fourth and fifth daughters are born simultaneously. Jinn, Goddess of Desire, and Anjelique, winged titan; the last one is not a Goddess and she has no powers.

Jehoel, Abaddon also Abbadon (let. Place of Destruction) was the daughter of Hḗphaistos and Anjelique Metatron. She had a violish body colour. She died forced to lick and spread through her mouth melting metal by Hḗphaistos while being raped.

Metatron, Anjelique (let. Angel) was the fourth genit of Rangi and Papa, a winged titan with no powers. She died crucified by Adam, after he cut her wings.

Nathanael, Layla (let. Born during the night) was the daughter of Poseidon and Anjelique. She was frozen to death by Poseidon.
