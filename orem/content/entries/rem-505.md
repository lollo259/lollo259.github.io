## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Ra is a titan of Paradis: one of the secondborn twins, Ra (titan of Fire, female) and Poseidon [REM-507](#/rem-507) (titan of Water, male). The two fell in love. Ra's spinal cord was made into the Sword of Ra [REM-506](#/rem-506), with which Adam killed Lucifer [REM-504](#/rem-504). Ra appears in the "worst deaths" list. See also Ra, Goddess of Scorching [REM-1044](#/rem-1044).
