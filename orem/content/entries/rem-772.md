## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Rebels live in a dystopian future and decided to travel to the past to change events, so that they can live in a dimension where their future is positive. Members include Esmeralda [REM-773](#/rem-773), Be. [REM-774](#/rem-774) and Lala [REM-775](#/rem-775).
