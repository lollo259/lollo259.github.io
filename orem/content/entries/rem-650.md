Rodhår, Senshi (let. Warrior) is the West Anisodon.

-kaidou, Mulher (let. Female) was the only female kaidou in history, considered as strong as Sēnṭaurakaidou himself, if not stronger. She managed to beat the previous kaidou in mere seconds. Licking herself, especially her blood, expanded her adrenaline, making her a deadly opponent. Her fighting style was based on submissions. She chose to retire only after 5 years, making her the shortest reigning kaidou ever. She recieved critique numerous assassination attempts, and she was once raped by a centaur. Despite that, she managed to expand her territories over the entire lands, killing numerous centaurs. As Kaidou she used to paint her whole body in white, and in red making a tattoo-like figure on the left side of her body. On the upper body she only wears a shoulder guard on the right side, after having it injured during her fight with the previous kaidou. The guard’s belt covers her nipples.
