## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Wind is Eve's brother. When she lost contact with her body and it suddenly aged into dust, Wind spread the dust across the world, generating what is now called Life: the dust fertilised the Earth, creating plants.

Thus the wind blows, to resume the title, and knowing the political nature of the works of Rzewski is most likely a protest against war. From the title of the same name, in fact, a graphic novel by Raymond Briggs (1982, made in a film animated in 1986) was born that shows a nuclear attack in Britain by the Soviet Union, from the point of view of an elderly couple, Jim and Hilda Biggs. Life in the quiet of the rural settlement, and its sweetness, is in sharp and tragic contrast to the reality of nuclear bombing. The film begins with a radio warning that leads the couple to prepare the house for an imminent attack. When the bomb strikes, the protagonists exist in the oblivion of reality, continuing to live normally. Rzewski seems to refer to the last moments of the novella, when the two, now dying due to radiation, die suffering in the shadow and silence.
