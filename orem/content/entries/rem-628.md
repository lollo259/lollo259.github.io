## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Vilkai Rodhår, number 12 in the Bounties of Paradis (bounty 9,000,000,000), is the head of the Vilkai Klan [REM-635](#/rem-635), based on Lupus homo and Homo vulpes, whose members shapeshift into werewolves at the full moon and keep traits such as a great sense of smell, reflexes and strength. He is so strong he was named after the red hair.
