## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Forgotten Monastery is an old monastery crushed beneath a huge wreck in pieces. It is followed in the notes by the Story of the Monk.

## Catalogue record

Old monastery crushed by a huge wreck. — Quote: "Monastero dimenticato è costituito da un vecchio monastero sovrastato da un enorme relitto in pezzi. Segue Storia del monaco"
