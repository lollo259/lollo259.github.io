## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Hel system is the star system at the centre of the paracosm map. It has a diameter of about 84 AU, with the Leti debris disk [REM-280](#/rem-280) between 34.67 and 42 AU; at those two points lie the biggest dwarf planet, Doppelgangger [REM-279](#/rem-279), and the smallest, Phantasus [REM-281](#/rem-281). Tuonela [REM-212](#/rem-212) is part of it.

## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23]
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: no -->
The Hel system has a diameter of approximately 84 AU, with the Leti debris [[REM-280](#/rem-280)] (let. "detritus of the edge", its debris disk) located between 34.67 AU and 42 AU. At these two points are located respectively the biggest dwarf planet of the system, Doppelgangger (let. "double, twin", referring to Tuonela [[REM-212](#/rem-212)]) and the smallest one, Phantasus [[REM-281](#/rem-281)] (let. "fantasy"; its existence is only speculated but it has never been seen, thus the name).
