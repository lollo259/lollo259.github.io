## Description: Under my leadership, The Beyonders comprises a group of outerversal travelers
<!-- type: description | source: notes on beyonders | translated: no -->
Description: Under my leadership, The Beyonders comprises a group of outerversal travelers, stationed in the empty expanse between the realms of fantasy and actuality. Established by the SCP Foundation, The Beyonders are responsible for safeguarding the fictional universes by eliminating or deleting any sentient creatures that pose a threat to the continuity.
I have a second personality, namely The Beyonder. He is a violent and dangerous criminal with command inducement powers. Suffering from fictophilia, hypersexuality, and power incontinence, it's a dictator sailing across the outerverse with a crew of seemingly brainwashed fictional characters, nicknamed The Beyonders.
Following SCP-3812 discovery, and the subsequent confirmation of the existence of higher narratives over the fictional universe, The Beyonders are currently the sole tribunal of all lower universes, in an executive position even higher than the SCP Foundation.
The SCP Foundation has actively tried to cure The Beyonders. None of them has been successfully rescued.
Physical appearance: The Beyonder sacrificed his eyes in Anansi's Pot of Wisdom to gain knowledge of the past and many world interpretations. He also sacrificed his voice, inheriting a fraction of The Marquis of Death's powers: he can speak the Word of Wyncham making any fictional character obey his orders; his powers have cognitohazard effects on any conscious being, can't be countered, and have numerous side effects. He obtained Freya's falcon cloak and Aphrodite's girdle. He also wears the Cap of Darkness and the Aegis. He wields All Black the Necrosword in his shadow.
