## Source excerpts
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Access to the blueprint, which is located within an unknown space station orbiting the Armageddon Loop, is strictly prohibited unless necessary for continued study. Any unauthorized attempts to access the blueprint or activate REM-420 will result in immediate termination of access privileges, followed by a standard memory wipe procedure.

Lily Springs is a facility located within the Armageddon Loop, where it has been discovered that a small, but active cult has taken root. This cult, composed of both personnel working at the facility and others who have been lured into its influence, worships an idealized form of the goddess Lilith.

REM-920, known as Lilith, is contained within a specialized research facility located on the largest asteroid within the Armageddon Loop, near the infamous Lily Springs complex. The complex is designed to both house and study Lilith while also monitoring the infected personnel and deities exposed to the fungal organism. The containment area is to remain under constant surveillance by a specialized team of REM-920-affected researchers, who maintain the fungal growth and track any potential risk of contamination beyond the designated site.
