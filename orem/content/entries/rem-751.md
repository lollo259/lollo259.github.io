## From the source: Djesica Imperii
<!-- type: description | source: Djesica Imperii | translated: no -->
Roc, the mythical colossal bird, is not plausible in this setting. Instead, it has been substituted by the Quetzalcoatlus and the Argentavis Magnificens.

The Kraken is just a giant squid the size of a colossal squid, named Titanic squid. It mistakes ships for predators like sperm whales or levyatans, therefore it always attacks them. It's impossible to reach the city via sea.

Wyverns and Vampires are just Flying Foxes as big as humans.

The Loch Ness monster is an Elasmosaurus.

Jörmurgand and Grootslang are simply a Titanoboa [[REM-752](#/rem-752)].

Stymphalian birds [[REM-750](#/rem-750)] are simply Terror birds with metal beek and feathers, named Horror birds [[REM-750](#/rem-750)]. They are flightless.
