## Astral Task Force "Azrael" (draft)
<!-- type: description | source: Author's chat notes (13 Oct 2026; original Drive file not located) | translated: yes -->
From the author's "draft of the Astral Task Force 'Azrael'". In the paracosm, information about consciousness is called the "soul". Sapient beings have organs that act as valves, sealing the soul inside the body. Unblocking the valves frees the soul, which gives potentially infinite power, because the soul is the paracosm itself.

Agents: Posterity [REM-334](#/rem-334), Neuron [REM-335](#/rem-335) and Golgotha [REM-336](#/rem-336). Astral is TTF Omega-1 [REM-307](#/rem-307).

Why don't the Azrael rebel, given their unbeatable power? Originally they too were dead. Their body and mind were never fully restored, so they can only perform their functions.


## Theorem of Everything: "Trinity"
<!-- type: description | source: Author's chat notes (13 Oct 2026; original Drive file not located) | translated: yes -->
Chapter "Trinity", the last chapter of Act II of the Theorem of Everything. After the trial of the paracosm, the Azrael appear before the judge, ready to eliminate him (Hecatekali [REM-148](#/rem-148) being part of the paracosm). Posterity [REM-334](#/rem-334) spreads his wings for the first time in history; Neuron [REM-335](#/rem-335) fully unsheathes his sword [REM-191](#/rem-191) for the first time; Golgotha [REM-336](#/rem-336) summons the cemetery of eyes. At the same time, Hecatekali returns to her "executioner" form for the final clash. The chapter ends with a clash between two impossible forces.
