## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Natasha, born Olivia, is the drug dealer in The Bounty Hunter. In 1983 she changed her name, scared by her father's enemies; in 1991 she moved to a small town wanting to change her life, sick of her own crimes; in 1992 a bounty hunter [REM-869](#/rem-869) is sent to kill her and finds her drunk in a pub in a red dress. Her father is [REM-870](#/rem-870). Flagged explicit.
