## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Amoux Vilkas ("Eagle Wolf"), number 16 in the Bounties of Paradis (bounty 2,859,000,300), is second in command of the Vilkai Klan [REM-635](#/rem-635): 185 cm, 140 kg, very muscular with black hair, wearing a werewolf mantle and a 224 cm claymore on his back. His brother is Groyl [REM-632](#/rem-632).
