## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Tank ants are one of the roles of the knight ants of Paradis: with an expandable abdomen storing large quantities of liquid. Colonies also include queens, attackers, defenders, hunters, spotters, diggers and tanks (see [REM-1061](#/rem-1061)).
