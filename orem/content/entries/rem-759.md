## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
The madman who kills all passengers. — Quote: "Personaggio 7: il killer. Alla fine del romanzo il pazzo, o finto pazzo, uccide tutti i passeggeri, stufo della loro impossibilità nel comprendere la Realtà. Giustifica le proprie azioni fingendosi pazzo, quindi diventando ciò che le altre persone pensavano che fosse."

Quote (English, machine-assisted translation): "Character 7: the killer. At the end of the novel the madman, or fake crazy, kills all the passengers, sick of their impossibility to understand the Reality. He justifies his actions by pretending crazy, so becoming what other people thought it was."

## From the source: ROMANZO FILOSOFICO.docx

PROVOCED TYPE FROM A UNIFORMITY PIATTA

(monotonia)

Criticism towards man’s position towards reason: man defines certain things as incomprehensible things, because our minds cannot explain or conceive them. Man has no confidence in himself. Our mind is, on the contrary, complete with everything. We understand anything.
