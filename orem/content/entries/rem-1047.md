## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23]
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: no -->
Nimfs (short for "God de Nimf", let. "nymphs of God"),  the non-cetacean primates, sapient inhabitants of Paradis, are characterized by their bipedalism and high intelligence. They are omnivorous animals with high metabolism. Their anatomy depends on their gender, which can either be intersex (breasts and penis), female (breasts and vagina) or rare male (penis). Nimfs are characterized by having dense musculature, slend skeleton (making them both graceful and strong at the same time), protruding ears that allow for better hearing, and sharp eyesight with color cells in their iris that change based on their emotions (a feature common in mammals on this planet). They have a tail for better balance on trees. The tail is located, following the sacrum, in between the glutes, and the buttocks are partially pushed to either side. Therefore they are great climbers, but have difficulties to sit. All members of this species feed only from liquids and chewing. All female nimfs give birth to their offspring by laying eggs as large as their pelvic bone. After hatching, their babies grow at fast speeds and become fertile. Their bodies mature after 2-3 years. Nimfs train in agility and strength without the usage of any weapon, and are extremely stealthy and dangerous in forest biomes. After 15 years of training they can be classified as "screaming howlers", an oxymoron to describe their abilities. Living in tribal autarchies, where the rulers are the strongest, the most confident warriors go on a mission to kill a great ape and bring back a trophy, a symbol of their victory.

Pluvial biomes near the coastal regions have mangrove forests, while in their internal regions they have ficus forests. Nimfs crafts their houses suspended by trunks, and are formed by leather tents. They are built with the help of a particular slime. The uadi (let. "korowai") are houses placed on top of trees, while the jtiloo (let. "stilt") are houses placed on bodies of water.
