## From the source: EDEN.odt
<!-- type: description | source: EDEN.odt | translated: yes -->
Sicaria is sent as a slave to the city of Abella. Here he escapes slavery, penetrating the queen's palace and killing it. He starts wearing his lingerie as a joke, and releases some slaps. Everyone thought, in fact, that it was the queen to give such orders. Once free, the slaves become the Sicaries, a special body of murderers directed by the queen, wearing lingerie. They become so feared that they are considered as the ninth tribe of Eden, and thus Sicaria as the ninth Wonder. So far nobody knows the real person of the Queen of Abella.

## Mentions in other documents: LEGGI DEL PARADISO
<!-- type: description | source: LEGGI DEL PARADISO | translated: yes -->
Sicaries are the first in order of importance.

Before hearing the task of the assassins, then before becoming him, they swear to commit suicide in case they were contrary to conditions.

In fact all sicaries are exchanged, apparently as slaves, with the Lexi Empire, in exchange for weapons and objects. The Empire manages to circumvent the agreement by sending terrible weapons that are, even though always, weapons. On the other hand, the sicaries are sprinkled in erogenous zones by the Edere venom; this allows them to kill enemies with sexual intercourse or with foreplay, taking it by mouth, without stealing or suspicious moves since its effect on man takes several days. Most of the time, for the beauty of the elves are sent to the nobles or major warriors, which allows to eliminate the greater enemies without difficulty. In case they are mistreated more than expected, being formidable warriors usually have the best. They are destined to never return to the motherland again.
