## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Vilkai Klan is a werewolf clan based on Lupus homo and Homo vulpes, headed by Vilkai Rodhår [REM-628](#/rem-628) with Amoux Vilkas [REM-631](#/rem-631) second in command. Members shapeshift into werewolves at the full moon. The uMvuni Klan is related to it by blood.

## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia

Abandoned as a baby, she was raised by the Vilkai Klan.

uMvuni Klan (let. Clan of Reapers) is a clan bloodly related to the Vilkai Klan. It’s based in the most outer peripheral regions of the Lexi Empire. They have purple, violet, blue, red tattoos. They gain protection from the Vilkai Klan.

Vilkai Klan (let. Clan of Wolves) is a group of individuals capable of shapeshifting into werewolves during a full moon. They also have incredible features such as a great sense of smell, reflexes, and strength. Every member has Vilkas as its surname, meaning “wolf”. Females cannot transform into werewolves but they possess irremovable features such as long nails and canines, wolf ears, and multiple tails; they are more agile than men, but overall they are weaker. The Vilkai Klan is notable for being pacific.

End of the Vilkai Klan. During the Holy Moon [[REM-1027](#/rem-1027)], they attack the main capital, Lexy.
