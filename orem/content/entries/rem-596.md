SCHIAVITU. One day there comes an explorer along with his crew. Hel (the Eastern Empire) sought the source of gravitational distortions detected by compasses. It is in fact the Time Breach that, breaking space-time, generates gravitational abnormalities. (DOMANDA: Why doesn't a black hole come up? Answer: There is a breach in time but not in the flow. The near material tends to change the flow of its particles, creating mutations). The source is located in the middle of the forest. They begin to dig, as they model the dug land to create a structure to protect the excavations. The excavations take place using elves.

They can find the material that causes distortions. The closeness to such material, however, hurts most explorers. Elves are, though little more resistant to radiation, because they have lived in Eden for millennia. They are therefore used for the transport of the material. Most people die. Those who survive are used for a variety of things. To make explorers more resistant, preventing death, one opts for organ transplantation. The genetic resistance of elves arouses great interest in Hel, who sends numerous scientists to perform experiments. Some elves are altered the brain, to make them completely obedient; they also come to make them spontaneously propensity to serve, in various ways. To others, or to the same, a fusion with Hel animals is attempted, to create new species more resistant or powerful. These experiments are made possible thanks to the genetic mutations of elves. Among these experiments some elves are melted with plants. Only one manages completely; others tend to lose pieces becoming substantially sentient water.

It was NEW. One of the elves, after seeing all these abuses for almost two centuries, unconsciously awakens a hallucinating power linked to the material that caused distortion. He can horribly increase the level of his own radiation, killing all explorers and even different elves. To prevent someone from returning to the structure that gives the strange material, he basically writes a ban on the building. All elves agree not to tell the next generation of the truth of the building, made a temple. After all artificial abuses the elves create a lifestyle preventing further change of nature. They decide not to build anything, houses, weapons and clothes included. The modified elves, now genetically changed, live free in nature, destroying the fauna and flora of the forest. Over 500 years only trees and insects remain. In addition, the genetics of these elves change much faster than normal, so much so that they become completely different species.

It was OK. By now the ancient victims are real species. Let's see centauri, elves with floral appearances or felines.

Lifestyle becomes a true religion. Still afraid, Hel starts negotiations with Eden. Knowing their religion, they provide boats and weapons in exchange for elves, to be able to study them. From here comes the rigid military society of Eden.
