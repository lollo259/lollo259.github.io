## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Lancillotto Bordeaux (Sir Lancillotto, Sire Maniac), number 6 in the Bounties of Paradis (career bounty 8,999,998,000), is the most influential hunter and collector in history: a prince of the City of Love [REM-695](#/rem-695), one of the most sadistic and violent people on the planet, who collects exotic beings from all over the world, including Furia Jehoel.
