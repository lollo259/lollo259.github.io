## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Nyctophile, or Incubus Morningstar, is the dual entity formed by Ishtar [REM-115](#/rem-115) and the Cicisbeo [REM-117](#/rem-117), said to satisfy one's deepest desire. It is always present in the mind except before the light, and all sentient beings are said to have their own nyctophile. It controls the body of Orpheus [REM-000](#/rem-000), who never turns to look at his shadow. Flagged explicit.
