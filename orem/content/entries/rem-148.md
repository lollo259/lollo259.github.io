## Technical observations of the Orem (Saints, Four Horsemen, story beats)
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
An ant is nothing compared to a praying mantis. But the same insect, if pitted against an entire anthill, would be completely annihilated. This was the main concept behind the theorem (the Orem), an organization of the lower creatures created to unite all creatures and defeat the gods. But there is still a big problem: if all creatures form an anthill, a single god is more like a diplodocus. But the Orem is not a simple anthill: its units attack the body of the gods like a virus. When the Created is at war with its own Creator, the mind collapses. The domain is no longer dominated by the Artist. The Orem studies the structure of the divine, of the pastures in the sky. It codifies its parts, in order to bring it to collapse. The council of the gods, the Wardogs (as the Machinist calls them, but as Ion Henri also called them), is composed of five fragments of the artist's childhood. Below I report some technical observations of the Orem.

The four horsemen—inspired by Yuki-onna, Nietzsche, Camus and Lovecraft. The Four Horsemen are the main commanders of the Celestial Meridian. Each of them is assigned an army. They are called to arms by a horn. Phasis, Bringer of Death, is the commander of the 1st division. Her favourite weapon is Diamonds Are Forever, a sword made of diamonds and ice, an artifact she can draw from any part of her body. The artist thinks of every single way in which death can cut him off from the rest of the world. Every single way. Mutoryu Oiseaux techniques (from the weakest to the strongest): Hanauta Sanchou: Yahazu Giri - the user moves so fast that the blow is noticed only after a few seconds; Hanauta Sanchou: Yuki no mai - the user grabs the sword with both hands and moves it from behind the back to in front of them. The coldness of the sharp blade's movement and its extreme precision, combined with the sudden stop, create cold vapours and freeze the frontal attack. In practice, the target cannot move.; Hanauta Sanchou: Gamengiri - the user stands with their back to the opponent and the sword in front of them, held with both hands. The blade faces the user, who turns quickly and strikes the opponent; Hanauta Sanchou: Harakiri - the sword is in the right hand and the left hand is above the other. It is positioned with the hilt on the right side of the abdomen. The user then pierces the opponent on the left side of the abdomen; Hanauta Sanchou: Yakkodori - the sword is in the user's right hand, and is extended with the tip to the right, touching the ground. The user then launches a projectile slash, which will not stop for miles; Hanauta Sanchou: Shibire - same position as Yuki no mai, but the blade is held with one hand. The target's body will notice the attack after a few hours, even days; Warai Sanchou (laughing Sanchou) - some of the previous techniques are performed with the opposite side of the blade and in order of continuity, resulting in a larger cut because of the greater difficulty; Aisusukai - The sword remains still and the user rests a hand on the side of the blade. Any subsequent attack will pass through the side of the sword. It is a defensive technique; Warai Sanchou: Yakkodori - identical to Yakkodori, but reversed. The cut will propagate behind the user and will be much larger. The target is behind the user. [English in source from here:] Warai Sanchou: Baccarat - basically a reversed Yuki no mai without putting the sword behind the back. The force generated by the slash is so great that the slash itself can appear in any direction, and the user wishes for their luck to slash the opponent; Gyojin Sanchou: Surasshu - a simple sword slash with the Fishman Karate technique.
