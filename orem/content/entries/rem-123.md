[REM-000](#/rem-000) songs are eternal. They have no end. This raises an ontological question: if a song never ends, when creation completes? The answer is: never. The creations of [REM-000](#/rem-000) are not *complete*, but *in progress*. The singing universes are not finished and then left to themselves, but continually sung in existence. What happens to interrupted songs? The question presupposes that the songs can be interrupted, but this is impossible if they are eternal. An eternal song cannot be interrupted because it does not flow in the linear time — it is always in place, always present, always resonant. [REM-000](#/rem-000) cannot "dis-cantate" something, it cannot destroy through an anti-canto. Destruction is out of its domain. The soul of [REM-000](#/rem-000), in the Church of Light, carries out three eternal actions: 1. ** Sing.*** He never stops singing. The song is its function, its essence, its reason to be. Singing is not something that the soul * does*, but something that the soul *is*. 2. **Put your eyes in the void.** Not any vacuum, but *the* empty — the primordial absence, the space before creation, the nothing from which everything emerges. Looking at the void is watching your creative act in its origin. 3. **You never look at your body.** This is active denial, deliberate refusal. The soul knows that the body is there, in the shadow, corrupt, played by [REM-115](#/rem-115). But he chooses not to look. Why? Because looking would recognize, and recognizing would have to act, and acting would require interrupting the song, and interrupting the song would mean the cessation of creation.
