## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Sword of Ra was made by Adam [REM-503](#/rem-503), who melted Ra's [REM-505](#/rem-505) spinal cord with his own flames. With it he pierced Lucifer [REM-504](#/rem-504) in a legendary battle, creating the Breach in Time [REM-615](#/rem-615); without it the two would have clashed forever. Related passages are explicit.
