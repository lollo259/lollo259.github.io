## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Arcane's ship is the vessel of expedition XLII, during which the Machinist visited the Silver City [REM-186](#/rem-186). Deities authorised for daily reuse are brought back to it.

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Ship of expedition XLII. — Quote: "Io: Capisco. Sei mai stato sulla nave dell'Arcano?"

## From the source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
Concluding statement: The deity has been authorized for daily reuse. They will be brought back to the Arcan ship.

Preface: Observation register, recorded by [REM-307](#/rem-307), of an interaction between the Macchiniist and two residents of the Silver City, during the XLII expedition of the Arcano ship.
