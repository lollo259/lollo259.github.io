## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Jelena Demiurge ("Torch"), nicknamed "The Monster", is an Airman. She lives with an apparent illness that is actually her clan's power: her cells multiply chaotically, changing her genes. The explicit result is kept in the source excerpts.
