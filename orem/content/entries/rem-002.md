## Edward Lawrence (page)

Edward Laurence (alternatively written Edward Lawrence, c. 2001 -), better known as Captain Nero (from oz-nero-L), is an Italian pirate and collector who operates across the fictional omniverse. He is known for having the word of God that allows him to speak with the authority of an omniversal entity over fictional characters, effectively commanding anyone to do as he says. He has a bounty of £3,140,000,000 and commands a crew. Each member is from a different universe. 
The first-mate of the crew is Kuzan, better known as Aokiji, a former marine admiral and Tenth Titanic Captain of the Blackbeard pirates. He ate the Hio Hio no Mi, making him an ice man capable of freezing things up to absolute zero. He has a bounty of £4,480,000,000.
The second-mate of the crew is King. He has meta luck powers, meaning that he has infinite luck. He has a bounty of £4,000,000,000.
