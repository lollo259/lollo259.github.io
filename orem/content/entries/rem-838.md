## From the source: Fairy Tales
<!-- type: description | source: Fairy Tales | translated: no -->
Tonight, the air grew colder, the silence heavier. As her slender fingers danced over the canvas of his chiseled chest, she felt his muscles tighten, his breaths shallow. Her heart skipped a beat, a sudden jolt of terror. "Zoro?" she breathed, the name a shivering whisper. His eyes remained closed, his body unyielding. The pulse beneath her hand grew faint, and she realized, with a sinking dread, that his heart had ceased its rhythmic dance. Panic surged through her veins, a torrent of despair. In desperation, she turned to her only weapon - the fiery passion that burned within her. Straddling him, she began to move, her soft moans a silent plea for his life. Each kiss, each bite, each suckle a declaration of love and life, a fervent attempt to reanimate his still form. Her body writhed against his, a serpent of passion entwined with the lifeless marble of her beloved. Despite her exhaustion, she continued, her breaths coming in ragged gasps, her hips moving with a determination born of fear. The night dragged on, an eternity of agony and hope, until finally, his chest rose, his breaths deepened. He was alive, but only just. Her love had held the Reaper at bay, but she knew the battle was far from over. The cold embrace of the night remained, whispering dark secrets of loss and grief. Yet, she clung to him, her lover, her hero, her lifeline in this world of endless struggle.
