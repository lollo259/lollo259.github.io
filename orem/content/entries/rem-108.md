This was the main concept behind the theorem (the Orem), an organization of the lower creatures created to unite all creatures and defeat the gods. But there is still a big problem: if all creatures form an anthill, a single god is more like a diplodocus. But the Orem is not a simple anthill: its units attack the body of the gods like a virus. When the Created is at war with its own Creator, the mind collapses. The domain is no longer dominated by the Artist. The Orem studies the structure of the divine, of the pastures in the sky. It codifies its parts, in order to bring it to collapse. A paradox arises here: if Prisma breaks instantly, how can some fragments "derive" from others? [REM-000](#/rem-000) generates [REM-107](#/rem-107) and Falari. The lamp generates [REM-110](#/rem-110). [REM-115](#/rem-115) merges with the cicisbeus to form the nightmare. This apparent genealogy is real from the internal point of view of Paracosm, but illusory from the point of view of Prism in its totality. It is as if we observe a photograph of an explosion: we see fragments that seem to derive from others, but the explosion itself is an instantaneous event.

The sons of [REM-000](#/rem-000) are his own prayers. [REM-107](#/rem-107) is the god of destruction. [REM-107](#/rem-107) does not forget, does not exile created by paracosm. It erases it completely. It hurts deep down. It's trauma, the indelible scar. It's an invaluable force. Moreover, the sword [REM-109](#/rem-109) is knowledge, curiosity, the burning flame that the artist never turns out. Falari, on the other hand, is the goddess of time. Death. Not of the chronological time (the succession of instants), but of the time as fabric, as a manipulatable dimension, as a substance that can be be bent, ironed, frozen. These are the pages of life, wet by the melma of memories, torn, consumed by life itself. They become sand, debris, an infinite desert around the fertile soil of the pastures in the sky. They are described as "the strongest of which will never exist. "Why? Because destruction and time are the two most absolute forces: the artist does not resist the melancholy of pain, and inexorably awaits death. [REM-103](#/rem-103) is different. Born from the body of [REM-000](#/rem-000) and [REM-115](#/rem-115) — not from prayers (like [REM-107](#/rem-107) and Falari) but from flesh. He is a carnal, not spiritual son. And his power is peculiar: he is demiurgo as the father, but he creates everything through vomiting. The vomiting is expulsion, rejection, emptying the body. What comes out of the body through vomiting is what the body cannot contain, cannot digest, cannot assimilate. [REM-103](#/rem-103) creates expelling — its creation is cosmic nausea, involuntary act of regurgitation. The creation actively wants to annihilate the entire paracosm, creating a new reality with [REM-103](#/rem-103), in their image and likeness. The art that controls the artist.

[REM-000](#/rem-000)' soul doesn't know what the body does with [REM-115](#/rem-115) in the shadows. He doesn't want to know. He deliberately chooses not to look. This choice is the only form of agency that remains to the soul: the refusal to see. [REM-000](#/rem-000) does not blame [REM-115](#/rem-115) for corruption. He doesn't blame himself. It simply accepts everything as its own creation. Because *is your creation. [REM-115](#/rem-115) exists because [REM-000](#/rem-000) sang her into being. Cicisbeus exists because it is the body of [REM-000](#/rem-000). Corruption exists because [REM-000](#/rem-000) has created conditions for corruption. This acceptance is not resignation, but ontological understanding: the creator is responsible for everything he creates, including non-intentional consequences, including perversions, including corruptions. He can't disregard his creations by saying "I didn't want this. "All that exists in Paracosm is there because [REM-000](#/rem-000) sang a world in which he could exist. [REM-000](#/rem-000) no longer has his own thoughts. He is unable to want, desire, decide. It is fully automated, mechanized, reduced to pure function. [REM-107](#/rem-107), exiled, plans to return with his sister Falari to destroy all creations and free [REM-000](#/rem-000). But this is the will of [REM-107](#/rem-107), not of [REM-000](#/rem-000). [REM-000](#/rem-000) does not ask to be freed because he can no longer ask for anything. [REM-000](#/rem-000) doesn't love [REM-115](#/rem-115). It doesn't feel emotions. Love would require hearing, and hearing would require connection with the body, and the body is irreparably separated. But you can hear the music. The celestial organ produced by the nictophile (the molten entity of [REM-115](#/rem-115) and Cicisbeus) reaches the soul. Not as an aesthetic experience, but as a spell — vibration that modifies the state of the soul, which maintains separation, which perpetuates the split.
