## Description: Under my leadership, The Beyonders comprises a group of outerversal travelers
<!-- type: description | source: notes on beyonders | translated: no -->
Description: Under my leadership, The Beyonders [[REM-001](#/rem-001)] comprises a group of outerversal travelers, stationed in the empty expanse between the realms of fantasy and actuality. Established by the SCP Foundation, The Beyonders [[REM-001](#/rem-001)] are responsible for safeguarding the fictional universes by eliminating or deleting any sentient creatures that pose a threat to the continuity.
I have a second personality, namely The Beyonder [[REM-002](#/rem-002)]. He is a violent and dangerous criminal with command inducement powers. Suffering from fictophilia, hypersexuality, and power incontinence, it's a dictator sailing across the outerverse with a crew of seemingly brainwashed fictional characters, nicknamed The Beyonders [[REM-001](#/rem-001)].
Following SCP-3812 [[REM-902](#/rem-902)] discovery, and the subsequent confirmation of the existence of higher narratives over the fictional universe, The Beyonders [[REM-001](#/rem-001)] are currently the sole tribunal of all lower universes, in an executive position even higher than the SCP Foundation.
The SCP Foundation has actively tried to cure The Beyonders [[REM-001](#/rem-001)]. None of them has been successfully rescued.
Physical appearance: The Beyonder [[REM-002](#/rem-002)] sacrificed his eyes in Anansi's Pot of Wisdom [[REM-037](#/rem-037)] to gain knowledge of the past and many world interpretations. He also sacrificed his voice, inheriting a fraction of The Marquis of Death [[REM-228](#/rem-228)]'s powers: he can speak the Word of Wyncham [[REM-043](#/rem-043)] making any fictional character obey his orders; his powers have cognitohazard effects on any conscious being, can't be countered, and have numerous side effects. He obtained Freya's falcon cloak [[REM-038](#/rem-038)] and Aphrodite's girdle [[REM-039](#/rem-039)]. He also wears the Cap of Darkness [[REM-042](#/rem-042)] and the Aegis. He wields All Black the Necrosword in his shadow.
