## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Selussa is the most skilful and feared warrior of the Eastern Empire [REM-574](#/rem-574). Born in the Lexi Empire and sent against the Eastern Empire at five as punishment for rebelling against slavery, she survived 15 years in desolate northern Utopya, then joined the Eastern Empire to avenge her motherland.
