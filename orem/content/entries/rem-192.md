## Author interview: Bypass Agent
<!-- type: interview | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
Author: In a very physical manner, your body could be sold in markets, as a piece of furniture or trophy; if an artist buys you, you'll be utilized as displayed art, becoming a statue or similar. Your body could also be treated as food, especially in the arcipelagos. For parades in the Laka Imperii, you could be lobotomized and revived. But one thing is certain; if you were a magician, your body WILL be molded into magical potions. [Translated from Italian:] For the Orems, your brain could be replaced with the Bypass Agent™ formula, and you will become a brainless agent, guided by the formula. Or you could be fed to BLACKBOXX. For political reasons, you could be part of a marriage even if you are dead.
