## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Bloodsuckers, Layla (let. Born during the night) was one of the Four Horsewomen, the best warriors in the entire continent of Araceli. She was strangled to death by her adoptive father, Kemonokaidou.

Demiurge, Lašiša (let. Salmon-colored) dresses with the remains of a young Argentavis Magnificens that she killed. She tames a gruesome cave bear with relative ease. She’s one of the many wives of Kemonokaidou and she has four children, all males. She has no power regardless of her origin clan; in fact, the only strange attribute about her is her hair colour. She died after being injured and laying down after the Battle of the Four Kingdoms, trampled by a herd of horses. She died the next day, 11 hours after that.

-kaidou, Kemono (let. Road), original name Mirai Hatsujikai (let. Future / Agricoltor) is the strongest Kaidou that has ever lived, and the last one.
