## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23]
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: yes -->
Worms are multipods that lost their legs.

Solitaires (phronima sedentaria).

Knight ants have different roles:

Queen ants, 2 per colony, making it a diarchy.

Attacker ants, tusk-horn mouth.

Defender ants, head shield.

Hunter ants, trap-jaw mouths.

Spotter ants, winged.

Digger ants, strong mandibles to excavate.

Tank ants, expandable abdomen to store large quantities of liquid.

[REM-1062](#/rem-1062) are animals with four limbs.
