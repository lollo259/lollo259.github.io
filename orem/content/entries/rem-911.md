## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Lily Springs (Object Class: Keter), the "blossom of Lilith", is a facility in the Armageddon Loop [REM-248](#/rem-248) whose exact coordinates are classified. A small but active cult has taken root there, made up of facility personnel and others lured in, worshipping an idealised form of the goddess Lilith [REM-920](#/rem-920). Its doctrine centres on fertility and sexual pleasure; its rituals cause an intense fixation on sexuality and a breakdown of moral boundaries. The explicit details are kept in the source excerpts.

## Containment
<!-- type: article -->
The facility is under constant surveillance; anyone exposed to its teachings is quarantined immediately and any interaction is prohibited. Unauthorised visitors are detained for psychological evaluation and reconditioning. Lilith is cultivated in an enormous pit at the site.

## Description
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Description:
Lily Springs is a facility located within the Armageddon Loop [[REM-248](#/rem-248)], where it has been discovered that a small, but active cult has taken root. This cult, composed of both personnel working at the facility and others who have been lured into its influence, worships an idealized form of the goddess Lilith.
The belief system that has emerged from this facility is centered around dual themes of fertility and sexual pleasure. Central to their doctrine is the representation of Lilith in the form of sexual imagery, specifically the act of procreation and its subsequent indulgence in sexual pleasure. Symbolic representations frequently depict a penis as both penetrating the womb, representing the continuation of life, and extending toward the mouth, symbolizing oral pleasure disconnected from reproduction.
The cult’s religious practices blend the themes of survival, pleasure, and indulgence. The cult’s ceremonies involve intense rituals where members perform acts of devotion, often related to the worship of Lilith as a figure embodying these dual aspects. These rituals have been shown to cause significant psychological effects on individuals, leading to an intense fixation on sexuality and a breakdown of traditional moral boundaries.
Cult members demonstrate a deep reverence for Lilith, viewing her as a guiding force of both creation and sensuality. The worship of Lilith has led to the construction of numerous altars, shrines, and depictions of the goddess, many of which emphasize sexual symbols as part of their rituals.

## Psychological Effects
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Psychological Effects:
Exposure to the teachings and rituals of the Lily Springs cult has proven to have severe psychological consequences. Individuals who have been indoctrinated into the cult often exhibit obsessive behavior focused on the symbolic imagery of Lilith. These individuals show an extreme deviation from normal societal behavior, with increasing tendencies towards indulgence in hedonistic activities, often at the expense of personal relationships or societal duties.
Additionally, cult members frequently express an almost fanatical attachment to the idea of Lilith as a goddess of dual purpose—one who represents both the survival of the species and the pursuit of personal pleasure. Those who have had prolonged exposure to the cult’s ceremonies often begin to lose their ability to distinguish between pleasure-seeking behavior and moral or ethical considerations.

The spores are particularly effective at infiltrating the minds of those who come into contact with them, causing a slow degradation of cognitive and physical autonomy.
