## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Lašiša Argentavis, number 14 in the Bounties of Paradis (bounty 3,000,000,000), is a Tamer in the Army of Öý [REM-636](#/rem-636). She dresses in the remains of a young Argentavis she killed and tames a cave bear with ease. She is one of Kemonokaidou's [REM-525](#/rem-525) wives, with four sons, and has no power despite her clan; only her hair colour is unusual.
