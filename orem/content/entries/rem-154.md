## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Four Horsemen, inspired by Yuki-onna, Nietzsche, Camus and Lovecraft, are the main commanders of the Celestial Meridian [REM-171](#/rem-171). Each is assigned an army and they are called to arms by a horn. Phasis, Bearer of Death, commands the 1st division. In the lower dimension they are half transformed.
