In the first circle the artist creates a tulpa, a being inside the mind, a reflection of his strengths. He sits on an unexistent throne, as the center of that church beyond all self. The [REM-123](#/rem-123). His body is no longer owned by the artist, because the nictophile controls it. His Mirror is the Overwhelmed Man, the puppet master. His avatar is the Black God, who once took a backpack full of stars and laid them in paracosm as [REM-102](#/rem-102). His first son is Mbombo, and he continues to throw up these horrible scenarios I have prepared for you. Forgive me, my children. My tulpa reigns supreme and if you refuse His baptism, your souls will remain arid.

As you reflected, [REM-110](#/rem-110) underwent a very reckless and shocking ceremony. He celebrated a black Mass.

The sons of [REM-000](#/rem-000) are his own prayers. [REM-107](#/rem-107) is the god of destruction. [REM-107](#/rem-107) does not forget, does not exile created by paracosm. It erases it completely. It hurts deep down. It's trauma, the indelible scar. It's an invaluable force. Moreover, the sword [REM-109](#/rem-109) is knowledge, curiosity, the burning flame that the artist never turns out. [REM-108](#/rem-108), on the other hand, is the goddess of time. Death. Not of the chronological time (the succession of instants), but of the time as fabric, as a manipulatable dimension, as a substance that can be be bent, ironed, frozen. These are the pages of life, wet by the melma of memories, torn, consumed by life itself. They become sand, debris, an infinite desert around the fertile soil of the pastures in the sky. They are described as "the strongest of which will never exist. "Why? Because destruction and time are the two most absolute forces: the artist does not resist the melancholy of pain, and inexorably awaits death. Mbombo is different. Born from the body of [REM-000](#/rem-000) and [REM-115](#/rem-115) — not from prayers (like [REM-107](#/rem-107) and [REM-108](#/rem-108)) but from flesh. He is a carnal, not spiritual son. And his power is peculiar: he is demiurgo as the father, but he creates everything through vomiting. The vomiting is expulsion, rejection, emptying the body. What comes out of the body through vomiting is what the body cannot contain, cannot digest, cannot assimilate. Mbombo creates expelling — its creation is cosmic nausea, involuntary act of regurgitation. The creation actively wants to annihilate the entire paracosm, creating a new reality with Mbombo, in their image and likeness. The art that controls the artist.

Or perhaps — and this is the most disturbing possibility — [REM-000](#/rem-000)' body is waiting to be born again as something totally different. Not as a reconnection with the soul (that possibility is precluded until the cup exists), but as a new entity. As if nictoà SI+ was the seed from which a tienne will eventually be born a new god. Or maybe, he has already been born. Mbombo.
