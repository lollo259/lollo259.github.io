## From the source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: no -->
Virgo (Homo persephone) is a term referring to a specimen of bipedal/pinned sapient organisms living in the waters of Paradis. Common individuals have a humanoid body, and a tail. A virgo is the peak of humanoid form on the trees of Paradis. Virgo live in the northern hemisphere, in mangrove forests biomes, mostly around the mangrove branches on the sea.
