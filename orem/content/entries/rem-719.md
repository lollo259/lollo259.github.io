## Mentions in other documents: The Beyonders 2023
<!-- type: description | source: The Beyonders 2023 | translated: no -->
The Hollow of Lily [[REM-044](#/rem-044)] is a colossal flesh pit, with its deeper point being the goddess Lilith's main body. Her intestinal villi spread out and expanded from her anus and her mouth, merging into gigantic flesh walls. No one that ventures inside is ever able to reach her body, for the amount of tentacles and obstacles is such that it makes walking and advancing impossible. Researchers Betsy Braddock and Diana TBA, thanks to their superhuman abilities, are able to withstand and rebel against the tentacles, therefore they're often sent to explore and map the pit. The tentacles grab and rape whoever comes in contact with them, and lay eggs in all available orifices. Sometimes they hatch, hence the name Mother of all succubus, procreating other succubus.
