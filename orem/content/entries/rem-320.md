## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Annuaki are the main inhabitants of Tuonela [REM-212](#/rem-212): a technologically advanced nation of bloodthirsty titans responsible for the recent genocide of angels, whom they hunt for fun, and for destroying Tuonela's atmosphere with constant atomic and hydrogen bomb tests. Their capital is the former Silver City, where emperor Apollyon Khan [REM-233](#/rem-233) resides.
