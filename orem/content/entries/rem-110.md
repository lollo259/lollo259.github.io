## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Aphrodite, the Untouchable Muse, is the artist's muse: the first woman and the most beautiful creature of the paracosm, living in the gardens of Eden. Her chapter in the Quadrante (VI) names her three aspects as Aphrodite, Hathor [REM-112](#/rem-112) and Eve [REM-069](#/rem-069); per the author they are kept as separate entries.

## In the Quadrante
<!-- type: article -->
The artist painted her several times and never sinned with her or hurt her: she is the part of art that resists temptation, alongside the Lamp [REM-104](#/rem-104). She is also called Orpheus' daughter. When she sought her freedom, the text describes jealousy and a black Mass celebrated before Orpheus [REM-000](#/rem-000); afterwards the narrator forgot her existence. The text contains explicit material.
