## From the source: Città
<!-- type: description | source: Città | translated: yes -->
Lucifer, who wants to return to darkness in such a way that Rangi and Pope can gather, creates the Seed of Time, and plant it. In fact, he wants to make the Universe grow old in order to turn it off completely, returning to the Primordial Darkness. Lucifer is a huge serpent (you would like to gigantoboa). Adam is the firstborn of mankind, the Great Father.
