## From the source: Djesica Imperii
<!-- type: description | source: Djesica Imperii | translated: no -->
In this planetary system there are four planets. The inner ones include Tuonela [[REM-212](#/rem-212)] (finnish for "underworld"), named after the fact that it's only visible when it is eclipsing the star, and therefore it appears pitch black; Paradis (french for "paradise"), the planet home to different, huge lifeforms thanks to its low gravity; Hathor [[REM-112](#/rem-112)] (egyptian for "goddess of beauty, sensuality and fertility") is the moon of Paradis. There's also an asteroid belt. The outer planets are Ander Reus (afrikaans for "distant other"), a gas giant with accretion disks; Venus Lacrima [[REM-735](#/rem-735)] (latin for "tears of Venus, goddess of love, sex and beauty"), an ice giant with storms and rains of glass.

Ander Reus (let. "outer giant") is a gas giant and the third planet from Hel, orbiting around its star at an average distance of 13.34 AU. It has a radius of 108,307 kilometers, making it by far the largest planet in the system. It has various rings. The planet has a magnitude of -22.28 and it's extremely bright at night, making it visible with the naked eye.
