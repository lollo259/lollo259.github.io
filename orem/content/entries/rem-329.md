## Cameraman (author's notes)
<!-- type: description | source: Author's chat notes (13 Oct 2026; original Drive file not located) | translated: yes -->
Based on the cameraman meme. He survives the most devastating fights and films anything at any speed; he is as fast as the Flash. A punch thrown at the camera is blocked by a first-person arm. Scenes that show him are photos, videos or reflections; a change of scene means he has teleported. The ice man freezes the whole of Yggdrasil (freezing time) and even defeats the cameraman, so nothing can be seen any more.

The Beyonders [REM-001](#/rem-001) monitor the multiverse by capturing or eliminating the most dangerous entities of each universe; captured ones sometimes join TTF Psi-1 "Tartarus" [REM-318](#/rem-318). Each universe is a different frequency or vibration: you vibrate to change universe.
