## TTF Delta-1 (mentions Gamma-1)
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
TTF Delta-1 ("Privateers" “Pirates”)
Task Force Mission: Terra Task Force Delta-1 is formed by military personnel deployed in the open sea. TTF Gamma-1 personnel are required to mix with the pisces specimens, in order to activate an Astral-Dragon Scenario. They attack islands via digging tunnels and/or aerial maneuvering. The grand fleet is commanded by Zheng Yi Sao. Other important captains include Anthony Barbarossa (living skeleton with burning beard). All the captains have sealed a contract with demons that grants them various abilities. [Translated from Italian:] Astral-Dragon Scenario: following the song Per Aspera Ad Astra by The One Beyond, it is foreseen that "[...] the strongest of all [the Pisces] will rise to the stars, meeting the Thunder Dragon". The meaning of the quotation translates into the transformation of a TTF Delta-1 agent into a dragon, who will then be able to face St. Namygal Caesar in person [the Thunder Dragon].
