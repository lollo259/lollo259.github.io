## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Abaddon Jehoel is the daughter of Hephaestus [REM-114](#/rem-114) and Anjelique Metatron [REM-660](#/rem-660). She had a violet body colour; her death at Hephaestus' hands is explicit and kept in the source excerpts. Furia Jehoel, "the almost perfect copy of the Holy Abaddon Jehoel", was one of the most feared warriors of the Lexi Empire until captured by Sir Lancillotto [REM-622](#/rem-622).
