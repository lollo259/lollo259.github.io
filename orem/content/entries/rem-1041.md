## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23]
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: no -->
[...] If by any chance they were to be observed beyond reality, their template would look like a black monolith [the monolith/entity has a name, Menhir (let. "tall upright stone")] in the emptiness of light or, better, the impossibility and absurdity of nothingness. This canvas is where the metaphysical world casts its shadow. [the latter sentence literally describes the very act of writing this worldbuilding project.]

Based on this axioms, the following is an ontological proof in 20 steps:

Nothing by definition cannot exist, since it is null.

If the Universe does not exist, nothingness exists. Therefore the Universe exists.

The Universe is, by definition, all that exists, and therefore omnipresent.

If omnipresence does not exist, then the Universe itself cannot exist. Therefore it exists.

If omnipresence exists, and is a fundamental property of a supreme entity, then the other properties of a supreme entity (Menhir) exist.

If omnipresence exists, and is a fundamental property of a supreme entity, then Menhir is all that makes up the Universe, therefore the Universe itself.

If Menhir is the Universe and is omnipotent, any possibility is reality, so such results are defined by functions and exist by necessity.

If such functions exist by necessity, then any event exists by necessity, so it is present.

If Menhir is omniscient, and every event is present, then past and future do not exist and Menhir is eternal. Time is just a form of measurement.

If Menhir is the Universe and is omniscient, then any subject retains that property, varying the degree of consciousness depending on the state of complexity in which it finds itself.

A subject can be aware of itself and, depending on the degree of consciousness, reflect on itself.

If a state reflects on itself, it can generate fundamental questions related to the reason for its existence.

If a subject tries to express itself, it can generate art.

If a subject tries to define itself, it can differentiate itself from others by generating culture.

Every subject of the Universe, and therefore of the supreme entity, is inevitably in eternal juxtaposition and coexistence with other states.

If a subject wants its existence in relation to or at the expense of others, it can generate morality, or other types of codes.

If most subjects vote by a majority, and there is a margin of error, then the ultimate resolution is the consensual process.

If a state tries to understand its existence through logical processes, it can create axioms.

If a state tries to answer fundamental questions, it can create a religion.

If Menhir is the Universe itself, and every state is the Universe itself, then every religion is Mehir trying to explain themselves.

From this ontology it is clear that all religions in this world have something in common, even if they seem completely different. That's the belief of omnism. Actually this way of thinking is mostly agnostic omnism, because the deities mentioned in all religions seem to manifest only before death, in a dream or illusion. The thriving force of desire here is visualized by a distant, behemoth genital of the afro mentioned deity, and the insertion of one's body is the ultimate metamorphosis into a titan (primordial god), or more plausibly the return of the consciousness to the Whole universe. Omnist philosophers crafted a Myth of Creation to conciliante all deities ever conceived, and it is true to the mind of the holder, and therefore to reality (based on point 8 of the ontological proof) because those deities are referred to as dreams and exist in the conceptual state of reality, just another state of emotions. They may not be described as persofinications as the myths say, but they surely exist as mere interdimensional concepts; the legends are just easier to comprehend for the masses. Those dreams are differentiated in three categories: dualism of movement (dreams of kinetic and flow, representing the quantum field or the microcosmos), dualism of manipulation (dreams of elektrokinesis and scorching, representing the chronotopic field or the empirical cosmo), dualism of omnism (dreams of ether and unknown, representing the hypersphere or the macrocosmos).
