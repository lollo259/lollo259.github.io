## Interview: Hana Nanami
<!-- type: interview | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
The Machinist
Interviewed: Hana Nanami, a possible muse of the author
Interviewer: the Author
Foreword: Hana Nanami, the Machinist's personal maid, is considered an intelligent and sharp woman, gifted with sophisticated acting skills.
<Start of record>
Author: Please, identify yourself.
H. Nanami: I am your admiral's favourite symbol [the Machinist's former nickname].
Author: Since when have you been part of the crew?
H. Nanami: I was taken away as a souvenir of His Majesty's voyage to [REDACTED].
Author: What do you mean by "taken away"? Aren't you a voluntary member of the crew?
H. Nanami: What you are trying to instigate does not concern me. His Highness took my dignity and made it his own. He raised me like a trophy, he shaped my person according to his ideal of perfection.
Author: From our archives, it seems you are an extremely cultured woman, with a great passion for art and philosophy. Don't you have any other ambitions, besides being the Machinist's conquest?
H. Nanami: What I couldn't understand when I was young, I now see fully. My life goals and my dedications were only a mere instrument to be reached by the greatest mind. And I found that in my Admiral.
Author: You keep calling him your greatest idol. You seem faithful to him; do you think he is equally faithful to you?
H. Nanami: He is free to behave as he prefers. He is the great conqueror and, as such, he can act following his instinct without thinking about the consequences. His desire is second to none and his orders must be obeyed.
Author: You still seem to be jealous of him.
H. Nanami: Of course I am! In the whole crew, I am the only one who knows him in the depths of his mind.
Author: Could you tell me more?
H. Nanami: I am his chest. He is the key. You are asking me to commit mutiny and treason.
<End of record>
Closing statement: Future questions will be reviewed before the interview. Future interviews must be conducted by personnel with androphilia.
