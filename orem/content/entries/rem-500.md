## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Paradis is a world formed from a piece of the shattered mother: when Adam struck her, one fragment blinded Rangi [REM-501](#/rem-501) in one eye, and he keeps watching everything with the good eye (the God's Eye nebula [REM-1035](#/rem-1035)). One piece became Paradis. The catalogue also describes it as vomited by [REM-103](#/rem-103).
