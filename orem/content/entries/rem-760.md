## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Tramp (character 8) of the philosophical novel TEDIO represents a man "who has lived": wise, but having lost everything by following his own thought.

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Wise man who lost everything. — Quote: "Personaggio 8: il barbone. Rappresenta un uomo “che ha vissuto”, un uomo saggio, ma che ha perso tutto vivendo seguendo il suo pensiero."

Quote (English, machine-assisted translation): "Character 8: the bum. He represents a man “who lived”, a wise man, but who lost everything by following his thought."

## From the source: ROMANZO FILOSOFICO.docx
<!-- type: description | source: ROMANZO FILOSOFICO.docx | translated: yes -->
PROVOCED TYPE FROM A UNIFORMITY PIATTA

(monotonia)

Criticism towards man’s position towards reason: man defines certain things as incomprehensible things, because our minds cannot explain or conceive them. Man has no confidence in himself. Our mind is, on the contrary, complete with everything. We understand anything.
