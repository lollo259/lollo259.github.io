## Description
<!-- type: article -->
"scarlett" (E███ S███████) is a ranked member of the Beyonders' crew [REM-001](#/rem-001). Although she is effectively acting under the Word of Wyncham [REM-043](#/rem-043), the Beyonder [REM-002](#/rem-002) calls her his personal waitress, or butler.

## Interview
<!-- type: article -->
In his 17th interview with the Administrator, the Beyonder was asked about her apathetic behaviour after joining the crew, and the suspicion that she acts against her will. He answered that his behaviour is none of their business and that he cannot control the way he speaks to her.
