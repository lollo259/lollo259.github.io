## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Star Troopers were founded during the titanomachy by a subdomain of the Saints [REM-131](#/rem-131): soldiers trained from infancy. Early members include a mirrored image of [REM-133](#/rem-133), a young George Méliès and Belphegor [REM-136](#/rem-136). One killed a titan alone by tactics; another became known as The Hunter. They built the wooden wall that became the Geraas Wall [REM-120](#/rem-120) around the Pastures in the Sky [REM-242](#/rem-242).
