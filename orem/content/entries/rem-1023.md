Vilkai Klan [[REM-635](#/rem-635)] (let. Clan of Wolves) is a group of individuals capable of shapeshifting into werewolves during a full moon. They also have incredible features such as a great sense of smell, reflexes, and strength. Every member has Vilkas as its surname, meaning “wolf”. Females cannot transform into werewolves but they possess irremovable features such as long nails and canines, wolf ears, and multiple tails; they are more agile than men, but overall they are weaker. The Vilkai Klan [[REM-635](#/rem-635)] is notable for being pacific.

Vilkas, Amoux [[REM-631](#/rem-631)] (let. Eagle Wolf) is a notable warrior. He’s 185 cm tall and weighs 140 kilos. He’s very muscular. He has black hair. He wears a werewolf mantle with its head on the left side. He wields a claymore sword (224 cm - 10 kg), wearing it on its back.

Vilkas, Groyl [[REM-632](#/rem-632)] (let. Horror) is a gruesome warrior. He’s extremely aggressive, showing no remorse. He wears a big belt with the relief of a face as the central golden plate.

-kaidou, Amtsmkbsh (der. Amatsu Mikaboshi) was the first Kaidou. He was exiled by his sister in the land of the devil, where he managed to call for the giants for help. His wife was actually a giant. He took the name of Kaidou (dragon) and he ruled the lands for 70 years. He was known as a fearsome and villainous ruler. As a result, he was brutally killed by his wife, who then left the continent with the giants.

-kaidou, Kemono (let. Road), original name Mirai Hatsujikai (let. Future / Agricoltor) is the strongest Kaidou that has ever lived, and the last one.

-kaidou, Mulher (let. Female) was the only female kaidou in history, considered as strong as Sēnṭaurakaidou himself, if not stronger. She managed to beat the previous kaidou in mere seconds. Licking herself, especially her blood, expanded her adrenaline, making her a deadly opponent. Her fighting style was based on submissions. She chose to retire only after 5 years, making her the shortest reigning kaidou ever. She recieved critique numerous assassination attempts, and she was once raped by a centaur. Lost her courage and focus, she chose to retire against the East Anisodon [[REM-1014](#/rem-1014)] making him win with ease, making a fool of herself and everyone forgetting her. Despite that, she managed to expand her territories over the entire lands, killing numerous centaurs. As Kaidou she used to paint her whole body in white, and in red making a tattoo-like figure on the left side of her body. On the upper body she only wears a shoulder guard on the right side, after having it injured during her fight with the previous kaidou. The guard’s belt covers her nipples. On the lower body she wears a ring belt.

-kaidou, Sēnṭaura (let. Centaur) was one of the 3 strongest kaidous ever. He was known for mastering the use of a weapon called kyoketsu shōge. Even without his weapon he was considered “stronger than a centaur” despite dying by the hands of one of them. His fighting style without a weapon consisted in bringing down the adversary by continuously attacking one particular area; this fighting style became known simply as “the way of the kaidou”. The result was 100% secure, with bringing down the enemy at a cost of dangerous injuries.

Search of Her [[REM-1028](#/rem-1028)]. Groyl Vilkas wants to find the only nymph alive to see her beauty. He kills all the priests in her realm. He finds her in a circular pond in the biggest hall. He gains the upper hand only in the end, and he assaults her.

End of the Vilkai Klan [[REM-635](#/rem-635)]. During the Holy Moon [[REM-1027](#/rem-1027)], they attack the main capital, Lexy.
