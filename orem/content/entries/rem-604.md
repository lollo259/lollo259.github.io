## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Eve as Primordial Mother, with mouths on her breasts. — Quote: "…figli, essendone i genitori in parte immortali e in parte umani, nascono mortali, altri immortali. Eva è dunque la Madre Primordiale. Al seno ha delle bocche, come fosse il simbolo della fertilità."

## From the source: Città
<!-- type: description | source: Città | translated: yes -->
Obviously we don't talk about conventional beings, so Eva is able to procreate while having had a relationship with Adam Eoni ago. Some children, being their parents partly immortal and partly human, are born mortals, other immortals. Eva is therefore the Primordial Mother. Breast has mouths, as if it were the symbol of fertility.
