The mass of those titans was so high to literally break the size, creating inexplicable powers that crushed chances and possibilities. That is why the ancient Tibetans are currently unrivalled in terms of power. ( By the way, those titans were sons of titans even more primordial, called Ideas, creatures I will never explain). Some Monsters were even just imagined, perhaps directly from the Ideas. A monster can be challenged by anyone, whose use is limited only to imagination. If this imagination is weak, the Monster will take control of the body, killing the user or controlling it (that is, because even non-Titanic creatures could overcome a god by branding a Monster). A monster can be melted with anyone, perhaps creating an even stronger monster. A monster can be melted with another monster, creating a Beast. A monster can instill more users at the same time. Many monsters can be marinated, creating a liquid pod with some of the powers of a Behemoth. From this preamble derived many, many different magic systems. Therefore, every species or subspecies of those planets have their own version of magic of that particular enjoy. Every species of the planet transform, via ignition, into "something". That ignition differed in various things, such as: does the body burn inside or outside? Does the environment burn?
