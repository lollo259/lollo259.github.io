## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Golgotha is an agent of the Astral task force Azrael [REM-333](#/rem-333). He unblocks one valve at a time and channels the soul through the mouth into the eyes, the "mirror of the soul". Depending on which valves are open, he can see the structure of anything through other people's souls, see anyone's mind, see into infinity (across universes and beyond the paracosm), or summon the "cemetery of eyes", the Cenotaph or Cemetery of Orbs. The Cemetery is essentially a KO for anyone, though nobody knows what it really does.
