## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Niji Rodhår ("Rainbow") is the East Anisodon. Layla Bloodsuckers [REM-621](#/rem-621) was the first woman to reach this rank. Flagged explicit through linked material.

Bloodsuckers, Layla [[REM-621](#/rem-621)] (let. Born during the night) remembered only as The Red Harlot [[REM-692](#/rem-692)], was the first female warrior to reach the West Anisodon [[REM-1015](#/rem-1015)] rank, and the first to reach the East Anisodon rank. When the previous Kaidou chose to retire she had to fight him, losing in the end. She was then renamed The Red Harlot [[REM-692](#/rem-692)] in a disregarding way to remember her.

Rodhår, Senshi [[REM-1015](#/rem-1015)] (let. Warrior) is the West Anisodon [[REM-1015](#/rem-1015)].

Rodhår, Ulaka [[REM-633](#/rem-633)] (let. Aggressive) is a female Raider. She paints her face with a war paint reminiscent of three scratches.

Shoge Clan [[REM-1019](#/rem-1019)] (inv. Clan of Fields) is a clan based on the periferic territories of the Lexi Empire. They are specialized agricoltours.

Shoge, Kyoketsu [[REM-618](#/rem-618)] (inv. Runner) is the originator of a famous weapon, called kyoketsu-shoge in his honor.

-kaidou, Mulher (let. Female) was the only female kaidou in history, considered as strong as Sēnṭaurakaidou himself, if not stronger. She managed to beat the previous kaidou in mere seconds. Licking herself, especially her blood, expanded her adrenaline, making her a deadly opponent. Her fighting style was based on submissions. She chose to retire only after 5 years, making her the shortest reigning kaidou ever. She recieved critique numerous assassination attempts, and she was once raped by a centaur. Lost her courage and focus, she chose to retire against the East Anisodon making him win with ease, making a fool of herself and everyone forgetting her. Despite that, she managed to expand her territories over the entire lands, killing numerous centaurs. As Kaidou she used to paint her whole body in white, and in red making a tattoo-like figure on the left side of her body. On the upper body she only wears a shoulder guard on the right side, after having it injured during her fight with the previous kaidou. The guard’s belt covers her nipples. On the lower body she wears a ring belt.
