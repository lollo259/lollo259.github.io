## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Mutoryu Oiseaux is the sword school of Phasis. Its techniques, from weakest to strongest, begin with Hanauta Sanchou: Yahazu Giri [REM-158](#/rem-158) and Hanauta Sanchou: Yuki no mai [REM-162](#/rem-162).
