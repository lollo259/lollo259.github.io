## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Captain Barbarossa was a notorious pirate captain who placed his legendary treasure, priceless artifacts and ancient manuscripts, inside BLACKBOXXX [REM-999](#/rem-999) 137 years ago. Two years later, on retrieving the chest, he was consumed by a fire that erupted from the box itself, possibly an effect of its expanding consciousness, and was recorded as "lost to time".
