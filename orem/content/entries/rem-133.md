## Description
<!-- type: article -->
St. Elias Yensid (Star Trooper; mirrored from Walt Disney) is a Wargod: the military god of the concrete environment, the artist's hand. On the Council he holds Environment. He embodies the absurdity of drawing and concertante skill; if he vanished, the Artist's hand would stop working.

## Weapon
<!-- type: article -->
He owns a .44 Remington Magnum [REM-134](#/rem-134) whose bullets have anomalous properties: some contain objects, materials, organisms or concepts. He can shoot a hydrogen bomb, shoot memories away, shoot someone from the future, or shoot the entire domain.

## History
<!-- type: article -->
With Ste. Ishtar [REM-115](#/rem-115) he infiltrated the Church of Light [REM-123](#/rem-123), Orpheus' home. Before that, Disney was among the members of the first command meant to rule the artist's domain in place of Orpheus.
