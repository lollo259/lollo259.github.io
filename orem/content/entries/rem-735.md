## From the source: Djesica Imperii
<!-- type: description | source: Djesica Imperii | translated: no -->
In this planetary system there are four planets. The inner ones include Tuonela [[REM-212](#/rem-212)] (finnish for "underworld"), named after the fact that it's only visible when it is eclipsing the star, and therefore it appears pitch black; Paradis (french for "paradise"), the planet home to different, huge lifeforms thanks to its low gravity; Hathor [[REM-112](#/rem-112)] (egyptian for "goddess of beauty, sensuality and fertility") is the moon of Paradis. There's also an asteroid belt. The outer planets are Ander Reus [[REM-734](#/rem-734)] (afrikaans for "distant other"), a gas giant with accretion disks; Venus Lacrima (latin for "tears of Venus, goddess of love, sex and beauty"), an ice giant with storms and rains of glass.

## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23]
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: no -->
Venus Lacrima (let. "tears of aphrodite") is an ice giant and the fourth planet from Hel, orbiting around its star at an average distance of 26.46 AU. It has a radius of 69,000 kilometers, making it the second largest planet in the system. Its weather is extreme, having winds of 5,400 mph (2km/s, seven times the speed of sound), and sideways raining molten glass. Its clouds are laced with silicate particles, making the planet look like cobalt blue. The planet has a magnitude of -20.79 and it's bright at night, making it visible with the naked eye.
