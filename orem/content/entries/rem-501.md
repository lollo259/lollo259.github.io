## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Rangi and Papa live embraced together for eternity. Seen from the distance they almost seem like a monolith with their profile figure.

Living in the constant darkness brought by the embrace of Rangi and Papa, some of the gods want to see the light. They are Adam, Ra and Jinn.

Someone has to separate them, to see the light. Adam, the strongest amongst the sons, lashes out at Papa, reducing her to billions of billions of billions of pieces. One of them blinds Rangi from an eye, and he keeps watching everything with the good eye (the God's Eye nebula). One piece become Paradis.

Adam (let. First Man) is the primogenit son of Rangi and Papa and the God of Power.

Aura (let. Aurora, Ether) is the thirdgenit daughter of Rangi and Papa and the Goddess of Ether. She died impaled by Poseidon after three days and three nights of agony.

Jinn (inv. Desire) is the fourthgenit of Rangi and Papa. She’s the Goddess of Desire.

Lucifer, Eve (let. Evening) was the first genit daughter of Rangi and Papa. She was killed by Adam in her snake form, pierced by a sword made out of the spinal cord of Ra. She was reborn as a beautiful woman. After The Myth of Creation and The First Wars she slowly lost all her powers, making her as small as a normal human being. She lives in a temple, waiting for the end of the Universe hoping to meet his beloved Adam again.

Metatron, Anjelique (let. Angel) was the fourth genit of Rangi and Papa, a winged titan with no powers. She died crucified by Adam, after he cut her wings.
