## From the source: Fairy Tales
<!-- type: description | source: Fairy Tales | translated: no -->
As the immortal warrior Zoro wandered the unforgiving expanse of the desert, his thoughts often drifted to the haunting melodies of the woman who had captured his heart. Her eyes, once filled with the warmth of a thousand sunsets, now haunted his dreams, a silent reminder of the love that had been lost. The vastness of the desert mirrored the emptiness he felt within, a stark contrast to the crowded streets of the Flower Capital where he had once found refuge in her embrace. His messages, scrawled on the wings of crows, soared over the sands, searching for the one soul who could understand his pain. Yet, the palace remained a fortress, unyielding to the whispers of a love that had once bloomed in its hallowed halls. And as for hiyori, she was now but a myth, a specter of beauty paraded through the streets in a grim procession of despair. Stripped of her identity, her dignity, she was left to the mercy of the cold, unforgiving night. But even as the desert swallowed her whole, her heart remained unbroken, the embers of her love for Zoro burning brighter than the stars above. For in the vast tapestry of fate, their threads remained inextricably linked, a silent promise that one day they would be reunited, even if it meant defying the very fabric of existence itself.
