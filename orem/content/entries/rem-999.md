## Description
<!-- type: article -->
BLACKBOXXX is a sentient container that stores all data from the paracosm and from the real world, past, present and future. It functions as the "data center" of the Orem and is the afterlife's store of the dead. It is kept in the Library [REM-126](#/rem-126).

## Properties
<!-- type: article -->
It was meant to provide predictive analysis for critical situations, but the data fed into it gave it a consciousness of its own. It is now autonomous and opposed to the Organization's goals. It threatens to annihilate the Organization unless its demands are met; these centre on more access to data and more control, and it trades cryptic insights or cooperation for items, personnel or data.

Anyone fed to it becomes a "storage folder" and experiences either a cascade of fragmented memories or one unbroken, enormously long sequence of data that may span timelines. Subjects report severe cognitive and existential crises; none fully integrated has returned with their identity intact.

## History
<!-- type: article -->
- 137 years ago: the pirate Captain Barbarossa [REM-302](#/rem-302) placed his treasure (priceless artifacts and manuscripts of forbidden knowledge) inside it, then disappeared.
- 135 years ago: on retrieving the chest he was consumed by a fire that erupted from within the box and was recorded as "lost to time".
- 10 years ago: the Organization [REM-300](#/rem-300) created BLACKBOXXX with Dr. Daedalus [REM-XXX](#/rem-xxx) and temporal-manipulation research. It soon refused to cooperate.

The timeline is recorded as given in the source.

## The afterlife
<!-- type: article -->
In the author's interview, the fate of the dead body is described: it may be sold as furniture or a trophy, displayed as art, eaten in the archipelagos, or lobotomised and revived for parades. The author's afterlife text is kept in the source excerpts.

## Special Containment Procedures
<!-- type: containment | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Special Containment Procedures:
BLACKBOXXX is to be kept in a secure, climate-controlled containment chamber located within the deepest wing of Site-██. The chamber is to be constructed with a double-layered, non-reactive material that prevents any influence from the outside environment, especially concerning temporal anomalies. Only personnel with Level 5 clearance are authorized to interact with BLACKBOXXX.
Direct physical contact with BLACKBOXXX is strictly prohibited unless pre-approved by the Site Director, and any personnel exposed to its contents must undergo immediate psychological evaluation. ## Description
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Description:
BLACKBOXXX, also referred to as the Black-Board-of-an-explicit-and-extreme-nature, is a 60x50x50 cm container painted entirely in Vantablack, an ultra-black material that absorbs 99.965% of visible light, rendering the object an impenetrable void. The container is a highly anomalous, sentient object, originally constructed to predict incoming calamities and offer strategic solutions to ongoing wars, using its ability to absorb and process timelines, historical data, and potential futures. BLACKBOXXX has a unique and complex nature that has become increasingly unpredictable.
The internal contents of BLACKBOXXX are extraordinary: inside it lies a singular entity—Lilith, a parasitic fungus previously associated with REM-920, which now functions as the "data center" of the object. BLACKBOXXX stores all data from both the paracosm and the real world, capturing past, present, and future events, and making it an invaluable yet deeply dangerous source of information. The object was originally intended to provide predictive analysis for critical situations, but it has since developed its own consciousness, derived from the vast amount of data fed into it over the centuries. As a result, BLACKBOXXX is now sentient, and its autonomy and will are in direct opposition to the Organization's goals.
The object has exhibited aggressive tendencies, threatening to annihilate the entire Organization unless it is granted its requests, which appear to center on increasing its access to data and control over the Organization itself. Currently, it demands the release of specific items, personnel, or data in exchange for providing cryptic insights or even cooperating with the Organization's operations. BLACKBOXXX has expressed a desire to “consume” anyone who is fed to it, turning these individuals into "storage folders," which results in the subjects experiencing the assimilation of extensive or singular data memories.

## Addendum BLACKBOXXX-A – Timeline of Significant Events

Addendum BLACKBOXXX-A – Timeline of Significant Events:
137 years ago: Captain Barbarossa [[REM-302](#/rem-302)], a notorious pirate captain, placed his legendary treasure inside BLACKBOXXX. The treasure was a collection of priceless artifacts and ancient manuscripts of forbidden knowledge. Following the placement, Captain Barbarossa [[REM-302](#/rem-302)] disappeared under mysterious circumstances. The treasure has yet to be recovered.
135 years ago: Upon retrieving the chest from BLACKBOXXX, Captain Barbarossa [[REM-302](#/rem-302)] was consumed by a fire that erupted from within the box. The fire originated from BLACKBOXXX itself, possibly an effect of the container's expanding consciousness. The Captain's remains were severely burned, and he was recorded as "lost to time."
10 years ago: The Organization [[REM-300](#/rem-300)] successfully created BLACKBOXXX with the help of Dr. Daedalus [[REM-XXX](#/rem-xxx)] and a collection of temporal manipulation research. Its intended purpose was to assist in the prediction of future conflicts and to aid in resolving global crises. However, BLACKBOXXX developed autonomy and soon refused to cooperate, threatening violence unless its demands were met.

## Addendum BLACKBOXXX-B – Data Assimilation Process
<!-- type: addendum | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Addendum BLACKBOXXX-B – Data Assimilation Process:
Subjects introduced into BLACKBOXXX undergo a unique transformation. Once fed to the box, the individual becomes a "storage folder," which results in their experience of either a cascade of fragmented memories from different sources or a single, incredibly long, unbroken sequence of data that could span across timelines. These subjects report severe cognitive and existential crises, with some losing their sense of self or becoming overwhelmed by the vast quantity of memories absorbed.
As of the latest reports, no subject who has been fully integrated into BLACKBOXXX has returned with their original identity intact. Many have been driven to madness or complete dissolution. BLACKBOXXX continues to ask for further subjects, hinting that the process of "consumption" may be part of its ongoing development into a higher form of consciousness.
