## Interview: Agent Blue Pheasant
<!-- type: interview | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: no -->
Foreword: Agent Blue Pheasant of TTF Phi-1 asked for an interview.
<Begin log>
Blue Pheasant: Stop asking questions.
Me: Please identify yourself.
Blue Pheasant: Uhm… I'm [DATA EXPUNGED]. Oh wait, forget that. Note: "Blue Pheasant" told me his real name.
Me: And you are a member of TTF Phi-1, The Beyonders, correct?
Blue Pheasant: I've been here for the past few months, and haven't left yet, so yes. I am a Beyonder.
Me: In what circumstances have you joined the crew?
Blue Pheasant: Ahh, I don't really remember… I was drunk…
Me: At least can you tell us why you've joined the crew?
Blue Pheasant: Well, uh… we were all trying to find a place to be free, to reach our dreams…
Me: What is your dream?
Blue Pheasant: Something important. Equality.. Justice… Freedom from those stupid gods…
Me: Interesting how you pronounce those words while being under the rule of a dictator like The Machinist…
Blue Pheasant: We just have common goals. I do not act like him.
Me: So you think your captain wants the same things as you do, yet he does the complete opposite of what he preaches?
Blue Pheasant: You don't see the reason behind all his actions, do you? Have you ever wondered why all of us accept his ways? Because we know that if he departs, there will no longer be any hope.
Me: What about the people he enslaved? You think what happened to them is in the name of justice?
<End log>
