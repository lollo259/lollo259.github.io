## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23]
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: no -->
Tuonela (let. "underworld") is a rocky planet and the least distant planet in the system, orbiting its star at an average distance of 0.22 AU. The planet has a magnitude of -31.19, it is visible with the naked eye only when it transits in front of Hel; therefore it looks black to the anthropomorph eye, and it's associated with the underworld. The planet is the only one in the planetary system without an atmosphere.

The Hel system [[REM-278](#/rem-278)] has a diameter of approximately 84 AU, with the Leti debris [[REM-280](#/rem-280)] (let. "detritus of the edge", its debris disk) located between 34.67 AU and 42 AU. At these two points are located respectively the biggest dwarf planet of the system, Doppelgangger (let. "double, twin", referring to Tuonela) and the smallest one, Phantasus [[REM-281](#/rem-281)] (let. "fantasy"; its existence is only speculated but it has never been seen, thus the name).
