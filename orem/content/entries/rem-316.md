## Description
<!-- type: article -->
TTF Tau-1 ("Pekka") is formed by all agents together with TTF Alpha-1 [REM-310](#/rem-310), Alpha-2, Psi-1 and Omega-1, and is deployed only in apocalyptic scenarios. Pekka units appear in the Stalker incidents (see [REM-467](#/rem-467)).

## Mentions in other documents: THE OREM
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Addendum REM-467-A – The Pekka Task Force:

A group of Stalkers is referred to as a "Pekka." The Pekka represents a specialized task force designed to combat and neutralize a much larger, far more powerful threat: the celestial meridian. These celestial meridians are enormous, god-like entities that exist within various paracosms, representing complex forces of nature and the cosmos. The Pekka, with its relentless focus and overwhelming persistence, is used as a counterforce in situations where traditional measures have failed.

The Pekka is often deployed in high-risk scenarios and can be combined with other monster entities or weapons designed to neutralize celestial threats. The Stalkers that make up the Pekka often work in coordinated efforts, adapting to the celestial meridian's tactics and countering them with their specialized programming. While their numbers may seem small, the effectiveness of their combined pursuit can overwhelm even the most formidable cosmic entities.

Incident 467-07: During an operation involving a celestial meridian in the Goddess of the Spiral [[REM-303](#/rem-303)]'s domain, a Pekka unit was deployed. After 72 hours of continuous pursuit, the Pekka successfully neutralized the celestial threat, but at a significant cost. Two Stalkers were destroyed during the mission, and several Monsters were incapacitated. However, the mission was considered a success due to the overall neutralization of the target.
