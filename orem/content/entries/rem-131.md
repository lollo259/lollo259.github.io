## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Saints are the first creatures, deities created in the image of Orpheus [REM-000](#/rem-000) and so each representing a part of the artist. The later generations split into the Joutsen [REM-130](#/rem-130), new and weaker titans descended from Adam, and the Saints, the gods. Each Saint had its own magic system; lower-ranked ones could travel to the reality dimension without losing their humanoid form. They established the Table of Wargods [REM-143](#/rem-143).
