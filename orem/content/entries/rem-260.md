## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Two titans are born; they are two different twins. One is a male, Adam, titan, God of Power (kinetic energy, strength, speed); the other is a female, Lucifer, snake titan, the Goddess of the Unknown.

Lucifer wants to kill Adam, but he's too strong. She decides to poison him, beginning a plan. She plants part of herself in the soil. A tree is born, waiting for its fruit to be eaten by Adam.

In a legendary battle, Adam pierces through Lucifer with the Sword of Ra, creating a breach in the flow of things.

Lucifer mutates in a female body that comes out of her mouth. Just like a caterpillar when it becomes a butterfly, the new body does not remember anything from past life, and has only one, prescribed objective: make Adam eat the fruit. As a result, she's not evil nor good, and she changes with experience.

Lucifer, Eve (let. Evening) was the first genit daughter of Rangi and Papa. She was killed by Adam in her snake form, pierced by a sword made out of the spinal cord of Ra. She was reborn as a beautiful woman. After The Myth of Creation and The First Wars she slowly lost all her powers, making her as small as a normal human being. She lives in a temple, waiting for the end of the Universe hoping to meet his beloved Adam again.
