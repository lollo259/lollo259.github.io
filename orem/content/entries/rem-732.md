## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Hel Nebulae is the giant molecular cloud whose partial collapse formed the star Hel 3.5 billion years ago; the planet Paradis [REM-500](#/rem-500) formed about 100 million years later.

## From the source: Djesica Imperii
<!-- type: description | source: Djesica Imperii | translated: no -->
On the macro level this setting is described inside a small spiral galaxy (29,000 light years) named Maelstrom A [[REM-731](#/rem-731)] (parody of "Sagittarius A"). In a region known as the galactic habitable zone, 14,500 light years from the black hole at the center of the galaxy, and 11 light years in length, there's the hellish neighborhood consisting of 31 stars, amongst which the bigger one, Hel, (swedish for "whole") is the star at the center of the hellish system, and by far the brightest object and most important source of energy for life in the hellish system. It is an F-type main sequence star (F3.5V), with a radius of 807,707.7 kilometers. Hel formed 3.5 billion years ago from the collapse of part of a giant molecular cloud named Hel Nebulae. The planet Paradis formed approximately 100 million years thereafter. Hel will probably go supernova in 1,052 billion years, and become a white dwarf inside a planetary nebula.
