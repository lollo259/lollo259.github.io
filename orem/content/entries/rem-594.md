## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Male elves trained to defend Eden's borders. — Quote: "la corporatura degli esemplari maschi li rende poco agili negli stretti spazi dell’Eden, ma particolarmente portati a battaglie in campo aperto. Quote (English, machine-assisted translation): "the body of the male specimens makes them little agile in the narrow spaces of the Eden, but particularly brought to open field battles. Therefore they are ideal to defend Eden at the borders, where they are trained to become the Walls of Paradise"

## Mentions in other documents: LEGGI DEL PARADISO
<!-- type: description | source: LEGGI DEL PARADISO | translated: yes -->
Every 50 cadets there must be a Paradise Defender as Subcomandante and Judge. [For Walls of Paradise see Armed Section, Law 14]

If the cadet, finished physical training, ranks in the first 300, joins the Walls of Paradise. [see Armate Section, Law] If you rank in the next 9 becomes Subcomandante. [see Armate Section, Law] If you rank in the next places you receive the same treatment as those given to the Mariannes.

The Walls of Paradise are the first 300 warriors of Defense. Their name means defence (Mura) of Eden (Paradise), and for this reason they are placed immediately after the river that divides the Eden from the Lexi Empire. They need different specific trainings because they struggle in the territory to the most unknown Eden, the simple land.
