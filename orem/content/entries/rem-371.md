## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Farm is a series of structures in the Armageddon asteroid ring [REM-248](#/rem-248), controlled by the Astral Task Force [REM-307](#/rem-307), intended for experimentation and the creation of new things, in particular the re-education and training of new recruits (the dead). The deities named by the letters of "Angel", whose powers the A.N.G.E.L. Suit [REM-420](#/rem-420) harnesses, are locked there.
