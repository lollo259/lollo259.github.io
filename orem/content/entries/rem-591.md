They are chosen for courage and strategic-combat skills; the simplest way to evaluate them is the following mission: they have 1 day to kill and bring at least 1 [REM-767](#/rem-767), leaving the brain with a maximum of two cuts (they are not evaluated if they carry only nerves or a brain completely spread).
