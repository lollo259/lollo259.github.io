## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Chief (King) of Beasts is the male King of the Lands [REM-560](#/rem-560). To prove himself strongest he must kill an elephant seal bare-handed in the King's Trial [REM-675](#/rem-675). He strangled La Tagliagole [REM-523](#/rem-523) one-handed after disarming her in two moves, and was badly injured in the Battle of the Four Kingdoms [REM-664](#/rem-664), trampled by a herd of horses.
