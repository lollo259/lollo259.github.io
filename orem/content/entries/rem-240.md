## From the source: Paracosmia
<!-- type: description | source: Paracosmia | translated: no -->
Saints go on war with Titans, in what's usually called Angelic Wars, or Titanomakia.

The Star Troopers are created. A group of soldiers trained from their infant age.

Some of the first troopers are: Jean-Marie Maelstrom, with his interesting inventions. An even younger Elias Yensid and his spy abilities. Belphegor Morningstar and his insatiable vore for violence in war. Friona and her battle with a thousand titans. They were the power couple. Merlin and his infallible tactics, diplomat when necessary, perfect warrior when required.

Some other Star Troopers are also great. One manages to kill a titan by tactics, by himself. Another becomes known as The Hunter.

Star Trooper The White Death is a dangerous one. Time moves when he moves, but if he remains still nothing moves. So he can slowly move. He's a sniper. He's almost never active, because of his powers. So from the outside, if you see him move, he's basically moving at the speed of light, and killing you.

Star trooper Mobomimbisi is one of the worst soldiers, in the sense that he's vicious and lethal.

Star Troopers start building a wooden-like wall, the future Geraas Wall, around their territory (extremely small), called Pascoli nel cielo (although it's infinite)

St. (Star Trooper) Elias Yensid and Ste. (Star Trooper Emeralda) Ishtar Morningstar infiltrates the drifting Church of Light, where Orpheus resides.

Saints establish their power by sheer numbers, and they even corrupt Orpheus’ body with Ste. Ishtar Morningstar

Saints establish a Table of Wargods. They are Belphegor, Friona, Maelstrom, Yensid and Mobomimbisi. Although Mobomimbisi leaves almost immediately, for unknown reasons, and retires as The Fisherman. Merlin takes his place.
