## From the source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
♪ Imagine a distant ring, a halo. An endless library. Librarii Ofel Isandaria. The artist draws from it, sources obtained during the course of his life, to popular his own garden. Every plant, every rock, every being that lives and does not live. In this library there is a huge encyclopedia, an almanac of artifacts, of forgotten creations, of dimensional translations between titans and deities. The Beast. These artefacts, called Monsters, were part of the ancient titans, the sons of the Ideas (prirdial titans), as organs of the same. A titan falling in size is not measurable by the dimension itself, so its translation becomes an object with unknown, vast and unlimited powers, until it collapses into objects. The mass of those titans was so high to literally break the size, creating inexplicable powers that crushed chances and possibilities. That is why the ancient Tibetans are currently unrivalled in terms of power. ( By the way, those titans were sons of titans even more primordial, called Ideas, creatures I will never explain). The lamp studies and selects these objects, reforming them in Monsters. Some of them remained in their rudimentary form, while others were rebuilt or modified. Some Monsters were even just imagined, perhaps directly from the Ideas. A monster can be challenged by anyone, whose use is limited only to imagination. If this imagination is weak, the Monster will take control of the body, killing the user or controlling it (that is, because even non-Titanic creatures could overcome a god by branding a Monster). A monster can be melted with anyone, perhaps creating an even stronger monster. A monster can be melted with another monster, creating a Beast. A monster can instill more users at the same time. A monster can be melted with many other monsters, creating or recreating a Behemoth (although short-lived). Many monsters can be marinated, creating a liquid pod with some of the powers of a Behemoth. From this preamble derived many, many different magic systems. Since every Monster has its own characteristics and rules, each Monster is effectively its own magic system. And since the ancient titans' future generations basically splitted in two, the Joutsen (the new titans, weaker but still titans, descendants of Adam) and the Saints (the gods). Each Saint had its unique magic system, with the lower ranked being able to travel to the reality dimension without losing their humanoid appearance, the middle ranked (Celestial Meridian, Four Horsemen) being half transformed, and the Council of Wargods isolated into full Behemoths while in the dimension lower. Joutsen were able to live in the planet Tuonela (formerly Valhalla) in the reality dimension, because they were essentially empty containers for magic. Convergent evolution created a general magic system for all titans to follow, and each one of them specified in something, making it more unique and differentated. And that is basically what happened to each planet with life. Every planet is a enjoy in its monstrous form. Therefore, every species or subspecies of those planets have their own version of magic of that particular enjoy. There are 5000+ enjoys, and I've explored 3 planetary magic systems and tons of other general magic systems. Here are a few of them (just the general idea): On Paradise - Fiamma (Ignition Magic). Every species of the planet transform, via ignition, into "something". That ignition differed in various things, such as: does the body burn inside or outside? Does the environment burn? Does the soul burn? Does it burn in negative? What do they become? Etc. on Doppelgänger - White Mass (Missa of Words and Life). Magic power accumulate the longer and harder the fight gets. Interesting because normal martial artists can beat magicians in the first stage of their encounters, while magicians are more prone to win in theatter internships. Also, words are magical. on Phantasus - Umbra (Shadow Magic). That's exactly what it looks like. on Tuonela - Color of Clockworks (Gear Magic). Every titan controls a set of conceptual gears, creating a unique ability. Charon can distort and observe objects at extreme distances and connect to the same orbit those objects; Pendulum can switch gears to gain more or less power; Kamehameha can travel across one gear, or "orbit", and cant be stopped unless the centre of that orbit is disrupted; Ouroboros creates those gear constructs; Iapetus creates sniper gears from his eyes. on the entire universe (because the universe is the demiurge titan's mind) - Gates (Soul Magic) and many, many more.
