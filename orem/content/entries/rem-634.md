## Mentions in other documents: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
-kaidou, Sēnṭaura (let. Centaur) was one of the 3 strongest kaidous ever. He was known for mastering the use of a weapon called kyoketsu shōge. Even without his weapon he was considered “stronger than a centaur” despite dying by the hands of one of them. His fighting style without a weapon consisted in bringing down the adversary by continuously attacking one particular area; this fighting style became known simply as “the way of the kaidou”. The result was 100% secure, with bringing down the enemy at a cost of dangerous injuries.
