## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Kyoketsu Shoge ("Runner"), number 2 in the Bounties of Paradis (career bounty 9,000,000,000), from Öý [REM-1011](#/rem-1011), is the maker and first user of the infamous weapon named after him, the kyoketsu shoge [REM-673](#/rem-673), the single most dangerous weapon ever created. He was never found.
