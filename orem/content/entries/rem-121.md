## From the source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
[REM-000](#/rem-000)' language is not ordinary language. His speech *is* I sing in itself — not in the sense that "talking it" (as an air of work), but in the most radical sense: every word is already melody, every phrase is already musical composition. There is no separation between semantic meaning and sound form. Say something is singing it in existence. This song is transcribed in a written form called Assamic Scripture. For each vocal chord — that is, for each particular vibration of the voice — there are curves and lines representing the tonal height. Writing is not alphabetic (conventional symbols representing sounds), but *notational* (graphic tracks that map the frequencies directly). This writing is, literally, the display of the tears of [REM-000](#/rem-000) (as we will see later). It's frozen music. It is made visible sound. It is the song transformed into geometry. Every time [REM-000](#/rem-000) sings, he cries. Tears arise from your eyes — not as an emotional response ([REM-000](#/rem-000) does not feel emotions), but as an automatic, somatic phenomenon. The song * produces tears, physiologically, inevitably. This mechanical cry is the key to Assamic Scripture: it is written from above down, as frozen tears over time. Each curve, each line of notation is not arbitrary but traces the fall of a particular tear. Writing is the crystallization of the pain of the demiurgo — a pain that is not felt but that however manifests, is inscribed, is transmitted. This image recalls [REM-112](#/rem-112)/[REM-110](#/rem-110) who creates humanity crying (in real mythology). The divine cry is not sterile, but generative. Tears do not fall into the void, but create, form, give origin. The pain of the god becomes substance of the world. What about tears? The tears that flow every time you sing? I am the trace of pain that cannot be felt but that still exists — the pain of split, the pain of creation, the pain of being demiurgo and not being able to stop being. Tears fall, freeze, become writing. And that writing — Assamic Scripture — is what remains when the song becomes visible, when the pain becomes readable, when creation becomes transmissible to others. We, readers, read the frozen tears of the demiurgo.
