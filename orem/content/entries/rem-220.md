## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The monkey-queen form is what remains of Falari [REM-108](#/rem-108) after a ten-hour flaying: something divine reduced to a grotesque simulacrum of the human, resembling Hanuman or Sun Wukong. The account is explicit and kept in the source excerpts.

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
What remains of flayed [REM-108](#/rem-108) resembles a monkey queen. — Quote: "Poi la scuoiatura. Dieci ore per rimuovere tutta la pelle, lasciando intatti solo mani, piedi, viso, collo, seni e inguine. Ciò che rimane somiglia a Hanuman, a Sun Wukong — una regina scimmia. Cioè: qualcosa che era divino ma è stato ridotto a simulacro grottesco dell'umano."

Quote (English, machine-assisted translation): "Then shaking. Ten hours to remove all skin, leaving only hands, feet, face, neck, breasts and inguine intact. What remains resembles Hanuman, at Sun Wukong — a monkey queen. That is: something that was divine but was reduced to grotesque simulacrum of the human."
