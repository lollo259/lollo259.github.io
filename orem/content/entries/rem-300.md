## Technical observations of the Orem (preface)
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
An ant is nothing compared to a praying mantis. But the same insect, if pitted against an entire anthill, would be completely annihilated. This was the main concept behind the theorem (the Orem), an organization of the lower creatures created to unite all creatures and defeat the gods. But there is still a big problem: if all creatures form an anthill, a single god is more like a diplodocus. But the Orem is not a simple anthill: its units attack the body of the gods like a virus. When the Created is at war with its own Creator, the mind collapses. The domain is no longer dominated by the Artist. The Orem studies the structure of the divine, of the pastures in the sky. It codifies its parts, in order to bring it to collapse. The council of the gods, the Wardogs (as the Machinist calls them, but as Ion Henri also called them), is composed of five fragments of the artist's childhood.
