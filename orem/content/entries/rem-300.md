## Technical observations of the Orem (preface)
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
An ant is nothing compared to a praying mantis. But the same insect, if pitted against an entire anthill, would be completely annihilated. This was the main concept behind the theorem (the Orem), an organization of the lower creatures created to unite all creatures and defeat the gods. But there is still a big problem: if all creatures form an anthill, a single god is more like a diplodocus. But the Orem is not a simple anthill: its units attack the body of the gods like a virus. When the Created is at war with its own Creator, the mind collapses. The domain is no longer dominated by the Artist. The Orem studies the structure of the divine, of the pastures in the sky. It codifies its parts, in order to bring it to collapse. The council of the gods, the Wardogs (as the Machinist calls them, but as [REM-217](#/rem-217) also called them), is composed of five fragments of the artist's childhood. Below I report some technical observations of the Orem.

## Mentions in other documents: THE OREM
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
The internal contents of BLACKBOXXX [[REM-999](#/rem-999)] are extraordinary: inside it lies a singular entity—Lilith, a parasitic fungus previously associated with REM-920, which now functions as the "data center" of the object. BLACKBOXXX [[REM-999](#/rem-999)] stores all data from both the paracosm and the real world, capturing past, present, and future events, and making it an invaluable yet deeply dangerous source of information. The object was originally intended to provide predictive analysis for critical situations, but it has since developed its own consciousness, derived from the vast amount of data fed into it over the centuries. As a result, BLACKBOXXX [[REM-999](#/rem-999)] is now sentient, and its autonomy and will are in direct opposition to the Organization's goals.

The object has exhibited aggressive tendencies, threatening to annihilate the entire Organization unless it is granted its requests, which appear to center on increasing its access to data and control over the Organization itself. Currently, it demands the release of specific items, personnel, or data in exchange for providing cryptic insights or even cooperating with the Organization's operations. BLACKBOXXX [[REM-999](#/rem-999)] has expressed a desire to “consume” anyone who is fed to it, turning these individuals into "storage folders," which results in the subjects experiencing the assimilation of extensive or singular data memories.

10 years ago: The Organization successfully created BLACKBOXXX [[REM-999](#/rem-999)] with the help of Dr. Daedalus [[REM-XXX](#/rem-xxx)] and a collection of temporal manipulation research. Its intended purpose was to assist in the prediction of future conflicts and to aid in resolving global crises. However, BLACKBOXXX [[REM-999](#/rem-999)] developed autonomy and soon refused to cooperate, threatening violence unless its demands were met.

Although it does not engage in typical human communication, the Stalker is capable of relaying its mission status through encoded signals that can be intercepted by the Organization’s advanced linguistics systems. These communications primarily serve as status reports on its pursuit of the target, and occasionally, diagnostic updates on its internal systems.

In an investigation initiated by the Organization, several personnel from nearby facilities were found to have been influenced by the teachings of Lily Springs [[REM-911](#/rem-911)]. The cult’s psychological influence was immediate, with many individuals showing signs of complete devotion and exhibiting behaviors linked to the religious practices of the group. In one case, an individual attempted to recreate the rituals of the cult, nearly leading to a memetic cascade that could have resulted in the spread of the cult’s ideology to other areas.
