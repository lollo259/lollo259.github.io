## Mentions in other documents: LEGGI DEL PARADISO
<!-- type: description | source: LEGGI DEL PARADISO | translated: yes -->
The Elector of Embioptera is the first profession by order of difficulty. It consists of three tasks, to be carried out at rotations of 1 week each.

Breeding: is to provide food and protection to the Embioptera.

Extraction: is to extract the silicone produced by Embioptera.

Transport: is to transport the Embioptera to the tribe.

[for Embioptera see Section Flora and fauna, paragraph 1]

The manufacturer is the second profession by order of difficulty. It consists in building the tribe's dwellings through the silicone of the Embioptera.
