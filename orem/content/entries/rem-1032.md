## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Demiurge, Jelena [[REM-697](#/rem-697)] (let. Torch) is an Airmen. Nicknamed “The Monster” because of her strange factors, she lives with an apparent illness, that is actually the power of her clan, that makes her cells multiply continuously in chaos changing her genes. The result is the formation of a phallus and a clone. She’s the only reported case of a human with red hair other than the clans Bloodsuckers and Rodhår. Her destiny is to bring a new light to the world, hence the name. She lives almost in poverty in an old farm in the territories of Öý.
