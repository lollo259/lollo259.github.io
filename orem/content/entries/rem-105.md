## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Genies are the remaining genies of the Lamp. The Lamp says: "I'm the lamp that lost the light. I have only a few people left in my soul."

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
The Lamp's remaining genies. — Quote: "### The Genies"

## From the source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
Forgive me, my children, for I have condemned you all. Forgive me, because I became empty. I'm the lamp that lost the light. I have only a few people left in my soul.
