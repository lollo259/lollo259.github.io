## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Sirens are storms as big as mountains or tsunamis that still carry the voices of past victims as echoes in the wind. The Compendium lists them with the Hordes, the Witch, Wrath and Venus Lacrima [REM-735](#/rem-735) as the storms.
