## Mentions in other documents: Djesica Imperii
<!-- type: description | source: Djesica Imperii | translated: no -->
On the macro level this setting is described inside a small spiral galaxy (29,000 light years) named Maelstrom A [[REM-731](#/rem-731)] (parody of "Sagittarius A"). In a region known as the galactic habitable zone, 14,500 light years from the black hole at the center of the galaxy, and 11 light years in length, there's the hellish neighborhood consisting of 31 stars, amongst which the bigger one, Hel, (swedish for "whole") is the star at the center of the hellish system, and by far the brightest object and most important source of energy for life in the hellish system. It is an F-type main sequence star (F3.5V), with a radius of 807,707.7 kilometers. Hel formed 3.5 billion years ago from the collapse of part of a giant molecular cloud named Hel Nebulae [[REM-732](#/rem-732)]. The planet Paradis formed approximately 100 million years thereafter. Hel will probably go supernova in 1,052 billion years, and become a white dwarf inside a planetary nebula.

In this planetary system there are four planets. The inner ones include Tuonela [[REM-212](#/rem-212)] (finnish for "underworld"), named after the fact that it's only visible when it is eclipsing the star, and therefore it appears pitch black; Paradis (french for "paradise"), the planet home to different, huge lifeforms thanks to its low gravity; Hathor [[REM-112](#/rem-112)] (egyptian for "goddess of beauty, sensuality and fertility") is the moon of Paradis. There's also an asteroid belt. The outer planets are Ander Reus [[REM-734](#/rem-734)] (afrikaans for "distant other"), a gas giant with accretion disks; Venus Lacrima [[REM-735](#/rem-735)] (latin for "tears of Venus, goddess of love, sex and beauty"), an ice giant with storms and rains of glass.

The Paradis i Hathor system (let. "paradise and goddess of fertility") is a two-body system where a rocky planet, Paradis, is orbited by its moon, Hathor [[REM-112](#/rem-112)]. Paradis is the second planet from Hel, orbiting around its star at an average distance of 1.86 AU, making it habitable. It formed approximately 3.4 billion years ago. It has a radius of 0.747 REarth. The planet has a mass of 0.38 MEarth, of which 35% is made by its iron core. With an axial tilt of 29%, its tropics are located at 0 - 29 °N/C, and the polar circles at 61 - 90 °N/C. It has an average surface temperature of 21° despite 90% of its surface being covered in clouds (just 17% of thick clouds).

Tuonela [[REM-212](#/rem-212)] (let. "underworld") is a rocky planet and the least distant planet in the system, orbiting its star at an average distance of 0.22 AU. The planet has a magnitude of -31.19, it is visible with the naked eye only when it transits in front of Hel; therefore it looks black to the anthropomorph eye, and it's associated with the underworld. The planet is the only one in the planetary system without an atmosphere.

The Hel system [[REM-278](#/rem-278)] has a diameter of approximately 84 AU, with the Leti debris [[REM-280](#/rem-280)] (let. "detritus of the edge", its debris disk) located between 34.67 AU and 42 AU. At these two points are located respectively the biggest dwarf planet of the system, Doppelgangger (let. "double, twin", referring to Tuonela [[REM-212](#/rem-212)]) and the smallest one, Phantasus [[REM-281](#/rem-281)] (let. "fantasy"; its existence is only speculated but it has never been seen, thus the name).

Nimfs [[REM-1047](#/rem-1047)] (short for "God de Nimf [[REM-1047](#/rem-1047)]", let. "nymphs of God"),  the non-cetacean primates, sapient inhabitants of Paradis, are characterized by their bipedalism and high intelligence. They are omnivorous animals with high metabolism. Their anatomy depends on their gender, which can either be intersex (breasts and penis), female (breasts and vagina) or rare male (penis). Nimfs [[REM-1047](#/rem-1047)] are characterized by having dense musculature, slend skeleton (making them both graceful and strong at the same time), protruding ears that allow for better hearing, and sharp eyesight with color cells in their iris that change based on their emotions (a feature common in mammals on this planet). They have a tail for better balance on trees. The tail is located, following the sacrum, in between the glutes, and the buttocks are partially pushed to either side. Therefore they are great climbers, but have difficulties to sit. All members of this species feed only from liquids and chewing. All female nimfs give birth to their offspring by laying eggs as large as their pelvic bone. After hatching, their babies grow at fast speeds and become fertile. Their bodies mature after 2-3 years. Nimfs [[REM-1047](#/rem-1047)] train in agility and strength without the usage of any weapon, and are extremely stealthy and dangerous in forest biomes. After 15 years of training they can be classified as "screaming howlers", an oxymoron to describe their abilities. Living in tribal autarchies, where the rulers are the strongest, the most confident warriors go on a mission to kill a great ape and bring back a trophy, a symbol of their victory.

All of Paradis' animals evolved a bilateral symmetrical body. Thanks to the planet's low gravity, some carnivorous animals reached huge dimensions. Subsequently, their prey evolved in two different ways, either tanking the predator with size and defensive attributes, or presenting an elongated body to snuck underground.

The planet has a mass of 0.38 MEarth, of which 35% is made by its iron core. With an axial tilt of 29%, its tropics are located at 0-29 °N/C, and the polar circles at 61-90 °N/C. It has an average surface temperature of 21° despite 90% of its surface being covered in clouds (just 17% of thick clouds). For an average human observer, the distance to the horizon would be at 4.115 kilometers. Thanks to its low gravity (0.682 g) and high levels of oxygen in the atmosphere (35%) its fauna is full of giant organisms, extremely strong, or organisms adapted to hide (elongated animals that sneak in rocks or in the underground). All mammals lay eggs, and there are two sapient species.

Tuonela [[REM-212](#/rem-212)]:  [underworld] is a rocky planet and the least distant planet in the system, orbiting its star at an average distance of 0.22 AU. The planet has a magnitude of -31.19, it is visible with the naked eye only when it transits in front of Hel; therefore it looks black to the eye, and it's associated with the underworld. The planet is the only one in the planetary system without an atmosphere.

Venus Lacrima [[REM-735](#/rem-735)]:  [tears of Aphrodite [[REM-110](#/rem-110)]] is  an ice giant and the fourth planet from Hel, orbiting around its star at an average distance of 26.46 AU. It has a radius of 69,000 kilometers, making it the second largest planet in the system. Its weather is extreme, having winds of 5,400 mph (2km/s, seven times the speed of sound), and sideways raining molten glass. Its clouds are laced with silicate particles, making the planet look like cobalt blue. The planet has a magnitude of -20.79 and it's bright at night, making it visible with the naked eye.
