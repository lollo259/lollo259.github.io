## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Felines are the most numerous creatures of Eden: part of the spell to resurrect the elves worked, while the other part moved onto felines, making them human-like. Listed among the wild animals with mariannes and centauresses. Explicit details are kept in the source excerpts.
