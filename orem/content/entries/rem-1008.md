## Mentions in other documents: Djesica Imperii

The Garden of Eden is a great mangrove forest over the swamps of the Great River, basically an entire ecosystem inside the city. It was the native land. Many dangerous predators live there, therefore the human residents were modified to live there. They have pointy, protruding ears for 360° hearing, and some have cocks and testicles to reproduce.

The Kraken is just a giant squid the size of a colossal squid, named Titanic squid. It mistakes ships for predators like sperm whales or levyatans, therefore it always attacks them. It's impossible to reach the city via sea.

The individual is to be considered a member of the city-state if they are resident in the nation, regardless of their birthplace, and they are at least 20 years old.

The city is structured like babylonian hanging gardens, but it's all on the same level. The center of the city is the Sakura Palace (similar to the Grosse Halle), and inside, in front of the entrance, there's a colossal statue of the egyptian goddess Hathor naked, and a gigantic batthouse.
