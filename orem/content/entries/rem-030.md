<!-- type: log | source: notes on beyonders | translated: no -->
**Immediately before incident [DATA ESPURGED], SCP-001-1a requested a meeting with two angels of Universe 6 and Universe 11 to decide the fate of the [DragonBall] universe. The meeting has been recorded, although only the following transcription has been derived.**
-----
**<Begin log>**
**SCP-001:** I am the admiral of ev'ry reality yond exists.  Beholdeth mine own self.
**Vados:** We bow before your majesty, great leader. We wonder what the reason for your visit could be.
**SCP-001:** As thee shouldst already knoweth, thy univ'rse hast many pow'rful beings [the █████]. T hast been obs'rv'd yond those beings groweth stout'r the m're those gents square.  Although highly improbable, i cannot alloweth their pow'rs to beest near mineth.
**Vados:** What are you asking, great admiral?
**SCP-001:** I requesteth f'r those individuals to beest enroll'd in mine own crew, and visage their inevitable fate
**Vados:** You may not know that one of those individuals is a fundamental part of our pantheon of gods, sire.
**SCP-001:** █████, I wast kind enow to asketh a f'rmal requesteth. I didn't bypass thy auth'rity going directly to that gent.
**Marcarita:** With all due respect, we simply cannot sacrifice a member of our own council for the unknown sake of another universe.
**SCP-001:** T is the fate of the whole omniv'rse yond is at stake.  I und'rstand thee cannot comprehend the grand scheme of things, but i require thee to handeth me the subject
**Marcarita:** We ask you to leave, Grand Master. We cannot fulfill your request.
**SCP-001:** What insolence.
**Vados:** I beg your pardon on her behalf. Nonetheless, it's true that we can't answer your orders.
[DATA UNAVAILABLE]
**<End log>**
**Closing Statement:** Following incident [DATA ESPURGED], all angels were held captive by SCP-001 for being immune to the //Word of Wyncham//.
