## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The uMvuni Klan ("Clan of Reapers") is related by blood to the Vilkai Klan [REM-635](#/rem-635), from which it gains protection. It is based in the outermost peripheral regions of the Lexi Empire, and its members have purple, violet, blue and red tattoos. Members include Aries [REM-1023](#/rem-1023), Aquila [REM-1024](#/rem-1024), Boötes [REM-1025](#/rem-1025) and Canis [REM-1026](#/rem-1026).
