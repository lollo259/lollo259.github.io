## Description
<!-- type: article -->
TTF Delta-1 ("Privateers", "Pirates") is formed by military personnel deployed in the open sea. TTF Gamma-1 [REM-315](#/rem-315) personnel must mix with the pisces specimens to trigger an Astral-Dragon Scenario. They attack islands by digging tunnels or by aerial manoeuvres. The grand fleet is commanded by [REM-321](#/rem-321); other captains include [REM-322](#/rem-322), a living skeleton with a burning beard. All the captains have contracts with demons that grant them abilities.

## TTF Delta-1 ("Privateers" "Pirates")
<!-- type: description | source: Il Quadrante delle emozioni | translated: yes -->
TTF Delta-1 ("Privateers" “Pirates”)
Task Force Mission: Terra Task Force Delta-1 is formed by military personnel deployed in the open sea. [REM-315](#/rem-315) personnel are required to mix with the pisces specimens, in order to activate an Astral-Dragon Scenario. They attack islands via digging tunnels and/or aerial maneuvering. The grand fleet is commanded by [REM-321](#/rem-321). Other important captains include [REM-322](#/rem-322) (living skeleton with burning beard). All the captains have sealed a contract with demons that grants them various abilities. [Translated from Italian:] Astral-Dragon Scenario: following the song Per Aspera Ad Astra by [REM-000](#/rem-000), it is foreseen that "[...] the strongest of all [the Pisces] will rise to the stars, meeting the Thunder Dragon". The meaning of the quotation translates into the transformation of a TTF Delta-1 agent into a dragon, who will then be able to face St. Namygal Caesar in person [the Thunder Dragon].
