## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Immortal Army is a hundred statues repainted by X [REM-611](#/rem-611) in later millennia. Thanks to the Ring of Flow [REM-606](#/rem-606) they can move normally while remaining stopped in time, and so dense as statues.
