## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23]
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus [9/4/23] | translated: no -->
A multidimensional force exists to explain the existence of the cosmos. It's metaphysical, because it comes from outside the fictional setting: it's the author in itself, therefore it's a necessary being, and the probabilistic field is a contingent field created by the author. It's both nihilistic and absurdist, therefore it's represented by delirium and destruction. This force is the main consciousness of the cosmos, therefore it can be described as an entity. All states of active consciousness are called living or concept of desire, and all states of passive consciousness are the concept of death. Both exist at the same moment, in a constant embrace.

The Pekka, with its relentless focus and overwhelming persistence, is used as a counterforce in situations where traditional measures have failed.
