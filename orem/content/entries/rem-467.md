## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Stalker (Object Class: Euclid) is a small, golem-like automaton 1.2 metres tall, of reinforced alloys and organic compounds with mechanical appendages, built to track and neutralise a designated target. It has no consciousness and follows an algorithm that can be reprogrammed, pursuing its target with near-infallible accuracy until it is neutralised. A group of Stalkers is a "Pekka", a task force designed to counter the Celestial Meridians [REM-171](#/rem-171). Stalker armour is forged at The Forge [REM-306](#/rem-306).

## Containment
<!-- type: article -->
It is kept in a reinforced chamber in a robotics facility with electromagnetic fields that disrupt its guidance system; only authorised personnel may begin deactivation.

## Special Containment Procedures
<!-- type: containment | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Special Containment Procedures:
REM-467, known as "The Stalker," must be contained in a high-security robotics facility equipped with electromagnetic fields capable of disrupting its internal guidance system. The Stalker is to be housed in a small, reinforced containment chamber, capable of sustaining extreme pressure and environmental conditions. Due to its autonomous nature and its relentless pursuit behavior, any attempts to stop the Stalker before it completes its mission must be handled with extreme caution.
Personnel tasked with interacting with REM-467 must undergo a thorough analysis of its tracking algorithm and mission parameters. Given its pursuit-driven programming, subjects should not be placed in close proximity to any identified target objects unless fully debriefed. Only authorized personnel are allowed to initiate deactivation protocols.

## Description
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Description:
REM-467, commonly known as "The Stalker," is a small-sized automaton designed with a singular purpose: to track and neutralize a designated target object. Standing at 1.2 meters tall, the Stalker is an intricate golem-like creation, composed of reinforced metal alloys and organic compounds, with several mechanical appendages designed for both mobility and offensive action. Despite its small size, the Stalker is capable of extreme precision and persistence, guided by a complex algorithm that directs its relentless pursuit until the target object is neutralized.
The Stalker operates autonomously, without any true consciousness, and is driven by an algorithm designed to hunt and destroy. The algorithm can be overridden or modified to target specific objects, and it is programmed to identify these targets with near-infallible accuracy. Upon detection, the Stalker will begin its pursuit and will not cease its actions until the object has been neutralized or destroyed.
The Stalker is powered by a rare and anomalous space fungus, which serves as both a biological and technological fuel source. The fungus is a form of organic energy storage that enhances the Stalker's computational capabilities, enabling it to adapt its tactics and strategies as required. This fungus has been linked to a number of unidentified extraterrestrial phenomena, and attempts to replicate it have failed.

## Communication and Behavior
<!-- type: description | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Communication and Behavior:
While the Stalker does not possess true consciousness, it communicates using a complex, untranslatable language that consists of intricate signals and frequencies. These signals seem to be both technological and organic in nature, suggesting a synthesis of machine programming and biological influence. The Stalker does not appear to have the ability to understand or respond to human communication; it simply interprets its environment and interacts with it in accordance with its algorithm.
Although it does not engage in typical human communication, the Stalker is capable of relaying its mission status through encoded signals that can be intercepted by the Organization’s advanced linguistics systems. These communications primarily serve as status reports on its pursuit of the target, and occasionally, diagnostic updates on its internal systems.

## Addendum REM-467-A – The Pekka Task Force
<!-- type: addendum | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Addendum REM-467-A – The Pekka Task Force:
A group of Stalkers is referred to as a "Pekka." The Pekka represents a specialized task force designed to combat and neutralize a much larger, far more powerful threat: the celestial meridian. These celestial meridians are enormous, god-like entities that exist within various paracosms, representing complex forces of nature and the cosmos. The Pekka, with its relentless focus and overwhelming persistence, is used as a counterforce in situations where traditional measures have failed. 
The Pekka is often deployed in high-risk scenarios and can be combined with other monster entities or weapons designed to neutralize celestial threats. The Stalkers that make up the Pekka often work in coordinated efforts, adapting to the celestial meridian's tactics and countering them with their specialized programming. While their numbers may seem small, the effectiveness of their combined pursuit can overwhelm even the most formidable cosmic entities.
The Stalkers' ability to integrate with other monsters (both human-made and anomalous in nature) has proven to be an invaluable asset. These creatures, referred to as "Monsters," serve as both distraction and combat support, assisting the Stalkers in maintaining focus on their targets.

Addendum REM-467-B – Known Incidents:

## Incident 467-01
<!-- type: incident | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Incident 467-01: A Stalker was deployed to track and neutralize a high-value target identified as Subject F-12 [[REM-301](#/rem-301)], a heavily armored and mobile prototype weapon designed to escape containment. Despite the immense power of the prototype, the Stalker relentlessly pursued the target and neutralized it within 18 hours, showing no signs of fatigue or deviation from its algorithm.

## Incident 467-07
<!-- type: incident | source: THE OREM | translated: no -->
Incident 467-07: During an operation involving a celestial meridian in the Goddess of the Spiral [[REM-303](#/rem-303)]'s domain, a Pekka unit was deployed. After 72 hours of continuous pursuit, the Pekka successfully neutralized the celestial threat, but at a significant cost. Two Stalkers were destroyed during the mission, and several Monsters were incapacitated. However, the mission was considered a success due to the overall neutralization of the target.
