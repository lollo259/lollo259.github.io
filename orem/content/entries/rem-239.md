## 6.1 Ford Prefect — message 1

**Ford Prefect:**

Or, picking up a bit on the myth of Prometheus [[REM-109](#/rem-109)]:

One of the reasons John Henry [[REM-217](#/rem-217)] was executed was that he wanted to steal the Tecnica and give it to the creatures, in particular to the Machinist (as the name says, the Machinist is the creature who received the Tecnica, that is, he becomes the manipulator of the Machines).

Now the creatures, thanks to the Tecnica, are able to produce Artifacts, second-generation artifacts, while the gods have the primordial Artifacts but can no longer produce any others. So the creatures, led by the Machinist, prepare to fight against the celestial army.

The Machinist [[REM-XXX](#/rem-xxx)] takes the Tecnica and divides it into five (or however many members Orem has), giving each one a specialty and keeping for himself the ability to create artifacts.

Now that the Tecnica is personified, the only way the gods have to take it back is to kill the holders of the Tecnica.

That is why humanity has only one hope, which lies in the group of Orem.

## 6.2 Ford Prefect — message 2

John Henry [[REM-217](#/rem-217)] stole the Tecnica and some artifacts to show how the Tecnica can be used.

The Machinist [[REM-XXX](#/rem-xxx)], studying the primordial Artifacts, discovers how to produce new ones.

## 6.3 Ford Prefect — message 3

And how is it that the Tecnica passes from John Henry [[REM-217](#/rem-217)] on Earth to Orem on Paradis?

Anyway, it would be cool if John Henry [[REM-217](#/rem-217)] entered the Church of Light [[REM-123](#/rem-123)] in a dream. He discovers the Tecnica and takes it. When he wakes up, he realizes that the Tecnica is with him (he learned the Tecnica in a dream). Then he divides it into five and gives it to Orem before being executed.

## 6.4 Ford Prefect — message 4

The black box fits because to use the Tecnica fully you need data.

John Henry [[REM-217](#/rem-217)] wakes up and realizes that he has the Tecnica with him and that he has committed a sin. He hides it hoping no one will notice, but when he sleeps again the celestial quadrant comes crashing down on him,

which executes him but does not find the Tecnica because, not possessing the Tecnica, it is not able to search for it properly.

The Machinist [[REM-XXX](#/rem-xxx)] finds it again, and does something never done before: he uses the Tecnica to divide the Tecnica, and gives it to Orem.

John Henry [[REM-217](#/rem-217)] takes the Tecnica but does not know how to use it. He gives it to the world hoping they can use it in the future, knowing he will be executed. In reality not only is he executed, but the whole world dies, and so the Tecnica remains on the world, somewhere, hidden. The gods, no longer having the Tecnica, are unable to search for it. Then the Machinist arrives and finds it.

## 6.5 WHO? — message 5: the zones of interest of the Orem

**WHO?:**

the zones of interest of the Orem

Librarii Ofel Isandarìa [[REM-126](#/rem-126)] - a ring-shaped library, but titanic, as big as a planet. Here there are billions of books and/or pieces of information, including: the Bestiary (encyclopedia of the "Monsters", that is, of the magical artifacts), Information HAZARD "First Empress" (the letters of Friona [[REM-137](#/rem-137)], the first empress of Salolem [[REM-237](#/rem-237)] as well as the only being of Paradis more powerful than a god), Bypass Agent (a virus made of decryption information, able to adapt to any subject), BLACKBOXXX [[REM-999](#/rem-999)] (we already know it).

Babel - a branch of Orem dedicated to the writing of books. It has by far the largest staff of the organization, comprising billions of subjects. They constantly type random combinations on a stenotype, so that mathematically sooner or later they will write something sensible, possibly every single book that ever existed and every book of the future (obviously it is not really possible, but I like the fact that the staff dedicates work to something useless).

Arcadia [[REM-215](#/rem-215)] - a sector of the Church of Light [[REM-123](#/rem-123)] stolen from the gods, where Eve resides. She is used as a hostage, but in reality: she does not escape, she has all the comforts in the world, and the organization cannot capture her because of the side effects of her existence.

Bastille - the prison for the gods. I still have to describe it.

The Forge - a red star surrounded by a Dyson sphere. The star is composed of cremated gods, used to forge the armor of the Stalkers (the special force "Pekka", able to face and hunt the gods).
