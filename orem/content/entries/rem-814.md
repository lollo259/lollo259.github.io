## From the source: An Hourglass of Blood and Dreams

Meanwhile, Kiku was speaking with himself. He ate the kami kami no mi, making him a paper man, he took back one of his origami that had just returned from inspection. He looked preoccupied, his expression darkening by the second, as he continued to discuss the matter. Finally he got rid of the bird-like paper craft, and stood behind me. "My disciple, the navy fleet is looming in the distance. I've confirmed that all three admirals are on those ships." said Kiku. "All three admirals? Perfect" I replied. "Three buster calls have been activated at the same time, on this island. I do not know the exact details." he continued "but it seems like they were called by Cipher Pol agents and SWORD, shaking the navy system." "Who did this?" "One confirmed was captain Kuzan, who's on this island with his fiance Monet. I've also intercepted a call between Guernica and the fleet admiral, it seems like Issho himself is coming to destroy the island after your capture." "Very well, then. We shall begin."

There was a body of water near me. On the shore, a lifeless body of a giantess.  I could tell just from looking at her that she was a warrior, but not just any ordinary warrior, I could sense her power as well. She was magnificent. It was hard for me to take my eyes off her; drops of snow were on her body, so I assumed she ran into Monet, and she got paralyzed by her. The giantess' face. It was beautiful, even with those two horns spreading outwards  Her face looked calm, but her eyes were filled with rage. It was like seeing the face of a wild animal before you kill it.  Her cheeks were flushed red, but the blood seemed to have drained out of them already.   Her mouth had been agape, as if screaming something, but nothing came out.   I don't know why, but I felt like I couldn't move my own eyes away from her mouth. It took everything in me to tear my eyes away from hers to notice the rest of her.  Her body was densely packed as if she was sculpted, and she looked so heavy.  As I looked down on her, I could feel her immense weight pressing on me, crushing me.
