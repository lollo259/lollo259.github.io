## Description
<!-- type: article -->
A is an angel who falls in love with B [REM-613](#/rem-613), a woman. When the angels, following the word of X, agree not to intervene, A opposes them to save her; some angels side with him, but through the Ring of Fear [REM-614](#/rem-614) they are made terrifying to others. The catalogue records that he becomes an amnesiac monk.

## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Angel in love with B; becomes an amnesiac monk. — Quote: "A è un angelo. B è una donna. I due si innamorano."
