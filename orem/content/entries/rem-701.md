## Description
<!-- type: article -->
The Sakura Palace (similar to the Grosse Halle) is the centre of a city built like Babylonian hanging gardens on a single level. Inside, in front of the entrance, stand a colossal naked statue of the goddess Hathor [REM-112](#/rem-112) and a gigantic bathhouse.
