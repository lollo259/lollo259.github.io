## Description
<!-- type: article -->
Sir Lancillotto, or Sire Maniac, is the common name of Lancillotto Bordeaux [REM-622](#/rem-622), the sadistic prince and collector of the City of Love, who captured Furia Jehoel, the near-perfect copy of Abaddon Jehoel [REM-512](#/rem-512).

Jehoel, Furia (let. Anger) is referred by historians as being “the almost perfect copy of the Holy Abaddon Jehoel [[REM-512](#/rem-512)]; every single attribute of her body resembles her descriptions, and the many songs written and dedicated to her”. She was one of the most feared warrior in the Lexi Empire until she was captured by Sir Lancillotto.
