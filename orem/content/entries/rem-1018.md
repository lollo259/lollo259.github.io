## From the source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia
<!-- type: description | source: Paradis i Ander Reus — Worldbuilding Encyclopedia | translated: no -->
Bloodsuckers Clan (let. Clan of Bloodsuckers) is the second most notorious clan in the continent of Araceli, and the most feared in the entire world. Almost all of its members are all dead, and the remaining ones are mostly hunted.

Bloodsuckers, Layla (let. Born during the night) was one of the Four Horsewomen, the best warriors in the entire continent of Araceli. She was strangled to death by her adoptive father, Kemonokaidou.

Öý (let. Home) is the biggest territory in the Northern Continent.

Rodhår Clan (let. Clan of Red-haired) is the most notorious clan in the world, mostly because of Araceli Rodhår, and her descendents maintaining the name in prestige.

Rodhår, Araceli (let. Monument of the skies) was the protagonist of The Birth of Araceli, the most infamous story in the whole continent of a woman who single handedly drove away the Devil’s Army from the continent by making a clever deal. The Northern Continent is named after her. She’s known to be one of the most clever and courageous women to ever live, and obviously one of the most important.

Rodhår, Blade (let. Blade) was the daughter of Araceli Rodhår, born from a demon.

## Mentions in other documents: EDEN.odt
<!-- type: description | source: EDEN.odt | translated: yes -->
Location: Foresta Nera, Araceli

Mariannes – come from an attempt to resurrect the dead elves due to Araceli, partially worked but with side effects

Edo Tensei – created to resurrect the dead elves during the oppression of Araceli, beheaded by the same Araceli. The brain had to be extracted from the head, while the body had to develop and change on an organism such as a feline, as a container. The brain had to be connected to the feline to be “reactivated” and lead to change in a humanoid body. The side effect was the body of the feline that rejected the brain, once already developed. The brain thrown into the water continued to remain active, creating the first Mariannes

Discrimination: human beings. They once lived peacefully together; when humans with Araceli started attacking them they were forced to take refuge in the heart of the Black Forest, known by that name for the numerous internal pitfalls. For the elves, however, despite having to fight the pitfalls, it has become the only salvation, and therefore has been called Eden
