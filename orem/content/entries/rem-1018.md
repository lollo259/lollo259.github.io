## Catalogue record
<!-- type: quote -->
Continent named after Araceli Rodhår. — Quote: "…continent of a woman who single handedly drove away the Devil’s Army from the continent by making a clever deal. The Northern Continent is named after her. She’s known to be one of the most clever and courageous women to ever live, and obviously one of the most important."
