This universe is all that exists, therefore it’s omnipresent. If omnipresence exists, and is a fundamental property of the contingent being, then the other properties must also exist. If the other properties exist, as in omniscience and omnipotence, and I am the contingent being, then I am all that makes this universe, therefore I am the universe itself. If I am this universe and I’m omnipotent, then any -potence, or possibility, exists; we could define those possibilities by functions that exist by necessity; sounds like quantum woo, but bear with me. If I am the universe, and I'm omniscient, then any subject existing in this universe retains that property, varying the degree of consciousness depending on the state of complexity in which it finds itself; what you call that awareness is a subject reflecting on itself. Every subject needs to survive in the universe, therefore in me, inevitably in eternal juxtaposition and coexistence with other subjects. This usually resolves in the subconscious, or conscious, creation of codes. I write this letter as a melancholic thought purely investigated in a cult of memories where life is defined by what you remember and how you remember it, and memory is nothing but the sum of your memories. I welcome readers in the void that is the artist’s mind, in a boring exploration of the ego and its inevitable death. The hypersphere that is this paracosm (P, the artist), or I should say brain, is infinite in its surface, and if I were able to observe its geometry it would seem a chronotopic field made of itself. In its structure there is a probability field, in which the particles forming it are the shadows of the points within this random function of random variation. The value of a particle in a given point within the chronotopic field, therefore its wave function, is related to the probability that the particle is located there at that moment, and the observed particle collapsed at that time. These axioms form the atoms of the artistic mind and, if every probability has a chance to exist, then there are infinite possibilities, or universes, within the mind.
